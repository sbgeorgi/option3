How the 90/180 rule works

In the Schengen area, a short stay is limited to 90 days within any 180-day period. To check a day, count back 180 days from it. The stay days in that window must not exceed 90. Entry and exit days both count as days of stay.

Example: 90 days from 1 January to 31 March 2026 uses the full allowance. On 30 June 2026, 1 January leaves the 180-day window, so one day frees up.
