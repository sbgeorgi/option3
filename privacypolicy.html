NomadSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy explains what personal data NomadSwipe collects when you use our mobile apps and our website at nomadswipe.com, why we collect it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. We try to use plain words. If something is unclear, email privacy@nomadswipe.com.

1. Who we are

NomadSwipe is a travel planner. You swipe through real places in a city, save the ones you like into a trip, see them on a map, and open place details such as photos, rating, opening hours, directions, website and phone number.

The company that decides how and why your data is used (the "controller") is:

In this policy, "we," "us" and "our" mean NomadSwipe LLC. "App" means our mobile apps. "Website" means nomadswipe.com.

2. Summary of key points

  • What we collect. Your account details, the trips and places you save, your location while the app is open, reviews and messages you write, photos you choose to upload, your subscription status, and basic technical data when a request fails.
  • Location. We read your precise location only while the app is open, to show nearby places, your position on the map and trip stops. We do not collect it in the background.
  • No selling, no ads. We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app has no advertising SDK and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Who helps us. A small set of providers host our service, send email, handle subscriptions and draw the map. They are listed in section 5.
  • Your rights. You can see, fix, export or delete your data. You can delete your account in the app. See section 9.
  • Age. You must be at least 16 to use NomadSwipe.
  • Website analytics. Google Analytics on our website loads only after you click Accept in the cookie banner. See section 14.

4. More detail: location, subscriptions and moderation

Location

  • We read your precise location only while the app is open and only after you allow it in your phone. We do not collect it in the background.
  • We use it to show places near you, your position on the map, distances, and trip stops that start from where you are.
  • It is sent to our server (Supabase) so that the server can answer the place request. In Discover, your precise position is also sent to your phone's own location-name service (geocoder) to find the place name: Google on Android, Apple on iPhone. We do not share it with advertisers or sell it, and we do not use it for ads.
  • Trip stops and saved places contain the locations of places you chose to save. If you add a "where you are" stop, the stop contains your current GPS position instead. They are stored with your trip until you delete them.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time in your phone's Settings. The app keeps working without it, with fewer nearby features.
  • On Android, the Google Maps SDK draws the map. Google receives device and usage data through that SDK under its own privacy policy. On iPhone, the map is Apple Maps, and Apple receives map requests under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions

  • "Premium" is a monthly auto-renewing subscription. There is also a one-time lifetime purchase that does not renew. Both are sold only through the app store on your device: Google Play, and Apple's App Store where our app is available. The price is shown in the store before you buy. We currently offer no free trial.
  • The monthly subscription renews each month until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. On Android, open the Play Store, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Google or Apple charge you and handle refunds under their own policies. We never see your card details. We receive only your subscription status, through RevenueCat.
  • If you are in the EU or UK, you may have a 14-day right to withdraw from a digital purchase. Each store runs its own process, and we cannot cancel or refund store purchases ourselves.
  • Google Play: refunds and EU withdrawal requests follow Google Play's policy. See support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
  • Apple: under Apple's terms, customers in the EU and UK can cancel a purchase within 14 days of buying it, through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reports, blocking and removals

  • You can report and block users and content, for example by long-pressing it. A person reviews each report.
  • Before a chat message or review is posted, the app checks the text on your phone against a list of banned words. If it matches, nothing is sent and you are asked to reword it. The check runs on your device and nothing about it is sent to us.
  • If we remove your content, we tell you and give a reason. You can appeal by replying to our support message or by emailing support@nomadswipe.com.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Our database provider encrypts stored data at rest. Row-level security rules in the database limit each account to its own data. Business proof photos are kept in a private storage area. Staff access to your data is limited as described in section 5. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

  • Access. Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction. Ask us to fix data that is wrong. You can edit most profile data in the app.
  • Deletion. Ask us to delete your data.
  • Portability. Ask for your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Objection. Object to processing that relies on our legitimate interests.
  • Restriction. Ask us to pause the use of your data while a dispute is settled.
  • Withdraw consent. Where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before. For website analytics, use the Cookie settings link. For location, use your phone's Settings.

How to make a request

  • Delete your account in the app. Open Profile, then Account actions, then Delete account, and type DELETE. The page nomadswipe.com/account-deletion.html explains the steps.
  • Any other request. Email privacy@nomadswipe.com from the email address on your account and say what you want. We may ask you to confirm the request from the app, or ask one or two questions about your account, to be sure it is you. We will not ask for more than we need.
  • Time. We reply within 30 days. If a request is complex, we will tell you why we need more time, as the law allows. It is free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

What account deletion removes

Deleting your account removes your profile, trips, saved and liked places, reviews, chats on both sides, friends, business claims, uploaded photos and files, diagnostic records and your RevenueCat customer record. If you signed in with Apple, we revoke the Sign in with Apple token. If you email us instead, we do this within 30 days.

We keep only: anonymised moderation reports; public place data that you did not write; purchase records that Apple and Google hold; backups, which rotate out within 30 days; and anything we must keep under a legal hold or for a legal claim.

Selling, sharing and sensitive data

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we collected, used and disclosed about you, to give you a copy, to delete it, to correct it, and to stop selling or sharing it (we do neither). You will not be treated differently for using these rights. We do not deny service, charge different prices or give a different quality of service because of them.

To make a request, use the steps in section 9. We confirm receipt within 10 business days and reply within 30 days. If we need longer, we tell you, and the law allows up to 45 days or more in some states. We verify you by matching the request with your account email.

Authorized agents. You can use an agent. The agent must send us your signed permission, or a power of attorney, from the agent's email, and we may still confirm with you through your account email.

Appeals. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our email with the word "Appeal". We answer within 45 days (60 days in some states) and explain why. If you are still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general.
