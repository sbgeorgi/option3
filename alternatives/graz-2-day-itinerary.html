Graz itinerary: 2 days, 22 stops from real app data

A Graz 2-day itinerary of 22 places from the NomadSwipe app's data, all within 1.4 km of the city-centre pin: the old town on day one, the Joanneum quarter, Kunsthaus and galleries on day two. Thirteen stops have stored hours that every start time fits; nine have none.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Graz place data, read on 9 November 2026. I did not check when those rows were written, so treat the hours as a snapshot of unknown age and check before you go.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, the old town on foot (12 stops): Opera Graz, the Burgtor, the Mausoleum of Emperor Ferdinand II, the Grazer Stadtkern pin, Freiheitsplatz, lunch at Steak Boutique, the Landhaus, the Gemaltes Haus, the Altstadt von Graz pin, the Zanderhof, the Uhrturm and dinner at El Gaucho at Landhaus.
  • Day 2, the Joanneum quarter, Kunsthaus and Griesgasse (10 stops): the New Gallery Graz, Ducks coffee shop, lunch at cafe erde, the Kunsthaus Graz, gallery lendnine, Artelier Contemporary, Omas Teekanne Cafe, off_gallery graz, the Bertha von Suttner Statue and dinner at Ginko.
  • One day in Graz: day one on its own is a one-day plan. All 12 stops sit within 0.84 km of each other by straight line.
  • Days of the week: day one works Tuesday to Saturday, day two only on Wednesday or Thursday. I assume Tuesday and Wednesday.
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 2.2 km and day two to about 2.5 km by straight line between stored coordinates. These are not travel times.

A Graz deck with 31 sights, and only 12 of them rated

The Graz data in the app is one destination row with 377 cards and 377 distinct place IDs. 179 of those cards sit within 4 km of the destination's centre pin; the other 198 are farther away, and 67 of those are more than 1,000 km away (the ones I looked at are New York museums). Of the 179, 94 have a stored rating and 112 have stored opening hours. Counting by stored category, 92 are restaurants, bars and cafes, 29 are hotels and 31 are sights, in five categories: art gallery (11), historical site (10), tourist attraction (5), theater and concert hall (3) and museum (2). Two of the galleries share one pin, so there are 30 distinct sight locations. Only 12 of the 31 sights have a stored rating, 15 have stored hours and three clear a line of 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews: the Altstadt von Graz pin, the Grazer Landhaus and the Mausoleum of Emperor Ferdinand II. Three of the five tourist attractions are nightclubs, so I left them out.

  • One pin carries the reviews. The 12 rated sights hold 2,354 stored reviews between them, and the Altstadt von Graz pin alone holds 1,482 of them, 63 percent. The Kunsthaus Graz holds another 503. Most of the other sights are small galleries or monuments with no rating.
  • Nine stops have no stored hours. Opera Graz, the Burgtor, Freiheitsplatz, the Landhaus, the Gemaltes Haus, the Altstadt von Graz pin, the Uhrturm, gallery lendnine and the Bertha von Suttner Statue could not be matched to a weekday. They are flagged in the plan.
  • No Schlossberg, Murinsel or Eggenberg sight card. A title search of all 377 cards found none of them. The Uhrturm, which is on the Schlossberg, is in the data; I name the others because you will look for them, and they are not in the plan because the data has no card to plan around.
  • Prices, tickets and transport are left out. I read no official ticket or transit page this run, so check each place's own website the day you go.

A short real-world cross-check, from one read of Wikipedia on 9 November 2026: the article on Graz says the historic centre was added to the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1999 and was extended in 2010 to include Schloss Eggenberg, that the centre "consists of over 1,000 buildings, their age ranging from Gothic to contemporary", and that the Uhrturm is a symbol of Graz at the top of Castle Hill, the hill dominating the centre. Nothing in the plan depends on those facts; they explain why one old-town pin collects most of the reviews.

Questions about this Graz plan

Can you do Graz in one day?

On paper, yes: the 12 stops of day one never leave a patch 0.84 km across by straight line between stored coordinates, so day one alone is a one-day Graz walk through the old town. What it leaves out is the Joanneum quarter, the Kunsthaus and the galleries around Griesgasse, which are day two. If you only have one day, use day one and add the Kunsthaus, which is 0.43 km from the Landhaus by straight line.

Which days of the week work for this Graz plan?

By the stored hours, day one works from Tuesday to Saturday and day two works only on Wednesday or Thursday, so the pairs that work are Tuesday and Wednesday or Wednesday and Thursday. Day one fails on Monday because the Mausoleum of Emperor Ferdinand II is stored as closed, and on Sunday because Steak Boutique is stored as closed. Day two fails on Monday, Tuesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday because the two small galleries on the route (Artelier Contemporary and off_gallery graz) are stored as closed on those days. Nine stops have no stored hours and are not tested. The hours may have changed since they were stored, so confirm them before you go.

Do I need a car or a tram for this Graz plan?

I read no transit, taxi or parking source, so I cannot say what is available. By straight line between stored coordinates, no leg is longer than 0.91 km, day one totals about 2.2 km and day two about 2.5 km. At an assumed 5 km/h that is roughly half an hour of walking a day before stairs, hills and photo stops, so the plan is built to be walked.

Why does the app's Graz deck have no Schlossberg or Murinsel card?

I searched all 377 cards stored under Graz by title for Schlossberg, Murinsel, Eggenberg, Zeughaus and Joanneum and found no sight card for any of them; the title matches were a hotel named after the Schlossberg, an apartment and a bar named after the Joanneum quarter, and a theatre festival 9 km away (Schlossspiele Reinthal). The Uhrturm card, which stands on the Schlossberg, is in the data and in the plan. The missing cards are a gap in the stored data, not a statement about what Graz has.

How were the 22 stops chosen?

From the 179 cards within 4 km of the centre pin I used all 10 historic sites, both museums, one of three theatres and three of eleven art galleries, then added four restaurants and two cafes with stored ratings of 4.3 or higher. That gave 16 sights, four restaurants and two cafes. Each suggested start time was then tested against the stored weekly hours for the 13 stops that have them.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 9 November 2026 with read-only queries. Graz is stored as one destination row with 377 cards and 377 distinct place IDs (373 distinct places by case-insensitive title and address). That keeps 179 cards and drops 198; 67 of the dropped cards are more than 1,000 km from the pin. The counts below are of the 179 unless stated. I defined sights by stored category name: art gallery, historical site, tourist attraction, theater and concert hall and museum. That is 31 cards, or 30 distinct locations, since gallery lendnine and Helmi Mubarak share one pin. I first listed every distinct category name within 4 km (restaurant 67, hotel 29, bar 15, beauty and spa 13, art gallery 11, cafe 10, historical site 10, vacation rentals 5, tourist attraction 5, theater and concert hall 3, sports and recreation 3, museum 2 and six single-card categories) and found no other category that holds a sight; a stored amusement place, the Virtual Escape Room Graz (4.8 from 40 reviews), is an activity and I left it out. An empty or missing hours list counts as no stored hours. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates.
