What happened to Inspirock? Klarna bought the trip planner in 2021

What happened to Inspirock? Klarna announced on 22 October 2021 that it had acquired the trip planner, with no price disclosed. I found no shutdown notice, but on 12 November 2026 I could not load Inspirock's https website, and plain-http inspirock.com redirected to klarna.com.

What Inspirock was

TechCrunch described Inspirock in 2015 as a site that generates a customised travel itinerary. You entered a destination, an age range and travel dates, picked interest categories such as gardens or landmarks, then deleted suggestions or saved them into time slots. TechCrunch says the choices fed back into what the site showed next, and that it covered major destinations in the U.S. and Europe at the time, with plans to expand. Its stated revenue plan was a share of commissions on tickets and reservations booked through partners.

By 2021 the scale had grown. Klarna's release says Inspirock serves "over 25 million travelers a year", a figure from the buyer's own press release, which I could not check independently. TravelMole's July 2021 report says Skyscanner used Inspirock for the sightseeing and activities part of its Road Trips beta, which also covered routes, accommodation and car hire.

The Klarna deal

Klarna's release, dated 22 October 2021, says it will combine Inspirock's technology with the Klarna app so that users can plan a trip, shop for it and pay in installments. It also says Klarna's retail partners, which it counts at more than 250,000, could reach high-intent shoppers with personalised travel content. It cites 90 million Klarna users. The release gives no price. It does not say whether Inspirock would carry on as a stand-alone website or fold into the Klarna app. CB Insights agrees that the terms were not disclosed and lists $3 million as the total Inspirock had raised, which matches the 2015 seed round.

Questions about what happened to Inspirock

Did Klarna buy Inspirock?

Yes. Klarna's press release is dated 22 October 2021 and says it acquired Inspirock, and CB Insights lists Inspirock as acquired by Klarna on the same date. Neither source discloses a price.

Did Inspirock shut down?

I found no shutdown notice in any source I read, and no source says it closed. I also could not open its https website from my machine on 12 November 2026, so I cannot tell you whether the planner still works.

Who founded Inspirock?

TechCrunch's June 2015 article names Anoop Goyal and Prakash Sikchi as the founders and says the site launched in 2012. It reported a 3 million dollar seed round led by returning investor MakeMyTrip with angels and the founders.

Why did Klarna want a trip planner?

The press release says Klarna wanted users to plan a trip, shop for it and pay in installments inside the Klarna app, and to give its retail partners a way to reach shoppers with travel content. It does not say whether Inspirock would continue as a separate product.

What can I use instead of Inspirock?

That depends on whether you want an itinerary generated for you or a way to pick places yourself. I built NomadSwipe (Android) for the second job: you swipe real places in a city, save them, see them on a map and build a trip plan. Other planners are compared on the sibling pages linked above.
