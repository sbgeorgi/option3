How to use Komoot offline: a step-by-step guide

How to use Komoot offline: download the saved route and the regional map for your whole area while you have signal, because Komoot has no global offline switch. The condition is your plan: regions beyond the free one cost money, and rerouting, route planning and Live Tracking do not work offline. Custom areas need Premium or the World Pack. The steps below follow Komoot's help pages, read 28 November 2026.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), a different kind of app, and I mention it in a short card and in one section near the end. I did not run Komoot for this page: every Komoot fact comes from Komoot's own pages, read on 28 November 2026 and listed under Sources.

The short answer: two downloads, one test

Komoot's help centre says content "must be downloaded in advance, either as individual routes or as regional maps". In practice that means two things per trip: the route (so you can navigate it) and the regional map (so the area around it is there, including the start and the way back). Then you test it with Airplane Mode on and GPS left on. The nine steps below put that in order. For where Komoot sits among route apps, see the Komoot alternatives page; this one is only the how-to.

Nine steps to use Komoot without internet

These are Komoot's own instructions as its help centre words them. Its articles carry "updated" labels from 4 days to 6 months before I read them, so a button name may have moved.

  1. 1. Check that your start region is unlocked

    Komoot can plan a route anywhere, but per its plans article the region you navigate or record in must be unlocked. Its navigation article repeats it: the region around the starting point must be unlocked. When you sign up you get one free single region (free region help article); once redeemed it is permanently linked to your account and cannot be changed, and Komoot suggests picking your home region. Other regions are one-time Maps purchases or come with Premium.

  2. 2. Update the app and set Android permissions

    The Android settings article says to set Location to Always (or While using the app if Always is missing), turn on precise location if it is offered, set Battery to Unrestricted or No restrictions, and confirm Mobile data access is allowed if you will download maps over mobile data. Custom-area downloads also need the latest app version, per the download article. Komoot also recommends downloading over stable Wi-Fi.

  3. 3. Plan or save the route while you are online

    Route planning and editing need a connection, so finish them before you leave coverage (the download article lists them under what does not work offline). Saved routes live under Profile > Saved Routes.

  4. 4. Download the route

    Per the help centre: go to Profile > Saved Routes, select the route and tap Download or Save offline. While you are still online, open the route again so the app can finish pending synchronizations and confirm it opens without a warning. Then go offline and open it once more to verify. Routes and regions can only be downloaded in the app, not on the website.

  5. 5. Download the regional map for the whole area

    A route download is not the same as a map download. The help centre says to download the maps that cover your entire area, including the start and the return: go to Profile > Maps and tap Download next to a region. With Premium or the World Pack you can browse Suggested regions or search any region; if you bought single regions or bundles, your downloads appear under Unlocked. If the app shows "Offline data not synchronized" later, a missing route or region download is the first thing its troubleshooting article lists.

  6. 6. If you have Premium or the World Pack, download a custom area

    You are not limited to predefined regions. Open the map and tap the orange offline button (a crossed-out cloud), or go to Offline > Regions > Download a region and tap Download a custom area. Pan and zoom until the area fills the map view, name the region and tap Confirm. Areas you already saved show as a purple overlay so you can avoid duplicates and stitch several together for longer trips. Per the help centre, single regions and region bundles do not include custom areas.

  7. 7. If your region is not offered, download a route that covers it

    Some regions are not offered for download in the app, because of app store limits or coverage. The help centre says a downloaded route that covers the area lets you view the map, use turn-by-turn voice navigation and record your activity without internet: plan a route that covers the area, download it, and start recording when you are there. With Premium or the World Pack a custom area is the other option.

  8. 8. Test it in Airplane Mode before you leave

    The help centre says you can enable Airplane Mode to cut all connections while keeping GPS turned on. Do that once at home, open the downloaded route and check the map loads. If you get the unsynchronized warning, its offline navigation issues article says to reopen the route online, go offline and try again, then use Replan this route or Plan similar route to make a fresh copy and download that. It also says offline storage and navigation are not available if the route's start is outside an unlocked region, for example after reversing a route.

  9. 9. Navigate, and expect no rerouting

    Per the navigation article: Profile > Saved routes, select the route and tap Navigate. To navigate without internet the maps or routes must already be downloaded. If you leave the route offline, the Navigation FAQ says Komoot keeps showing the original route and alerts you that you are off-route, but cannot calculate a new path until you reconnect. On Android the navigation controls open from the arrow next to Time in Motion.

What offline costs: free region, Maps packs and Premium

Komoot has two kinds of plan, per its plans article. Maps are one-time purchases linked to your account that include voice navigation, offline functionality, lifelong map updates and GPX download, limited to the regions you bought. Premium is a subscription with worldwide maps and navigation plus extras such as multi-day routes, Live Tracking and weather on route. The standard komoot map and basic path overlays in the planner are free for everyone. The Play listing, updated 27 November 2026, says your first region is free forever, that regions and the World Pack are one-time purchases, and that Premium and Family subscriptions renew until cancelled.

Where beginners get stuck

  • Only the route is downloaded. The help centre says to download the route and the regional maps that cover the whole planned area; the "Offline data not synchronized" message means that content has not finished downloading or validating.
  • The start is outside an unlocked region. Its troubleshooting article gives reversing a route as an example: if the start lands outside a region you have unlocked, offline storage and navigation are not available.
  • Android kills the app in the background. Battery optimization can stop komoot during navigation or recording; the Android settings article lists the Unrestricted setting and extra steps for Samsung, Xiaomi and Huawei phones.
  • Expecting a detour to be solved. Offline, Komoot only shows the original route and warns you. To go a different way, the Navigation FAQ says to stop navigation, open the route in the planner (which needs a connection), remove the sections you have passed and restart.
  • Trying to download on a laptop. Region and route downloads happen in the phone app only.

Questions about using Komoot offline

How do I use Komoot offline?

Download the planned route (Profile > Saved Routes, then Download or Save offline) and the regional map that covers your whole area while you still have signal. Then open the route once online and once offline to check it, and go. Komoot's help centre says there is no global offline switch.

Is Komoot free to use offline?

Partly. Komoot's help centre says you get one free single region when you sign up, and its Play listing says that first region is free forever. Offline functionality is listed in both the one-time Maps packs and Premium, so any region beyond the free one costs money.

How much does Komoot cost for offline maps?

On komoot.com/product on 28 November 2026 a Single Region showed $3.99, a Region Bundle $8.99 and the World Pack $29.99, all with no recurring costs. The pages I read did not show a Premium price, so check the app or its Premium page.

Does Komoot navigation work without internet?

Yes for a downloaded route or region: the help centre says you can view the map, use turn-by-turn voice navigation and record your activity offline. Automatic rerouting, route planning and editing, and Live Tracking need a connection.

What if I also need places for the city part of a trip?

Komoot is built for hiking, cycling and running routes, so city sights and food are a different job. I built NomadSwipe (Android) for that: you swipe real places, save them and see them on a map. For other route apps, see the Komoot alternatives page.
