2 days in Ghent: a 17-stop itinerary from real app data

Two days in Ghent fit 17 stops from the NomadSwipe app's place data, 12 with stored opening hours tested by weekday. The plan works Thursday to Friday or Friday to Saturday, because Monday and Wednesday close several of the best-rated museums.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Ghent place data, read on 9 November 2026. Every Ghent row was written on 9 May 2026, so the hours are about six months old: treat them as a snapshot and check before you go. Outside facts come from the two pages named under Sources.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, the Museum Dr. Guislain and then the old centre: Museum Dr. Guislain, Huis van Alijn, Sint-Veerleplein, lunch at Pakhuis, Design Museum Gent, the Museum of Industry, a drink at 't Dreupelkot and Hot Club Gent.
  • Day 2, the centre and the museums to the south: coffee at Café Labath, the Stadshal, STAM, Music Center De Bijloke, lunch at Umamido Vooruit, MSK, SMAK, pastry at Aux Merveilleux de Fred and Opera Ghent.
  • Days of the week: I assume Thursday for day one and Friday for day two. Friday and Saturday also work; Monday and Wednesday are the days to avoid.
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 4.0 km by straight line between stored coordinates, day two to about 5.9 km. These are not travel times.

Ghent in the app: 28 sights, and only 16 of them rated

The Ghent data in the app is one destination row with 452 cards and 452 distinct place IDs. I kept the 213 that sit within 4 km of the destination's centre point (51.05, 3.71667). Of those 213, 146 have stored opening hours and 104 are restaurants. Sights are a thin slice: 28 cards in five stored categories, namely art gallery (8), museum (7), tourist attraction (5), theater and concert hall (5) and historic site (3). Only 16 of the 28 have a stored rating and 16 have stored hours, and they are not the same 16. Six clear my line of 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews: STAM, the Museum voor Schone Kunsten (MSK), Huis van Alijn, Museum Dr. Guislain, the Museum of Industry and Hot Club Gent. This plan uses all six.

  • No castle, church or park cards. None of the 213 is stored as a park, garden, church, castle or bridge. The Gravensteen shows up only as a restaurant and a hotel, and there is no card for St Bavo's Cathedral or the Graslei. I did not add them by hand, so the plan has no hours for them.
  • Five stops have no stored hours. Sint-Veerleplein, Design Museum Gent, the Stadshal, Music Center De Bijloke and Opera Ghent. They are flagged in the plan and not weekday-tested.
  • Prices, tickets and transport are left out. I read no official museum or transit page this run, so check each place's own website the day you go.
  • Add your own pins. Anything you want that the data lacks, such as the castle, is yours to add on the map yourself.

A stop fails a day if its stored hours are closed, do not cover the start time, or leave less than one hour before closing. The five stops without stored hours cannot be tested.

Day 1: the Museum Dr. Guislain, then the old centre

Day one starts at the one stop that is far out, then settles into a patch under 1 km across. By straight line between stored coordinates, the first leg from Museum Dr. Guislain to Huis van Alijn is 1.75 km, the longest in the plan, and every other leg is 0.80 km or less. The farthest pair, Pakhuis and Museum Dr. Guislain, is 2.00 km apart (Pakhuis and the Museum of Industry, the farthest pair without the Guislain, are 0.88 km apart), and going stop to stop adds up to about 3.99 km. Times below are my suggested start times.

  1. 10:00, Museum Dr. Guislain: stored as a museum, 4.2 from 332 reviews, open 10 AM to 5 PM from Tuesday to Friday, 1 PM to 6 PM on Saturday and Sunday, and closed on Monday. It is 2.13 km from the centre point, so I put it first.
  2. 11:45, Huis van Alijn: stored as a museum, 4.1 from 426 reviews, open 9 AM to 5 PM on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 10 AM to 6 PM on the weekend and closed on Wednesday. 1.75 km from the Guislain.
  3. 13:00, Sint-Veerleplein: stored as a historic site at Sint-Veerleplein 2, with no rating and no stored hours. 0.19 km from Huis van Alijn. Wikivoyage describes it as the square in front of the castle, so it is the plan's closest tie to the castle.
  4. 13:15, lunch at Pakhuis: stored as a restaurant, 3.9 from 2,338 reviews, open noon to 2:15 PM and 6:30 PM to 10:30 PM (11 PM Friday and Saturday), closed on Sunday. 0.36 km from the square. A 13:15 start leaves one hour of lunch service.
  5. 14:45, Design Museum Gent: stored as a museum, 3.8 from 586 reviews, with no stored hours. 0.28 km from lunch. It is the most reviewed sight in the app's Ghent data, but it falls below my 4.0 line and I cannot match it to a weekday.
  6. 15:45, Museum of Industry Ghent: stored as a museum at Minnemeers 10, 4.3 from 182 reviews, open 9 AM to 5 PM on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, 10 AM to 6 PM on Friday and the weekend, closed on Wednesday. 0.80 km from the Design Museum, with at least an hour and 15 minutes before closing.
  7. 17:45, a drink at 't Dreupelkot: stored as a bar, 4.6 from 253 reviews, open from 4 PM to 1 AM (2 AM on Friday and Saturday). 0.61 km from the Museum of Industry. It is the highest-rated card in the plan.
  8. 19:30, Hot Club Gent: stored as a tourist attraction at Schuddevisstraatje 2, 4.4 from 138 reviews, open from 3 PM to 3 AM (4 AM on Friday and Saturday). 0.02 km from 't Dreupelkot, so the last leg is about 20 m. I read nothing about it beyond the stored card.

Day 2: the centre first, then the museums to the south

Day two runs north to south and back. After a coffee and the Stadshal in the centre, it drops 1.22 km to STAM, then spends the afternoon around the Citadelpark pair of MSK and SMAK, which are 0.10 km apart. The farthest pair, Café Labath and MSK, is 1.87 km apart in a straight line, the longest leg is the 1.65 km back from SMAK to the bakery, and going stop to stop adds up to about 5.94 km. The middle of day two sits about 1.19 km from the middle of day one. Times below are my suggested start times.

  1. 9:00, coffee at Café Labath: stored as a cafe, 4.1 from 170 reviews, open 8 AM to 5:30 PM on Monday to Thursday and 9 AM to 5:30 PM on Friday, Saturday and Sunday. 0.48 km from the centre point, so 9:00 is safe every day.
  2. 10:00, Stadshal / City Pavilion: stored as a historic site, 3.3 from 93 reviews, with no stored hours. 0.53 km from the cafe. The lowest rating among the stops, so treat it as a look, not a destination.
  3. 10:45, STAM - Ghent City Museum: stored as a museum, 4.5 from 575 reviews, the highest-rated of the six sights with 100 or more reviews. Open 9 AM to 5 PM on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, 10 AM to 6 PM on Friday and the weekend, closed on Wednesday. 1.22 km from the Stadshal. Wikipedia describes it as documenting the city's past and its inhabitants.
  4. 12:00, Music Center De Bijloke: stored as a theater and concert hall at Bijlokekaai 7, 4.2 from 17 reviews, with no stored hours. 0.12 km from STAM. Only 17 reviews, so a thin rating; I use it as a stop to look at, not to book.
  5. 13:00, lunch at Umamido Vooruit: stored as a restaurant, 4.5 from 3,195 reviews, open noon to 10 PM every day. 0.73 km from the concert hall.
  6. 14:30, Museum voor Schone Kunsten (MSK): stored as a museum, 4.3 from 515 reviews, open 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM from Tuesday to Friday, 10 AM to 6 PM on the weekend, closed on Monday. 1.18 km from lunch. Wikipedia describes it as holding works by Bosch, Rubens and other Flemish masters.

  8. 17:30, pastry at Aux Merveilleux de Fred: stored as a bakery, 4.4 from 110 reviews, open 8 AM to 6:30 PM every day except Monday, when it is stored as closed. 1.65 km from SMAK, so this is the one leg back toward the centre. A 17:30 start leaves one hour.
  9. 19:00, Opera Ghent: stored as a theater and concert hall at Schouwburgstraat 3, 4.4 from 76 reviews, with no stored hours. 0.40 km from the bakery. With no hours stored, check the programme before you plan an evening around it.

What I left out, and why

The plan uses 12 of the 28 sights and five places to eat or drink. If a stop is closed or does not suit you, pick from the 16 sights I left out, and read the flag on each.

  • Eight art galleries. Francis Maere Fine Arts (5.0 from only 3 reviews, closed Monday to Wednesday, open Thursday to Saturday and on Sunday morning), PARCE, Galerie St-John, BARBE, Kristof De Clercq gallery and Valcke Art Gallery have stored hours and no or almost no ratings; De Buck / Siegfried and AD gallery have neither. Seven of the eight have no rating or reviews.
  • Theaters and shows with few reviews. Capitole Ghent (3.0 from 29 reviews), House of Mysteries (5.0 from 26 reviews, 0.78 km from the centre point) and Theater Tinnenpot have no stored hours; Roodsnor puppet theatre sits 3.22 km out.
  • Karaoke. SingCity Gent (5.0 from 2 reviews) and Baraoke Gent (3.0 from 2 reviews) are stored as tourist attractions with hours, but with two reviews each I could not tell them from a stray rating.
  • Other cards. The Zone (2.20 km out, no rating) and Leopoldskazerne (a historic site with no rating or hours) have too little data to plan around.
  • Other restaurants. 104 of the 213 cards are restaurants, so there is plenty to swap. I used two (Pakhuis and Umamido Vooruit), plus a cafe, a bar and a bakery, chosen by hand for a mix of rating, review count and stored hours.
  • Sights with no stored hours. 12 of the 28 sights have none. I used five of them and flag each one.

How many days do you need in Ghent?

By this data, two. The 28 sights inside 4 km come down to six that clear 4.0 with 100 reviews, and the 2-day plan holds all of them plus Design Museum Gent, SMAK, the Stadshal, Music Center De Bijloke, Opera Ghent and Sint-Veerleplein. What is left is the eight galleries, a few small theatres and two karaoke places. A third day would be a gallery and theatre day, and the data cannot rank those. That is a reading of the data and not a rule: the cards hold no castle, church or park, and a visitor who wants the Gravensteen, St Bavo's Cathedral or the Graslei would add time for them, which the stored data cannot measure.

A real-world cross-check: what a visitor needs to know

  • The centre is small and car-free. Wikivoyage describes it as quite small, so you can walk around on foot, and free of cars; Wikipedia also calls the centre a car-free area. My own numbers agree: 16 of the 17 stops sit within 1.42 km of the app's Ghent centre point, and the plan's legs are 1.75 km or shorter.
  • Day one has one long leg. The 1.75 km from Museum Dr. Guislain to Huis van Alijn is the only leg over 1.65 km in the plan, and it is the reason the Guislain goes first. Roads make real distances longer than straight lines, and I read no transit source.
  • The main station is outside the centre. Wikivoyage says Gent Sint-Pieters is not in the city centre and takes a walk of about half an hour. The station is not among the app's cards, so I measured nothing from it.
  • The big sights are not in the app data. Wikipedia lists the Gravensteen castle, the belfry and the cathedral among the city's highlights, and says the cathedral houses the Ghent Altarpiece; Wikivoyage describes the Graslei as a medieval harbour with many historic buildings. None of these is a card in the app, and I read no official page for opening hours or fees, so I left them out of the timed plan.
  • One gap bridges to the castle. Sint-Veerleplein, which Wikivoyage describes as the square in front of the castle, is stored as a card, so day one passes it. Opening times for the castle itself I did not read.
