Tirana itinerary: 2 days, 19 stops from real app data

Two days are enough for central Tirana in this data: all 28 sight cards the app stores sit within 1.5 km of the centre. Day one is eleven stops around Skanderbeg Square, day two eight stops through the galleries to the south-west. Eleven of the 19 have stored hours; day two works Tuesday to Saturday.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Tirana place data, read on 9 November 2026. Every Tirana row was written on 8 May 2026, so the hours are about six months old: treat them as a snapshot and check before you go.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Skanderbeg Square and the streets around it, on foot: the Skanderbeg statue, the National Theatre of Opera and Ballet, the National Archaeological Museum, the Clock Tower, the Kapllan Pasha Tomb, Bunk'Art 2, lunch at Grand Panevino, the Fortress of Justinian, Rinia Park, the Independence Monument and dinner at Delicatezze Di Mare Da Aldo.
  • Day 2, five galleries, then the south-west: the Gallery of Contemporary Art Tirana, Gallery 43, VivArt Gallery, ZETA Center for Contemporary Art, lunch at Pastaria, Art Gallery E.RIRA, dinner at Hayal Et and a drink at Hemingway Bar.
  • Days of the week: day one works on all seven days. Day two works Tuesday to Saturday; Monday and Sunday each break it.
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 1.57 km by straight line between stored coordinates, day two to about 4.71 km. These are not travel times.

A Tirana deck of 430 places, and 28 sights

The Tirana data in the app is one destination row with 430 cards and 430 distinct place IDs. I kept the cards within 4 km of the stored centre point of the destination, which is 306 places; the other 124 are far away, and at least 26 of them have US-style addresses. Of the 306, 184 have a stored rating and 132 have stored opening hours. The biggest stored categories are restaurants (110), hotels (56), bars (36) and cafes (18). Sights, by the seven stored categories I counted, come to 28: tourist attraction (9), art gallery (7), historic site (4), theater and concert hall (4), museum (2), park (1) and stadium (1). Only 14 of the 28 have stored reviews, 8 have stored hours and 2 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 reviews.

  • Eight of the 19 stops have no stored hours. The Skanderbeg statue, the Opera and Ballet theatre, the Archaeological Museum, the Clock Tower, the Kapllan Pasha Tomb, the Fortress of Justinian, Rinia Park and the Independence Monument. I could not test them against a weekday, and I flag each one below.
  • Ratings are thin. Of the 28 sights, 14 have no rating at all, and three of the art galleries I use are rated on only 9, 13 and 22 reviews. Treat a 4.9 on 9 reviews as a weak signal.
  • Prices and tickets are left out. I read no price or ticket page this run, so check each place's own site the day you go.
  • The data is thin on well-known names. I searched the Tirana cards for the Et'hem Bey Mosque, the National Historical Museum, the Grand Park, Mount Dajti and the Pyramid of Tirana by name and found none of them as cards. Skanderbeg Square's own neighbours are therefore missing from the deck even though a stored gallery address names the Pyramid.

Day 1: Skanderbeg Square and the streets around it

Day one never leaves a patch about half a kilometre across. By straight line between stored coordinates, the farthest pair, the Skanderbeg statue and the Independence Monument, is 0.56 km apart, the longest single leg is 0.26 km, and going stop to stop adds up to about 1.57 km. I read no transit or road source, so I give straight-line distances only and no travel times. Times below are my suggested start times.

  1. 9:30, Skanderbeg statue: stored as a tourist attraction at Place Skanderbeg, with no rating and no stored hours. It is 0.27 km from the centre point. Wikipedia says Skanderbeg Square opened to the public in June 2017 after a renovation that began in 2010 and was restarted in 2016, and that it won the European Prize for Urban Public Space in 2018.
  2. 9:45, National Theatre of Opera and Ballet of Albania: stored as a theater and concert hall at Sheshi Skenderbej, 4.3 from 22 reviews, no stored hours. 0.09 km from the statue. Wikipedia lists the opera house among the buildings around the square. I read nothing on whether you can go in, so treat it as an exterior stop unless you check.
  3. 10:00, National Archaeological Museum: stored as a museum at Sheshi Skenderbej 7, 3.4 from 39 reviews, no stored hours. 0.13 km from the opera house. Check its hours before relying on a 10:00 start.
  4. 10:45, Clock Tower: stored as a historic site at Rruga 28 Nentori, 3.8 from 348 reviews, no stored hours. 0.20 km from the museum. Wikipedia gives its height as 32.5 m, dates it to 1822 to 1830 and places it next to the Et'hem Bey Mosque. The app stores the tower twice, two IDs about 11 m apart with the same rating and review count. I read nothing on visiting hours.
  5. 11:15, Kapllan Pasha Tomb: stored as a historic site on Rruga 28 Nentori, 3.7 from 59 reviews, no stored hours. 0.15 km from the tower. Wikipedia says it was built in 1817, declared a cultural monument in 1948 and restored in 1985.
  6. 11:45, Bunk'Art 2: stored as a museum at Rruga Abdi Toptani, 4.3 from 953 reviews, the most reviewed sight in the data. Stored hours are 9:30 AM to 8 PM every day. 0.18 km from the tomb. The museum's own site, read this run, gives its location as Rruga Abdi Toptani opposite the Municipality and its hours as 9:30 to 18:30 every day, so the stored 8 PM closing looks about an hour and a half late. An 11:45 start fits both, and the site lists no prices.
  7. 13:30, lunch at Grand Panevino: stored as a restaurant at Rruga Abdi Toptani 16, 4.5 from 724 reviews, open 7:30 AM to midnight every day. 0.12 km from Bunk'Art 2.
  8. 14:45, Fortress of Justinian: stored as a historic site, address "Tirana", with no rating and no stored hours. 0.12 km from lunch. I read nothing about this place beyond the card, so I cannot say what you will find there.
  9. 15:15, Rinia Park: stored as a park on Bulevardi Deshmoret e Kombit, 3.9 from 190 reviews, no stored hours. 0.26 km from the fortress pin.
  10. 15:45, Independence Monument: stored as a tourist attraction, 3.6 from 53 reviews, no stored hours. 0.09 km from the park.
  11. 19:00, dinner at Delicatezze Di Mare Da Aldo: stored as a restaurant at Rruga Reshit Collaku 3, 4.7 from 825 reviews, open noon to 1 AM every day. 0.23 km from the monument. The long gap from 16:15 is deliberate: use it to rest or to swap in a stop from the list further down.

Day 2: five galleries, then the south-west

Day two spreads out. The first five stops are galleries and a lunch in a strip running south-west from the square, with legs of 0.29 to 0.47 km. Then it gets longer: 0.62 km to Art Gallery E.RIRA, 0.85 km to dinner, and 1.76 km back north-west to Hemingway Bar, the longest leg and also the farthest pair. Going stop to stop adds up to about 4.71 km. The middle of day two sits about 0.75 km from the middle of day one. Times below are my suggested start times.

  1. 10:00, Gallery of Contemporary Art Tirana: stored as an art gallery, address "Bulevardi Deshmoret e Kombit, Pyramid of Tirana", 4.9 from 9 reviews. Stored hours are 10 AM to 2 PM and 5 PM to 8:30 PM, closed Monday. Wikipedia says the Pyramid opened on 14 October 1988 as the Enver Hoxha Museum, and reopened in October 2023 after a renovation as the TUMO Tirana technology centre. The stored pin is 0.12 km from the centre point, so check where the gallery really is.
  2. 11:00, Gallery 43: stored as an art gallery at Rruga Myslym Shyri 5, 4.5 from 22 reviews, open 9:30 AM to 2 PM and 5 PM to 8 PM every day (Sunday to 2:30 PM). 0.41 km from the first gallery.
  3. 11:45, VivArt Gallery: stored as an art gallery on Pashko Vasa Street, no rating, open 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM from Monday to Saturday and 5 PM to 9 PM on Sunday. 0.47 km from Gallery 43.
  4. 12:30, ZETA Center for Contemporary Art: stored as an art gallery on the second floor of the Hekla Center, no rating, open 11 AM to 6 PM Monday to Friday, 11 AM to 2 PM on Saturday and closed Sunday. 0.29 km from VivArt. A 12:30 start leaves 1 hour 30 minutes on a Saturday.
  5. 13:30, lunch at Pastaria: stored as a restaurant at Rr. Sami Frasheri, 4.4 from 585 reviews, open 11 AM to 11 PM every day. 0.30 km from ZETA.
  6. 15:00, Art Gallery E.RIRA: stored as an art gallery in the Sculptors' Studios on Rr. Medar Shtylla, 4.4 from 13 reviews, open 9 AM to 9 PM Monday to Saturday and closed Sunday. 0.62 km from lunch, and the farthest stop from the centre in the plan at 1.44 km.
  7. 18:30, dinner at Hayal Et: stored as a restaurant at Rruga Sami Frasheri 8, 4.9 from 407 reviews, open 10 AM to 1 AM every day except Friday, when it is stored as opening at 1 PM. 0.85 km from E.RIRA.
  8. 20:30, Hemingway Bar: stored as a tourist attraction at Rr Kont Urani 2, 4.3 from 580 reviews. It is a bar, and it is the second of the only two sights with 4.0 and 100 reviews. Stored hours are 5 PM to 1 AM (6 PM on Thursday, 7 PM on Saturday), closed Sunday. 1.76 km from dinner.

What I left out, and why

Day one covers the sights nearest the centre, so the swap pool is small. If a stop is closed or does not suit you, read the flags below.

  • Six of the nine tourist attractions are not sights. By title, Virtual Games Lounge, an event listing (Live the Music InAlb), Smile Design Tourism, Wilderness Balkans, Visit Tirana and an adult club are businesses or listings, not places to visit. That leaves the Independence Monument, the Skanderbeg statue and Hemingway Bar as the three tourist attractions I used. Jazz in Albania Festival, stored as a theater and concert hall, is also an event, not a venue.
  • Other cards left out. Pellumb Rira Art Studio and Gallery 70 are stored as art galleries with no rating and no hours, so I could not place them on a weekday. The Puppet Theatre (4.0 from 3 reviews) and the Nationaltheater card carry no hours either. Arena Center (4.2 from 10 reviews) is a stadium 1.07 km out.
  • Food and walking tours. Albanian Food Tours (5.0 from 19 reviews, no stored hours) and Outdoor Albania (4.7 from 69 reviews, open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM) are stored, but I read nothing on what they run or cost, so I left them out of the route.
  • Other restaurants. Six restaurants in the data meet 4.4 or higher with at least 500 reviews and stored hours; I used Grand Panevino, Delicatezze and Pastaria from them. The others are Era Vila (4.5 from 2,571 reviews, 0.95 km out), Smoke House Bar&Grill (4.9 from 695) and The Crown Rooftop and Dining (4.9 from 611, 0.38 km out). Smoke House is stored as opening at 12:30 AM, which looks like a data slip, so I did not schedule it.
  • Outside the 4 km radius. Bunk'Art 1 is stored 4.3 km from the centre point, Petrele Castle 8.6 km and Kruja Castle 20.1 km. I read no source on getting there or on their hours, so they are not in the plan.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 9 November 2026 with read-only queries. Tirana is stored as one destination row and 430 cards, with 430 distinct place IDs and 426 distinct places by title and address. All rows were written between 19:48 and 19:50 UTC on 8 May 2026. The data is not limited to the city, so I defined "in Tirana" as a card whose stored coordinates fall within 4 km of the destination's own stored centre point (haversine distance, address text not used). That keeps 306 cards and drops 124; the 124 include at least 26 with US-style addresses, plus places such as Kruja Castle (20.1 km out). I defined sights by stored category name: tourist attraction, art gallery, historic site, theater and concert hall, museum, park and stadium. I first listed all 29 distinct category names within the radius and widened on purpose: the other names (restaurant, hotel, bar, cafe, spa, vacation rental, tours, food tours and similar) are not sights, and amusement place (one escape room), movie theater and tours are the borderline ones I left out. Counts below are of the 306 in-radius cards unless stated. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates; a card counts as having stored hours when the hours list is present and not empty.

  • Of the 306 in-radius cards, 184 have a stored rating and 132 have stored weekly hours. 256 of the 306 sit within 1.5 km of the centre point. The largest categories are restaurants (110), hotels (56), bars (36) and cafes (18). Hotels have no stored hours at all (0 of 56).
  • The 28 sight cards: 14 have a stored rating and reviews, 8 have stored hours, and 2 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews (Bunk'Art 2 at 4.3 from 953 and Hemingway Bar at 4.3 from 580, which is a bar).
