Apps like HappyCow

HappyCow is built to answer one question: where can I eat vegan or vegetarian here? The apps below answer it in three ways. PlantsPack, ItsVegan and Vegan Maps are vegan directories like HappyCow. Google Maps and Tripadvisor are general apps that can narrow to vegan-friendly places. NomadSwipe does neither, and it is listed so you can see where it fits. I read each page on 22 November 2026 and did not install or test any app, so none of this is a ranking by quality.

NomadSwipe

Different need: planning a trip, not checking a menu

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. You pick one or several cities, swipe through real places one card at a time, and save restaurants, sights and stays to one trip map. You can filter that map by category, color-label places and tap them in order to build a route of up to 10 stops. Swiping is the quick way to save or skip a place.

It is not a vegan directory. It has no dietary filter, so it cannot tell you whether a restaurant serves vegan food, and its category filter only separates kinds of places such as restaurants and attractions. For dietary certainty, use HappyCow or another app below. If you want the places you chose pinned on a trip map next to your sights, NomadSwipe is built for that, and a custom pin covers a place the cards miss. Custom pins have a monthly limit on the base plan, so check the Google Play listing.

  • Strength: your saved restaurants, sights and stays on one map, with a route builder.
  • Limit: no vegan, vegetarian or other dietary filter.
  • Limit: Android only today.

HappyCow

Best for: dietary certainty while you travel

HappyCow's mobile page calls it "the world's largest vegan community and food map" and says it covers 180+ countries, with 1M+ reviews, filters for "places that suit everyone", and 4.5 million+ downloads. Those figures are HappyCow's own. Saving places for offline use, one-tap translation and notifications of new spots are marked "app only". Its site also lists vegan hotels and B&Bs and organic stores.

AlternativeTo lists HappyCow for the web, Android and iPhone and calls it freemium with a one-time purchase and a free version with limited functionality. I found no amount on HappyCow's own pages, so check the price in the store. A separate vegan-travel blog I read says HappyCow is still the best all-around restaurant finder and suggests pairing it with other apps rather than replacing it.

  • Strength: built only for vegan and vegetarian food, in the widest coverage any page here states.
  • Limit: it is a finder, not a trip planner, and some features are app only.

PlantsPack

Best for: a vegan directory with no ads

PlantsPack's homepage counts 50,824+ vegan places in 168+ countries and ranks 1,319+ cities. Places are tagged 100% Vegan or Vegan-Friendly, which separates fully vegan kitchens from places with a few options. The site says it is free, ad-free and has no paid listings, and it shows free tools including a barcode scanner and a menu scanner. Its place data comes from OpenStreetMap, per its footer, and its site links to the App Store and Google Play.

One caveat comes from PlantsPack itself: it says most of its top-rated places have one review so far, so treat them as tips, not a ranking. HappyCow's own page claims 1M+ reviews, so for review depth in a big city, compare both before trusting either.

  • Strength: the tag for fully vegan versus vegan-friendly, and no ads.
  • Limit: young review base, by its own account.

ItsVegan

Best for: a community directory, if it loads for you

AlternativeTo describes ItsVegan as a free, community-built vegan directory and world map with thousands of vegan restaurants, cafes, hotels and shops in 100+ countries, plus reviews, recipes and a community. That is all I can confirm: the itsvegan.com site returned a connection error when I tried to read it, so I could not check its platforms, app or coverage.

  • Limit: unverified beyond one description. Open it yourself before relying on it.

Vegan Maps

Best for: vegan places plus recipes and meal plans, on iPhone

The veganmaps.app site says Vegan Maps helps you find vegan restaurants in your area and offers recipes and custom meal plans in the app. Its only download button is for iOS, so I cannot confirm an Android version, and the page states no price, coverage or review feature. Several similarly named apps exist, so check the developer name before you install.

  • Limit: Android not confirmed; little stated about coverage or reviews.

Google Maps

Best for: a quick vegan-friendly search with the app you already have

A TechCult how-to dated 30 January 2025 says the Google Maps app has a dietary setting: open your profile, then Settings, then Manage your preferences, then Dietary, and choose from alcohol free, gluten free, halal, kosher, vegan or vegetarian. It says Maps then shows results that follow your preferences, using menus and information restaurants provide. Google's own Maps help page that I read covers searching for places but does not mention this setting, so confirm it in your version of the app.

  • Strength: nothing new to install, and it already shows hours, photos and directions.
  • Limit: the diet match depends on what restaurants report, so a vegan result is not a vegan guarantee.

Tripadvisor

Best for: popular vegan-friendly restaurants in a big city

Tripadvisor's Travelers' Choice "Best of the Best" Vegan & Vegetarian list describes its winners as having menus that are plant-based, vegan, or vegetarian friendly. The page says the award goes to fewer than 1% of its 8 million listings, based on review volume over 12 months. That makes it a good shortlist of well-reviewed places and a weaker test of a fully vegan kitchen, because "friendly" is a looser bar than "vegan". Tripadvisor's restaurant search pages would not load for me, so I did not check its diet filters, platforms or price.

  • Strength: deep traveler review counts on the award winners.
  • Limit: "friendly" is not "fully vegan".

One option that is gone: abillion

AlternativeTo still lists abillion as a HappyCow alternative, but the abillion.com homepage says the platform has officially shut down, in a note posted 23 March 2026. I left it out of the comparison.

Which to choose

  • If you need to be sure a place is vegan or vegetarian: HappyCow or PlantsPack, which tag or filter by diet, and check the tag on each place.
  • If you want nothing new to install: Google Maps with its dietary setting, then read the menu before you go.
  • If you want popular, well-reviewed vegan-friendly places in a big city: the Tripadvisor award list.
  • If you want the places you picked on one trip map with your sights and a route: NomadSwipe, after you have picked them with one of the apps above.

Short version: HappyCow-class apps decide where you can eat vegan, and NomadSwipe, an Android app, only helps you lay out the places you have already chosen.

Questions people ask

What is the closest app to HappyCow?

From the pages I read, PlantsPack and ItsVegan are the closest: both describe a vegan directory with a map and reviews. each says it covers well over a hundred countries. I did not install any of them, so test your own city in each.

Is NomadSwipe an alternative to HappyCow?

No. NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. It has no vegan or vegetarian filter and cannot tell you whether a menu suits your diet. Use a HappyCow-class app for that, and NomadSwipe to put the places you picked on one trip map.

Can Google Maps find vegan restaurants?

A third-party how-to from TechCult (30 January 2025) says the Google Maps app has a Dietary setting that includes vegan and vegetarian, and that results follow the information restaurants provide. Google's own help page I read does not mention it, so check that the setting exists in your version of the app.

Is abillion still an option?

No. The abillion.com homepage says the platform has officially shut down, in a note posted 23 March 2026. AlternativeTo still lists it as an alternative.

How much does HappyCow cost?

I could not find a price on HappyCow's own pages. AlternativeTo describes HappyCow as a one-time purchase with a free version that has limited functionality, but gives no amount, so check the store listing.

Does Tripadvisor guarantee a place is fully vegan?

No. The Travelers' Choice list I read describes its winners as plant-based, vegan, or vegetarian friendly. That is a friendly tier, not a promise that a kitchen is fully vegan.
