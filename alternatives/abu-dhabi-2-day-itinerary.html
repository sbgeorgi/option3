Abu Dhabi in 2 days: 15 stops matched to opening days

An Abu Dhabi 2-day plan of 15 stops from the NomadSwipe app's place data, with every start time tested against stored opening hours. Day one runs from Saadiyat Island to the Corniche, day two from the mangroves and Khalifa Park out to Yas Island. I assumed a Tuesday and a Wednesday, and the plan also passes on the other five weekdays.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Abu Dhabi place data, read on 7 November 2026. The rows I used were last updated on 8 and 9 May 2026, so treat them as a six-month-old snapshot. Hours may have changed since then.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Tuesday, Saadiyat Island and the Corniche: breakfast at White, Saadiyat Beach Club, the Louvre Abu Dhabi, teamLab Phenomena, Abu Dhabi Art, Corniche Beach and dinner at Hakkasan Abu Dhabi.
  • Day 2, Wednesday, the mangroves, Khalifa Park and Yas Island: breakfast at Zahrat Lebnan, Mangrove National Park, Murjan Splash Park, Khalifa Park, Yas Beach, MADCAP Abu Dhabi, dinner at Mr. Kim Korean Bbq and The Fountains at Yas Mall.
  • Hours: 13 of the 15 stops have stored weekly hours and all 13 pass on every weekday at my start times. The Louvre Abu Dhabi and Mangrove National Park have none stored, so I cannot test them and I say so in the plan.
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 21.8 km and day two to about 29.1 km by straight line between stored coordinates. These are not driving times, and a few coordinates look unreliable (see the notes on the data below).

An Abu Dhabi deck of 394 places, with few sight hours

The app stores Abu Dhabi as one destination row holding 459 cards. Inside my city filter that is 415 cards, which become 394 distinct places once cards with the same title and address are merged. The list is mostly places to eat: 175 restaurants and 54 hotels. Sights are the minority, 48 places, about one in eight, in 9 stored categories: amusement places (11), art galleries (9), parks (8), beaches (7), museums (6), tourist attractions (4), and one each of stadium, historical site and zoo. Only 29 of the 48 have a rating, 22 have stored hours and 12 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 reviews. This plan uses 10 of those 12.

  • Parks are the thin spot. Of 8 park cards, only Khalifa Park has stored hours, and one of the three park cards that pass my rating line is a hotel (see below). Mangrove National Park has no stored hours and goes in flagged.
  • Prices, tickets and transport are left out. I read no official page this run, so I quote no fees, ticket rules or taxi times. Check each place's own site the day you go.
  • Add your own pins. A place the data lacks is yours to add on the map.

Which days of the week work

I parsed the stored weekly hours of the 13 stops that have them with a script and tested each start time on each of the seven weekdays. A stop fails a day if it is stored as closed, if its hours do not cover the start time, or if the start leaves under one hour before closing. The script reads day names rather than list positions, because the stored week starts on Saturday on most cards and on Friday on others. Times past midnight and split windows, such as White's breakfast and dinner, are handled.

Day 1: Saadiyat Island and the Corniche

Day one begins on Saadiyat Island, where five of the seven stops sit within 6 km of each other, and ends on the waterfront side of town. By straight line between stored coordinates, the legs run 0.36, 5.88, 0.94, 1.54, 10.75 and 2.35 km, about 21.8 km in all. The farthest pair, White and Hakkasan, is 17.5 km apart. The one long leg is the 10.75 km from Abu Dhabi Art to Corniche Beach. I sourced no lunch stop for this day, so the block between the Louvre and teamLab is yours to fill.   4. 14:30, teamLab Phenomena: stored as a museum in the Cultural District, 4.7 from 297 reviews, open 10 AM to 7 PM every day, so a 14:30 start leaves four and a half hours. 0.94 km from the Louvre.

Notes on the data

A few cards are off in ways that affect the plan, and I would rather say so than hide them.

  • A hotel filed as a park. The Anantara Eastern Mangroves Hotel & Spa card is stored as a park with the same 4.5 rating and 484 reviews as Mangrove National Park and the same street address, 0.9 km away by coordinates. It looks like one listing's numbers on two cards. I counted both in the 12 that pass my line and used only the national park.
  • Two Zahrat Lebnan cards. One has the rating, reviews and hours I used. The other, with the same title and address, has no rating or hours and coordinates 7.6 km away. Both can be right for different branches or one can be wrong, and I cannot tell which from the data.
  • The splash park and the park. Murjan Splash Park's address says it is inside Khalifa Park, but the stored coordinates put the two cards 3.31 km apart. I kept the order and the straight-line figure, and you may find them closer than the numbers say.
  • Two Louvre cards. A second Louvre Abu Dhabi card, at a delivery-access address 0.03 km away, has no rating and no hours. I merged it into the first.
  • Hours that look wrong. Al Bateen Beach (3.9 from 169 reviews) is stored as closed every day except 5 PM to 6 PM on Tuesday, and I left it out.

What I left out, and why

  • Cards outside the city. 44 of the 459 cards fall outside my coordinate box. 12 have a Dubai address, and the rest include the US, the Maldives and other places, among them three New York and Washington botanic gardens that carry high ratings.
  • SeaWorld Abu Dhabi. It passes my line (4.8 from 2,082 reviews, stored as open 10 AM to 8 PM every day) and is 1.09 km from MADCAP by straight line. I left it out because I sourced no visit length and day two is already full. It can replace Yas Beach and MADCAP if it is why you came.
  • Other sights with hours. The Etihad Modern Art Gallery (4.0 from 75 reviews, closed Friday) and Zayed Sports City (4.3 from 97 reviews, stored as closed on Saturday and Sunday) fall just under my 100-review line.
  • Sights with no stored hours. 26 of the 48 sights have none. I used two, the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Mangrove National Park, and I flag both in the plan.
  • Other restaurants. I checked cards near Saadiyat, the Corniche, Muroor Road and Yas by hand and kept the four with the best fit of rating and stored hours. Hotels are skipped for a day plan.

Getting around between the stops

  • Each day has two halves. Day one is a compact island cluster, then a long leg to the waterfront. Day two is a mainland half, then a long leg to Yas Island, where the last four stops are close together. I read no transit, taxi or parking source, so I give no travel times, fares or parking advice.
  • Straight lines understate the trip. Roads make each real leg longer than the figures above, and some stored coordinates are doubtful.
  • In the app: each card shows its distance and opening status, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z.

Questions about this Abu Dhabi plan

Which days of the week work for this Abu Dhabi plan?

At the suggested start times, both days pass on all seven weekdays by the stored hours, with a one-hour margin before closing. I assumed day one is a Tuesday and day two a Wednesday. What changes with the weekday is how early a stop can start: Khalifa Park is stored as opening at 3 PM from Monday to Wednesday and at 10 AM on the other days, MADCAP Abu Dhabi as opening at noon from Monday to Friday and at 10 AM on Saturday and Sunday, and Hakkasan Abu Dhabi as serving lunch on Saturday only. The Louvre Abu Dhabi and Mangrove National Park have no stored hours, so they are not tested. The hours were read on 7 November 2026 and may have changed, so confirm them before you go.

Do I need a car for this Abu Dhabi plan?

I read no transit, taxi or parking source, so I cannot say what is available. By straight line between stored coordinates, day one has one long leg, 10.75 km from Abu Dhabi Art to Corniche Beach, and its other legs are 5.88 km or less. Day two has two long legs: 13.07 km from Khalifa Park to Yas Beach and 7.11 km from the mangroves to Murjan Splash Park. The farthest pair on day two, Zahrat Lebnan and Mr. Kim Korean Bbq, is 18.55 km apart. Treat both days as rides or drives between clusters and check your own options.

Where are the mosques and palaces in this plan?

I searched the in-city cards for titles containing Mosque, Palace, Qasr, Heritage, Corniche or Emirates, and for the Arabic words for mosque (مسجد and جامع) and palace or castle (قصر). No card matched the mosque words. Qasr Al Watan is stored as a historical site with no rating, no review count and no stored hours, so I could not rank it or test it against a weekday, and Heritage Park has the same gaps. The other matches were hotels, restaurants and beaches. If a mosque or palace is why you came, add it to your own map and check its hours at the source.

How were the 15 stops chosen?

I picked cards by hand from the part of the data that sits inside Abu Dhabi: for sights, a stored rating of 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews, and for the four restaurants, a rating of 4.5 or higher with at least 100 reviews and stored hours. Then I tested each suggested start time against the stored weekly hours. Ten stops are sights from the 12 that meet the line, one is a sight that does not (The Fountains - Yas Mall, 4.1 from 42 reviews), and four are restaurants. Two of the 15 have no stored hours, the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Mangrove National Park, and I kept them for their ratings and review counts.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 7 November 2026 with read-only queries. Abu Dhabi is stored as one destination row (country code ARE) with 459 cards. I excluded destination rows with the country "Custom", which hold user-typed names, and none were involved here. The 459 cards carry 459 distinct place IDs, but the IDs in this data are one per card, so that count says nothing about duplicates. I counted places by case-insensitive title and address instead. The data is not limited to the city, so I defined "in Abu Dhabi" as a card whose stored coordinates fall inside a rough box (latitude 24.2 to 24.65, longitude 54.2 to 54.8). That keeps 415 cards, which merge into 394 places, and drops 44 cards. Where I merged duplicates I kept the card with stored hours, then the one with more reviews. An empty stored hours list counts as no stored hours. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates. The rows I used were last updated on 8 and 9 May 2026.

  • Of the 394 places, 215 have a stored rating (55%), 175 of those are rated 4.0 or higher (81% of the rated ones) and 100 are rated 4.5 or higher (47%). The median stored rating is 4.4. 175 of the 394 have stored weekly hours (44%). By category, 175 are restaurants and 54 are hotels.
  • I defined sights by stored category: tourist attractions, theaters and concert halls, parks, art galleries, historical sites, museums, gardens, amusement places, zoos, beaches and stadiums. After also merging same-title cards with coordinates within 0.005 degrees (the two Louvre cards), that gives 48 sights (about one in eight of the places) in 9 stored categories: amusement place (11), art gallery (9), park (8), beach (7), museum (6), tourist attraction (4), and one each of stadium, historical site and zoo. Garden and theater and concert hall appear in the data only outside the city box.
  • Sight cards are mixed on data. Of the 48 sights, 29 have a stored rating (60%), 22 have stored hours (46%, with 26 having none), and 12 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews, of which 9 have hours. Of the 12, 3 are amusement places, 3 are beaches, 3 are parks (one a hotel card, see above), 2 are museums and 1 is an art gallery. The 100 line is mine, not a rule in the app. Of the 8 park cards only 1 has stored hours.
  • Of the 175 places with stored hours, 17 show at least one closed day (about one in ten): Sunday 9, Friday 6, Saturday 5, Monday 4. None shows Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday as closed.
  • The 44 cards outside the box include 12 with a Dubai address and others stored with US, Maldives and similar addresses.
  • Landmark search: in-city titles were searched for Mosque, Palace, Qasr, Heritage, Corniche, Emirates and the Arabic words مسجد, جامع and قصر. No mosque card matched. Qasr Al Watan (historical site) and Heritage Park matched with no rating and no hours, and the rest were hotels, restaurants and beaches, so I could not include the mosque or the palaces as tested stops.
  • I chose the 15 stops by hand: sights with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 100 stored reviews (lowest rating 4.2, fewest reviews 124 among the ten qualifying sights I used), one exception, The Fountains - Yas Mall (4.1 from 42 reviews), and four restaurants rated 4.5 or higher with at least 100 reviews and stored hours (fewest reviews 110), chosen to keep each day in nearby clusters and fit the stored hours. They are not simply the 15 highest-rated cards. Eleven are sights, ten of which are among the 12 that meet the line, and 13 of the 15 have stored hours.
  • Using straight-line distance between stored coordinates, day one totals about 21.8 km (longest leg 10.75 km, farthest pair 17.5 km) and day two about 29.1 km (longest leg 13.07 km, farthest pair 18.55 km). The centres of the two days are about 15.5 km apart. These are not driving times.
  • Weekday test: with a one-hour margin before closing, the 13 stops that have stored hours pass on all seven weekdays for both days. The four deliberately bad start times described above fail on the days stated. The test script is mine and was run on the stored values.

Limits: the ratings, review counts and hours are stored values that may have changed since the dates above, and I did not compare any of them with an official source. The Louvre Abu Dhabi and Mangrove National Park have no stored hours and are not weekday-tested. The suggested start times are mine and are checked only against stored hours. The city box and the title-and-address merge are my own definitions and were not checked card by card, so a few duplicates or misplaced cards may remain, and several coordinates in this deck look unreliable. I did not map every card against its address. I left out fees, tickets, parking, transit and travel times because I read no official page for them, and I did not copy any third-party review text.
