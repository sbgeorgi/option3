What it costs, and how to get out

The plans page I read on 25 November 2026 offered a Plus tab and a Peak tab, in English (US) with dollar amounts and no country named. Plus read "7 days free, then $29.99/year", with a trial timeline of today, a reminder on day 5 and a charge on day 7. Peak read "7 days free, then $65.99/year", but only after the page was switched to its Peak tab with a script, so a quick visit can miss it. Neither tab showed a monthly price, and I did not log in, so I cannot see regional or one-off offers.

  • The trial: the free trial article says it covers Plus or Peak, needs a credit card on desktop, and bills the annual price straight away if you have used a trial before. A current Plus member cannot start a Peak trial.
  • Moving up: if you bought Plus from AllTrails directly, the upgrade article says you get a prorated refund for unused Plus months, then a full-price Peak year starts. Apple and Google purchases follow their own steps.
  • Leaving: turning off auto-renew keeps your access until the year you paid for ends (cancel article). Refunds must be requested within 30 days, and Apple purchases go through Apple (refund article).

Where the pages disagree or stay silent

  • Community heatmap: the benefits article, the overlays article and the heatmap article put it in Plus and Peak, while the upgrade article lists Community Heatmaps among the things Peak adds. I went with the three that agree.
  • The plans page is shorter than the help center: it lists five Plus features and five Peak features, while the benefits article names more than a dozen. The matrix uses the help center for the extra rows, which is why I link every row.
  • No Pro plan: the plans page and help center name two paid plans, Plus and Peak. The help center's footer link labelled "Maps" points to an alltrails.com/pro address, which is where the word survives.
  • Route download limits: the plans page promises "Unlimited Map Downloads" for both plans, and the pages state no download cap for free, so this page makes no claim either way.

The short version

As of 25 November 2026, AllTrails' plans page shows Plus at $29.99 a year and Peak at $65.99 a year, each with a 7-day trial. Plus is the plan that matters for most hikers because it holds offline maps, wrong-turn alerts and Live Share. Peak is for building routes and reading terrain, and I would not buy it for the heatmap.

Questions about AllTrails paid vs free

What does AllTrails Plus add over the free tier?

On AllTrails' own help pages, Plus adds downloads of trails, parks and areas for offline use, wrong-turn alerts, turn-by-turn directions, Live Share, Garmin and COROS syncing, 3D maps, printed maps, trail conditions and the community heatmap (benefits article, read 25 November 2026). The pages I read name no tier for recording an activity or following a route on screen, so what free covers there is not confirmed.

How much do AllTrails Plus and Peak cost?

On the plans page, read 25 November 2026 in USD with no country shown, Plus read "7 days free, then $29.99/year" and Peak read "7 days free, then $65.99/year". The page showed no monthly price for either. Prices can differ by country and offer, so trust the figure at checkout over this one.

Is there a tier called AllTrails Pro?

Not on the pages I read. The plans page offers Plus and Peak, and the help center describes two premium levels, Plus and Peak. The help center's footer link labelled "Maps" still points to an alltrails.com/pro address, so the word survives in a URL, not as a plan name.

Can I cancel AllTrails Plus or get a refund?

Yes, on the help center's terms. Turning off auto-renew keeps access until the end of the yearly billing period (cancel article). Refund requests must come within 30 days of purchase or renewal, and AllTrails cannot refund an Apple App Store purchase, which you must ask Apple for (refund article). Both read 25 November 2026.
