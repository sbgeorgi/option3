Tripsy vs Google Maps: Which One Fits Trip Planning?

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), which appears once below as a third option. Facts about Tripsy and Google Maps checked on 14 November 2026. Updated 14 November 2026.

Tripsy vs Google Maps for trip planning: pick Tripsy to keep flights, stays, documents and expenses in one itinerary, and Google Maps to find places, save lists and navigate. Tripsy has no native Android app yet; Google Maps does. Tripsy can pull in Google Maps lists, but only with a Pro plan.

Key facts

  • Tripsy's Android page said on 14 November 2026 that there is no native Android app yet. You can view itineraries in a browser, but the page says to create and edit trips in the Tripsy app, which its App Store listing shows on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.
  • On Google Play that day, Google Maps showed 3.4 from 19.6M reviews and 10B+ downloads, updated 12 November 2026, with a "Contains ads" label.
  • Tripsy's US App Store page shows 4.7 from 5.7K ratings and "Free · In-App Purchases". Its Pro page showed a Black November sale: yearly $19 (listed as $39) and lifetime $149 (listed as $199).
  • The two connect: the Tripsy App Store text says Smart Import can turn Google Maps lists into places and reservations, and that it requires Tripsy Pro on supported system versions.
  • Google's help pages cap multi-stop directions at 9 stops including the destination, and say transit directions cannot have multiple destinations.

Five questions that decide it

I built this walk-through from the pages above, in the order I would ask the questions. Each step says which app the pages favour.

  1. Which phone do you carry? On Android, Google Maps installs natively and Tripsy does not: its Android page offers a browser view and a waitlist, and says to create and edit in the Tripsy app. On iPhone both work. Google Maps if you are on Android.
  2. Where do your bookings live? Tripsy's site says to forward reservation emails and your flights, stays and activities appear in the trip, with flight alerts on top. Google Maps's Play listing mentions making reservations and booking hotels, but I found nothing about keeping your confirmations in one place. Tripsy.
  3. Do you already have a pile of saved places? Google's You tab article shows where lists and recent places sit, and the Play listing says lists can be shared. Tripsy's App Store text says Smart Import reads Google Maps lists, which makes the two a pair rather than rivals. Keep the lists in Maps and import them into Tripsy if you are on Apple devices and have Pro.
  4. Do you need turn-by-turn directions across many stops? Google's directions article allows up to 9 stops including the final destination, for every mode except transit and flight. Tripsy also builds multi-stop routes with arrival estimates, but its text says you open directions in your preferred navigation app. Google Maps for the driving itself.
  5. Will you be offline? Tripsy's App Store text says the itinerary is available offline. Google's offline maps article says you download an area first and that offline you lose transit, cycling and walking directions. Tripsy for the plan, Maps for the map, if you will carry both.

Where the pages leave gaps

Two things stood out while reading. Tripsy's Android page says to "create and edit in the Tripsy app", and the only store listing found for it is Apple's, so an Android phone can read a trip but cannot start one. And the Tripsy pricing I read today is a sale: the Pro page shows $39 and $199 marked as the old prices next to $19 and $149. Treat all four numbers as a snapshot, because they will change when the sale ends.

Google Maps has the opposite problem. Its own help article called Plan your commute or trip is about when to leave, traffic and route choice, not about building an itinerary. For a multi-day trip Google points to lists, and its separate My Maps help says a map can hold up to 10,000 lines, shapes or places and a layer 2,000. See Google My Maps alternatives if that is where you are headed. Ratings also need care: Google Maps's 3.4 from 19.6M reviews and Tripsy's 4.7 from 5.7K ratings come from different stores, different audiences and different jobs, so they are not a head-to-head score.

Who each fits

Tripsy

Tripsy suits an Apple user whose trip is mostly logistics: flights with delay alerts, hotels, tickets, documents, expenses and a shared plan with different permissions for each guest. Its Pro page sells a yearly plan and a lifetime plan, and its App Store text says some features need Pro. If you are paying for a group, I found nothing on whether guests need their own Pro. For comparison with other options, see Tripsy alternatives for Android.

Google Maps

Google Maps suits anyone who wants to decide where to go by looking at the map, check what is open and busy, and get driving, transit or walking directions once there. It is the only one of the two with a native Android app, and its lists are the lightest way to keep places together. It does not try to hold your bookings or expenses on the pages I read. For more on lists and offline use, see Google Maps lists alternatives and Google Maps as an offline trip planner.

FAQ

Is Tripsy or Google Maps better for trip planning?

They do different jobs. Tripsy is built to hold a trip's bookings, flights, documents and expenses in one itinerary, and Google Maps is built to find places, save them in lists and navigate to them. If you are on Android the choice is mostly made for you, because Tripsy has no native Android app yet. I did not install either app, so this is based on what their own pages say.

Is there a Tripsy app for Android?

Not yet. The Tripsy Android page said on 14 November 2026 that there is no native Android app, that you can view itineraries in a browser at my.tripsy.app, and that you create and edit trips in the Tripsy app. It lists an Android waitlist. The App Store lists Tripsy for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Can Tripsy import my Google Maps lists?

Its App Store description says Smart Import turns Google Maps and Apple Maps lists, screenshots, documents, pasted text and supported links into places and reservations, which you review before importing. The same text says Smart Import uses online processing and requires Tripsy Pro on supported system versions. I did not run an import.

Does Google Maps work offline?

Partly. Google's help page says you can download an area and navigate offline, as long as the whole route is inside the downloaded map. While offline, transit, cycling and walking directions are unavailable, and driving gives no traffic information or alternate routes. Downloads are not possible in some countries or regions.

How much does Tripsy cost?

The app is free to download with in-app purchases, per its US App Store page. On 14 November 2026 the Tripsy Pro page showed a Black November sale: a yearly plan at $19 (listed as $39) and a lifetime plan at $149 (listed as $199). Sale prices change, so check the page before you buy.
