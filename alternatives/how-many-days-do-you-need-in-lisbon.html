How many days do you need in Lisbon? 39 sights counted

How many days do you need in Lisbon? The NomadSwipe app's Lisbon data has 23 sights rated 4.0 or higher with 100 or more reviews within 5 km of the centre. At about five sights a day that is 4.6 days, so I would allow four, and three if you stay in the central districts. It is a counting rule, not a measured fact.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this page. Every number comes from the NomadSwipe app's stored Lisbon place data, read with read-only queries on 9 November 2026, except one line about a museum closure that comes from Wikipedia (see Sources). I read no visit times, prices or fares.

The counting rule

The data has no visit times, so I cannot measure how long a sight takes. I count instead, and I state the rule so you can change it. Days = sights worth seeing ÷ sights per day. I took the pace from the data rather than guessing: the Lisbon deck falls into four areas that hold 6, 5, 6 and 4 well-rated sights (table below), a mean of 5.25, and an area is the natural unit of a day because the stops in it are a short walk or a short ride apart. So I use 5 a day as the middle case and 4 and 6 as the slow and fast cases. My Lisbon 2-day itinerary is the fast case: it lists 16 places, 12 of them sights, so 6 sights a day.

"Well-rated" is also my choice: a sight stored with a rating of 4.0 or higher and 100 or more reviews. "Sight" means a stored type from a written list: art museum, history museum, museum, national museum, science museum, art gallery, lookout, park, landmark, historical landmark, monument, memorial park, plaza, bridge, zoo, marina, and one arts-and-entertainment complex. That is 17 types. I left out tour sellers, escape rooms, cinemas, spas, a visitor desk and Tram 28, which is typed as transportation.

Two things to read carefully. First, the National Museum of Ancient Art has no stored hours, and Wikipedia says it has been closed for renovation since 29 September 2025, with no re-opening date announced as of September 2026. If that is still true, day 3 holds 5 well-rated sights and the 3-day total is 16, not 17. Second, the zoo and the Azulejo museum are two of the most-reviewed sights in the deck but sit away from every area, so I treat them as swaps for a lighter day, not a day of their own. Ponte 25 de Abril is a bridge you see from the river, so on day 4 it costs minutes, not an hour.

That pattern lets you order the four days. Start on a Monday and the Gulbenkian area is open (only the Mãe d'Água reservoir is shut), Tuesday is open for all of Alfama, Wednesday is open for Baixa, and Thursday is open for the river west. In that order, none of the stops in the table above hits a stored closure except the Mãe d'Água reservoir on the Monday. Start on a Friday or at a weekend and you will need to swap. Sunday shuts the Carris, Pharmacy and GNR museums, so the Baixa and river-west days should not land on a Sunday. If you only have Monday for Alfama, you keep its three viewpoints and lose four of its five museums.

Across the three paces the well-rated sights need between 3.8 and 5.8 days, which is why I would allow four, and five if you want the zoo and the Azulejo museum. Seeing every card is a week or more, and most of the cards past the first 23 have few or no reviews.

Who needs fewer days, who needs more

  • Two days is enough if you want Alfama, Graça, Baixa and Chiado. That is 11 of the 23 well-rated sights, and the Lisbon 2-day itinerary shows a faster route that also reaches the Gulbenkian park.
  • Three days is the minimum for the central half of the deck: 17 of 23 well-rated sights, or 16 while the Ancient Art museum is closed. It leaves out the river west, the zoo and the Azulejo museum.
  • Four days is my default. Four areas, 21 of the 23 well-rated sights, and the order from the opening-day table avoids almost every stored closure.
  • Five days or more if you walk slowly (4 a day makes it 5.8 days for the same 23), want the zoo and the Azulejo museum, or add places the deck lacks. Searching card titles found no card for the Sé cathedral, Jerónimos or Santa Justa, and the deck's only castle card is the archaeological site inside the castle walls.
  • Add about a day for the far side. Beyond 5 km the deck has 6 more well-rated sights, 5.7 to 7.7 km from the centre: Belém Tower, the Monument to the Discoveries, the Palácio Nacional da Ajuda, the National Coach Museum, the Oceanário and the MAC/CCB art museum. Six sights at five a day is 1.2 days.
  • Food and nightlife do not change the number. The deck has 164 places to eat and drink within 5 km, 67 of them rated 4.0+ with 100+ reviews. At about three food stops a day you use a handful.

What this count cannot tell you

  • How long a sight takes. The data has no visit times, so five a day is my assumption, not a measurement. The Gulbenkian Museum can take half a day on its own.
  • Prices, tickets and getting around. I did not read them for this page, so check each place's own site and a transit map.
  • Opening days. 22 of the 39 sights have stored hours, and they are an October 2026 snapshot. Check the day you plan to go.
  • Sights the deck lacks. A deck holds only the places the app has, so a famous sight with no card is not counted.

Questions about how many days to spend in Lisbon

Is 3 days enough in Lisbon?

Three days covers the three central areas, Alfama and Graça, Baixa and Chiado, and the Amoreiras and Gulbenkian side. In the app's data that is 17 of the 23 sights rated 4.0 or higher with 100 or more reviews, or 16 if the National Museum of Ancient Art is still closed for renovation. Three days leaves out the river-west cluster (LX Factory, Santo Amaro, the Carris museum), the zoo and the Azulejo museum, so it is enough only if you accept that.

Is 2 days enough in Lisbon?

Two days covers Alfama and Graça plus Baixa and Chiado, which is 11 of the 23 well-rated sights at about 5 or 6 a day. My Lisbon 2-day itinerary packs 12 sights into two days by running across the city, which is the fast end of the pace table above. For the area-by-area answer, see is 2 days enough in Lisbon.

Which day of the week is worst for sightseeing in Lisbon?

Monday, in the app's stored opening hours. Of the 22 sights with stored hours, 8 are closed on Monday, including four of the five Alfama-area museums in the deck: the National Pantheon, the Fado Museum, the Aljube Museum and the Museu de Lisboa Roman Theatre. Tuesday closes the Gulbenkian Museum and its modern art centre. Wednesday, Thursday and Friday close none of them. Hours are a snapshot from October 2026, so check the day you go.

Do restaurants change how many days I need in Lisbon?

Not in this data. Within 5 km the deck has 164 places to eat and drink, 67 of them rated 4.0 or higher with 100 or more reviews. At about three food or drink stops a day, four days use 12, so sights set the day count and food does not.

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 9 November 2026 with read-only queries. No row named Lisboa exists. I measured distance by straight line from that stored centre point and kept the 256 cards within 5 km. I grouped cards by their stored type: sights are the 17 types listed above, and 39 cards qualify. "Well-rated" means a stored rating of 4.0 or higher with 100 or more stored reviews. I grouped the sights into areas by stored coordinates and computed the days myself as sights divided by sights per day.

  • Within 5 km: 39 sight cards across 17 types, 164 places to eat and drink, 41 activity and service cards, and 12 stays.
