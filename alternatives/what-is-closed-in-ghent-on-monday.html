What is closed in Ghent on Monday? 9 of 16 sights

What is closed in Ghent on Monday? Mostly art museums and galleries. In the NomadSwipe app's stored hours, 9 of 16 Ghent sights with hours show Monday as closed: MSK, S.M.A.K., Museum Dr. Guislain and six small galleries. The Gravensteen, STAM, Design Museum Gent and St Bavo's Cathedral are open on Monday per their official pages.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this page. The numbers come from my own read-only query on the app's stored Ghent place data, read on 11 November 2026, and I checked the big venues against their own sites the same day. I have not been to these places for this page. The stored hours are scraped text, possibly months old, so confirm on each venue's page before you go.

Four venues I checked that are not in the stored sample

Ghent's best-known sights are not in the sight sample above, so the app tells me nothing about their Monday status. I read their official pages on 11 November 2026 instead.

  • Gravensteen (Castle of the Counts): the city's information page says it is open every day from 10 am to 6 pm, last admission at 4.40 pm, closed on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. Monday is a normal opening day.
  • STAM (Ghent city museum): its practical-info page says it is closed on Wednesdays, also on public holidays and in school holidays, and on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. Monday is not listed as a closing day.
  • St Bavo's Cathedral and the Ghent Altarpiece: the opening-hours page lists the cathedral Monday to Saturday 8:30 am to 5.30 pm and the Mystic Lamb Visitor's Centre Monday to Saturday 10 am to 5 pm. Entry to the altarpiece closes at 5 pm. Visit Gent says the upper register of the inner panels is being restored and that on Tuesdays to Fridays the restorers can be watched at the Museum of Fine Arts, so check what is on show before you plan around it.
  • Design Museum Gent: its visit page says it is open every day except Wednesdays, with a few exceptions on some Wednesdays, and closed on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January.

One pattern from these pages: the art museums I checked (MSK, S.M.A.K.) close on Monday, while the city museum (STAM) and the design museum close on Wednesday. Monday and Wednesday each take out a different group, so a Monday plan and a Wednesday plan in Ghent use different venues.

What to do on a Monday in Ghent

This plan uses only places whose official page shows Monday as open. It is a sequence, not a measured route: the app holds no walking times, so I give none.

  1. Morning: Gravensteen. It opens at 10 am. Last admission is 4.40 pm, so it is not a stop to leave for the end of the day.
  2. Midday: STAM or Design Museum Gent. Both are open on Monday. STAM's page also mentions school-holiday closures, so check your dates.
  3. Late afternoon: St Bavo's Cathedral. The cathedral is open until 5.30 pm, but entry to the altarpiece and the visitor centre both end at 5 pm, so go before then if the altarpiece is the point.
  4. Move to Tuesday or later: MSK, S.M.A.K. and Museum Dr. Guislain. Dr. Guislain's page lists 10:00 to 17:00 from Tuesday to Friday.

Questions about Ghent on Monday

Are museums in Ghent closed on Monday?

Several of the main art museums are. In the app's stored hours, 9 of 16 Ghent sights with hours show Monday as closed, including the Museum voor Schone Kunsten (MSK), S.M.A.K. and Museum Dr. Guislain. MSK's and S.M.A.K.'s own sites say they are closed on Mondays. STAM and Design Museum Gent are closed on Wednesdays instead, so they are open on Monday.

Is the Gravensteen open on Monday?

Yes. The city's page for the Castle of the Counts, read on 11 November 2026, says it is open every day from 10 am to 6 pm, with last admission at 4.40 pm, and closed on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. The app has no stored hours for it, so this comes from the official page only.

Is STAM open on Monday?

Yes. STAM's practical-info page says it is closed on Wednesdays, also on public holidays and in school holidays, and on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. Monday is not a closing day on that page. Because the page says closed in school holidays, check the dates of your trip.

Is St Bavo's Cathedral open on Monday?

Yes. The cathedral's opening-hours page lists Monday to Saturday 8:30 am to 5.30 pm, with entry to the Ghent Altarpiece closing at 5 pm, and the Mystic Lamb Visitor's Centre Monday to Saturday 10 am to 5 pm. Visit Gent adds that part of the altarpiece is being restored, so check what is on display.

How reliable are the stored closing days in the app?

Treat them as a planning prior, not opening times. They are scraped text and may be months old. In this check, the closed-on-Monday status matched the official site for MSK and S.M.A.K., and the official Dr. Guislain page lists no Monday hours, which is consistent with the stored text. I did not check the six small galleries. Check the venue's own page the week you go.
