TripScout app not working? Which TripScout it is, and what to try

TripScout app not working? Check which TripScout you have: two different iPhone apps share the name, and I found none on Google Play. On 13 November 2026 the TripScout AI planner's website and App Store listing were both live, so no outage showed up. On an iPhone, Apple's four official fixes are below.

Updated 13 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), a different kind of trip app, and I mention it in one short section near the end. I did not install or test either TripScout. Every TripScout fact comes from a page I read on 13 November 2026, linked inline and listed under Sources.

Key facts

  • Platform: both TripScout apps found are iPhone-only. The TripScout site says an Android version is "on the way" and gives no date.
  • Status on 13 November 2026: tripscout.app and both store listings loaded, and no outage notice or status page turned up.
  • Minimum iOS: 15.1 for the AI planner, 17.6 for the Map Tracker, per their App Store pages.
  • Official fix: Apple lists four steps: close and reopen, restart, update, then delete and reinstall. Then contact the developer.
  • Account: the AI planner's privacy policy says it needs no sign-up, so a login problem is unlikely there.

Is TripScout down or shut down?

I found no sign of either. On 13 November 2026 the tripscout.app home page, its privacy policy and its terms loaded, and Apple's lookup record still lists the AI planner. What stands out is age: the record shows version 1.2.0 released 15 May 2026, and its release notes read "Place details now has more useful info", "Design changes" and "Performance improvements". I found no newer version. A quiet six months is not proof of a fault, but it means a bug that appeared after an iOS update may not have a fix waiting. The Map Tracker is more active, with version 1.45 on 1 November 2026 and notes about smoother route playback and fixed distance display.

TripScout publishes no status page that I could find, and tripscout.app/support returns the home page rather than a help page. So "down for everyone" cannot be confirmed from outside; the checks below are for your own phone.

Seven steps, in the order I would try them

  1. Confirm the phone. If you are on Android, stop here: neither iPhone app runs there, there is no Google Play listing that I found, and the TripScout FAQ says only that an Android app is "on the way". If you installed something from a Play search, it is a different app. Google's Fix an installed Android app that isn't working page covers any Android app that crashes or will not open.
  2. Check your iOS version. The AI planner's listing says it requires iOS 15.1 or later and the Map Tracker's says iOS 17.6 or later. Both say "Only for iPhone". If the App Store will not offer the app, or it closes at launch on an old iPhone, the version is the first suspect.
  3. Close and reopen the app. This is the first step on Apple's page If an app on your iPhone or iPad stops responding, closes unexpectedly, or won't open: force the app to close, then open it again.
  4. Restart the iPhone. Apple's second step. Restart, then open the app and see whether it behaves.
  5. Check for updates. Apple says to open the App Store, tap your profile icon, then tap App Updates. If the app has no update, update the iPhone's software.
  6. Delete the app and download it again, but read this first. Apple warns you might lose data stored in the app. For the AI planner this matters: its privacy policy says saved trips stay on your device and that deleting the app removes locally stored data. Note down your destinations and dates before you delete.
  7. Contact the developer. Apple's page says that if the app is still not working, contact the app developer. I found no support address for the AI planner on tripscout.app; the policy gives privacy@tripscout.app for privacy questions only, and the terms give legal@tripscout.app for legal ones. Use the developer link on its App Store page and say your iOS version and app version.

If the itinerary will not build or the map looks empty

These are my readings of the AI planner's own policy and terms, not tested behaviour.

  • Connection. The privacy policy says your destination, dates and preferences are sent over an encrypted connection to a third-party AI provider to produce the plan. My inference is that building a new itinerary needs signal or Wi-Fi.
  • Location permission. The policy calls location optional and says it is used to show your position on the map and give guidance while you use the app. If you declined it, a missing position is expected, so check the app's location setting in iOS Settings.
  • No account. The policy says the app requires no sign-up and collects no name or email, so there should be no login to fail.
  • Wrong or closed places. The terms say AI-generated itineraries are suggestions that may not reflect current conditions, opening hours, prices or availability, and that you must verify them. A closed museum is, by those terms, not a malfunction.

Questions about TripScout not working

Is the TripScout app down?

I found no outage on 13 November 2026. The TripScout AI planner's website returned a normal page, its App Store listing loaded, and Apple's lookup record still lists version 1.2.0. TripScout publishes no status page that I could find, so I cannot rule out a problem on your phone or network.

Is there a TripScout app for Android?

I found none. A Google Play search for "tripscout" returned no app with that name, and the TripScout website says in its FAQ that an Android app is on the way and that for now the app is on iPhone, with no date given. Both TripScout apps on the App Store are marked as iPhone only.

Why will my TripScout itinerary not load?

I cannot see your error, but its privacy policy says your destination, dates and preferences are sent to a third-party AI provider to build the plan, so my reading is that generating an itinerary needs a working internet connection. The policy does not describe any error messages, so I cannot say what a failure looks like.

Do I need an account to use TripScout?

For the AI planner, apparently not. The privacy policy says the app requires no sign-up and collects no name, email or account information. That means a login or password problem is unlikely to be the cause on that app. The Map Tracker app has in-app purchases, and I did not read how its purchases work.

How do I contact TripScout support?

I found no support page or support email for the AI planner on tripscout.app. The policy lists privacy@tripscout.app for privacy questions and the terms list legal@tripscout.app for legal ones, and Apple's guidance is to contact the developer of an App Store app. The planner's developer link on its App Store page is the place to start.

Is TripScout still being updated?

The AI planner: Apple's record shows version 1.2.0 released on 15 May 2026 and I found no newer one, so about six months without an update as of 13 November 2026. The Map Tracker app is more recent: version 1.45, released 1 November 2026.

What can I use on Android instead?
