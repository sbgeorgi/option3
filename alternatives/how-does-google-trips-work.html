How Does Google Trips Work? It Doesn’t Anymore: What It Did and What Replaced It

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and it appears in one list near the end, so read that entry with that in mind. I did not test the old app; every Google fact below comes from a page I read on 21 November 2026, listed under Sources.

Google Trips no longer works: Google ended support for the app on 5 August 2019. While it ran, it found your flight, hotel, car and restaurant reservations from Gmail and put them with day guides and things to do for your destination. Today those jobs are split between Google Maps lists, google.com/travel and other apps, and the table below shows which feature went where.

If you want a ready list of other apps rather than the explainer, see Google Trips alternatives, which compares six of them. This page covers how Google Trips worked and where its features live now.

How Google Trips worked

  • Launch and sign-in. Wikipedia says the mobile app launched on 19 September 2016 for Android and iOS, and that you needed a Google account to use it.
  • Reservations from Gmail. Per Wikipedia, the app helped you find flight, hotel, car and restaurant reservations for a trip based on emails in your Gmail. Google’s May 2019 post describes the same idea for the trips timeline: reservations such as hotels and flights were added when you were signed in and had a confirmation in Gmail.
  • Day guides. Wikipedia says the app launched with complete day guides to more than 200 major cities, and describes the Google Travel service as summarising a destination in categories such as day plans, reservations and things to do.
  • Offline. 9to5Google, in 2023, described the 2016 to 2019 app as a utility that grouped travel details and stored everything offline.
  • Research tracking (web, 2019). Google’s May 2019 post says recent searches, saved places and flights you were tracking were added automatically to your trips when you were signed in to your Google Account, and waiting at google.com/travel.

What happened to it: dated timeline

  • 14 May 2019. Google said that as it continued to evolve Google Trips, trip information would be “more accessible at google.com/travel, and in Google Search and Google Maps”, and that trips, including hotel and restaurant reservations, would come to Google Maps in the next few months.
  • 5 August 2019. 9to5Google reported, citing the help page, that support for Trips would end on 5 August 2019. The sunset message read “Many of your favorite features will live on in Google Search and Google Maps”. On 6 August it updated the story: the screen now said “We’ve said goodbye to Google Trips” and the rest of the app was unavailable.
  • 8 August 2019. Google said Maps would show flight and hotel reservations in one place under Your Places, then Reservations, and that you could open them offline.
  • 1 May 2023. A notice on google.com/travel, quoted by 9to5Google, said trip summaries would no longer be available beginning 1 May, “though your travel reservations may still appear in other Google services”.

How to rebuild the Trips workflow in Google Maps today

These steps come from Google’s Maps help pages (lists, You tab and offline maps) as I read them on 21 November 2026. I did not run them in the app. The lists page has separate Computer, Android and iPhone tabs, and the text I could read was the Computer tab, so menus on a phone may differ.

  1. Open Google Maps and sign in to your Google Account.
  2. Click Saved, then New list. If you do not find Saved, the help page says it may be behind the Menu. Enter a name, and optionally an icon and a description.
  3. Search for a place or click it on the map, click Save and choose your list. Repeat for each place on the trip.
  4. To share, click Menu, then Saved, then More next to the list. Choose Send a link to view, or open Sharing options and pick Private (only you) or Shared (anyone with your link). The page says you cannot share starred places.
  5. To let others add places, choose More, then Invite to edit, then Get a link, and send the copied link. To stop it, open Sharing options and turn off “Let others edit your list”.
  6. On a phone, the You tab lists your lists, recently saved places and nearby saved places, per the You tab help page.
  7. For offline use on Android, per the offline maps page: search for a place such as a city, swipe right on the place sheet, tap More, then Download offline map, then Download. Or tap your profile picture, then Offline maps, then Select your own map.

One part has no step: I could not confirm today where Gmail reservations appear in Maps, so check your own app. The offline page also warns that some countries or regions cannot download offline maps.

Short answer

  • Google Trips no longer exists: support ended on 5 August 2019.
  • It was a Google-account app (Android and iOS, from 19 September 2016) that found reservations from Gmail and offered day guides for more than 200 cities.
  • Google did not replace it with one app. It pointed users to google.com/travel, Google Search and Google Maps.
  • Saved places now live in Google Maps lists, which you can keep private, share by link or open to other editors.
  • Offline use now means downloading a map area in Google Maps; navigation works if the whole route is inside it.
  • Trip summaries on google.com/travel ended on 1 May 2023, and where Gmail reservations show up in Maps was not confirmed in my 21 November 2026 read.
  • For picking places by swiping on Android, there is NomadSwipe, which I built; it does not read email.

FAQ

How does Google Trips work?

It does not work anymore: Google ended support for the Google Trips app on August 5, 2019. While it ran, it was a Google-account app that found flight, hotel, car and restaurant reservations from your Gmail and showed them with day guides for more than 200 major cities. Google moved the idea into Google Search, Google Maps and google.com/travel.

Is Google Trips still available?

No. Wikipedia says the mobile app was shut down on August 5, 2019, and 9to5Google reported on August 6, 2019 that the in-app message had changed to “We’ve said goodbye to Google Trips”. The trip summaries that lived on at google.com/travel were set to end on May 1, 2023.

What replaced Google Trips?

No single app. Google’s May 2019 post said trip information would be more accessible at google.com/travel, in Google Search and in Google Maps. Today I could confirm Maps lists for saved places and offline map areas, and a google.com/travel page that opens on Flights, with tabs for Explore, Hotels and Vacation rentals.

Where do my Gmail reservations show up now?

I could not confirm it today. In August 2019 Google said Maps would show flight and hotel reservations under Your Places and then Reservations, and in 2023 9to5Google reported that reservations “may still appear in other Google services”. Google’s current help page for the You tab in Maps does not mention reservations, so check your own Maps app.
