Layla AI not working? What users report, what to try and when to switch

Layla AI not working? On 14 November 2026 I found no outage notice: layla.ai loaded, both store listings were live and recently updated, and I found no status page. Reviewers mostly report lost chats, freezes and ignored instructions. Try the store-standard fixes below, then email help@layla.ai.

Fixes for Android

Google's own page, Fix an installed Android app that isn't working, covers crashes, apps that will not open and apps that will not respond. In the order it gives them:

  1. Restart and update. Restart the phone, check for an Android update, then update the app from Google Play under Manage apps & devices.
  2. Force stop the app, then reopen it.
  3. Clear the app's cache and data from Settings. Google describes clearing cached data as a short-term fix.
  4. Sync the phone with Google servers by switching automatic date and time off, setting a wrong time, and switching it back on.
  5. Uninstall, then reinstall. Google warns that data saved in the app is erased. Layla's privacy policy mentions saving and exporting itineraries, but I could not confirm that an unsynced trip survives, so export or screenshot yours first.
  6. Contact the developer. Google says to do this if only one app misbehaves. For Layla that means help@layla.ai.

Fixes for iPhone

Apple's page, If an app on your iPhone or iPad stops responding, closes unexpectedly, or won't open, gives four steps: close and reopen the app, restart the device, check for updates (open the store, tap your profile icon, then App Updates), and delete and redownload the app, which can lose data stored in the app. If it still fails, Apple says to contact the developer, and that a recent purchase can be refunded by request. Layla's App Store page lists iOS 16.6 or later as the minimum.

Is it your phone or Layla?

  • Other apps fine, Layla not: by Google's own advice that points at the app, so go to the developer.
  • Chat answers wrong but the app runs: that is not a crash. Layla's terms say it makes no claims about the accuracy of the chatbot's information.
  • Cannot subscribe or Premium is gone: the terms say that as of August 2026 Premium is no longer offered to new subscribers, and that existing subscriptions will not renew and revert to the free service. The store listings I read still carry subscription wording, so what you see in the app may differ.
  • Trip missing between web and app: one reviewer's case above. Layla's privacy policy lists Google, Facebook, Apple and email as sign-in providers, so signing in with the same provider on both is the first thing I would check.
  • Surprised by what it sells: the home page and FAQ now describe plans that Layla's travel advisors book, with planning free and booked arrangements paid for. The FAQ gives a recommended minimum budget of 100 euros per person per night. A mismatch with that can feel like the app not working.

When to switch

These are my criteria, not Layla's. I would stop trying fixes and move on if:

  • you lose a long chat or itinerary a second time;
  • it keeps dropping hotels or flights you already booked, after you correct it;
  • you need numbers you can trust, such as costs and totals, and cannot check each one;
  • you want a plan that does not depend on an AI conversation, or that works without the chat at all.

Whether Layla fixes any of this is up to Layla. Its Android build was updated on 5 November 2026 and its iPhone build on 6 November, and the notes for the new version mention a new home screen, in-app booking, edits from the trip page and "smoother everywhere", but name no specific bug fix.

Questions about Layla not working

Is Layla AI down right now?

I found no outage on 14 November 2026. layla.ai loaded normally, the Google Play listing was updated on 5 November and the App Store listing on 6 November. I found no status page, though, so I cannot rule out a fault on your side or a short outage I did not catch.

Why does Layla ignore my instructions or leave out my hotels?

I do not know the cause. Several store reviewers describe exactly this, including a 3-star App Store review that says booked hotels were left out or swapped for others, and a Play reviewer who reports errors in cost totals. Layla's terms say it makes no claims about the accuracy of the chatbot's information, so check every hotel, price and opening time yourself.

How do I contact Layla support?

The address I found on the contact page, the terms and the privacy policy is help@layla.ai. Its reply to an App Store reviewer with a web-account problem asked for the email used on the web version and a short description of the issue. I found no help centre or chat widget page to link to.

Can I still subscribe to Layla Premium?

Probably not. The terms page I read says that as of August 2026 Layla Premium is no longer offered to new subscribers, and that existing subscriptions will not renew and revert to the free service. The store listings I read still carry subscription wording, so the store text and the terms do not fully match.

What can I use on Android instead of Layla?

It depends on the job. Wanderlog and Stippl both have Android apps with day-by-day planners, and Layla alternatives compares more. If you only want to choose places in a city and see them on a map, NomadSwipe (Android, which I built) is a narrower tool without a chat.
