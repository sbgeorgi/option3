4 Stippl Alternatives Compared in 2026

Updated 28 October 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only.

Stippl turns a prompt into a day-by-day itinerary, with budgets, journals and packing lists, and its Pro plan starts at 4.99 euros a month. Its App Store listing shows 4.58 stars from 486 ratings. NomadSwipe does a smaller job on Android: you swipe through real places in one city and save the ones you like onto a map.

People look past Stippl for three reasons: they want a simpler way to choose places before planning anything, they want a different price model, or they hit the bugs and usability complaints that show up in its App Store reviews. This page covers four alternatives whose public pages we read on this date, and says where Stippl or another app is the better pick.

NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in a city by swiping

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Tap Save or swipe right to keep a place, and tap Skip or swipe left to pass. Saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list can be sorted by rating, distance or A to Z.

  • Strength: a quick shortlist of places you chose yourself, before you have a plan.
  • Limit: it does not build a day-by-day itinerary or generate one with AI. You plan each day yourself around the map.
  • Limit: it is Android only.

The base plan includes 3 trips, plus 2 destination searches and 2 custom pins a month, with a limited number of swipes. Premium (Monthly or Lifetime, bought in the app) removes those limits. Up to 20 recent trips are available offline.

Stippl

Best for: an AI-built itinerary with budgets, journals and packing lists

Stippl's homepage describes a day-by-day plan with times, places and notes in one timeline, route planning that calculates distances and travel times, and an AI that turns a single prompt into a full itinerary. It also lists expense splitting with your travel crew, hotel search, documents, a journal and a packing list. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, published by Stippl B.V.

On price, Stippl's pricing text says the free plan includes unlimited trips, budgets, journals, packing lists and travel tracking. A single paid plan, Stippl Pro, starts from 4.99 euros a month and adds unlimited AI trip and day-plan generation, expense export with custom categories, and reusable packing checklists. We did not find an annual price.

Store ratings differ by platform. The App Store listing shows 4.58 from 486 ratings and the Google Play listing shows 3.7 with more than 100,000 downloads. Some recent App Store reviews report problems: one 2026 review describes double-booked expenses, freezing and trouble changing dates, and others call it hard to use or say it lags. Another review from 2026 calls it well worth the money. Reviews are individual experiences, so test the app on your own trip before paying.

  • Strength: a full planner with AI itineraries, budgets and journals, available on iPhone, Android and web, with a generous free plan.
  • Limit: more features mean more to learn, and some App Store reviewers report bugs and usability problems.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary with a map in one view

Wanderlog's homepage describes one app for all travel planning: your itinerary and your map in one view, with bookings in the same place. It says you can plan with friends in real time, with live syncing and collaborative editing. We did not confirm its price, because its pricing page returned a 404 this run, so check its site before you decide.

  • Strength: the itinerary and the map sit in one view, and collaborative editing is listed.
  • Limit: we did not confirm its price or which plan includes which feature.

TripIt

Best for: keeping flights, hotels and bookings in one timeline

TripIt's homepage describes forwarding your booking confirmations to plans@tripit.com to build an itinerary. Its pricing page offers a free tier, and TripIt Pro is 49 dollars a year, with a 30-day free trial of Pro offered on its Pro page.

  • Strength: it organizes bookings you already made, with no manual entry.
  • Limit: it organizes trips more than it helps you choose what to do in a city.

Polarsteps

Best for: recording a trip as a travel log

Polarsteps titles its homepage "Plan, Track & Relive Your Trips", and the page says it works offline. Its Plus page says you can still plan, track, share and relive trips for free. Its Plus plan costs 8.99 euros a month or 29.99 euros a year, and its Plus page says there is no free trial. Polarsteps records the trip. NomadSwipe helps you choose the places for it.

  • Strength: a good fit when you want to remember and share where you went.
  • Limit: it is a log of the trip, so it is a weaker fit for picking places in advance.

Which to choose

  • You want an AI itinerary with budgets and a journal, on iPhone or Android: Stippl.
  • You want to shortlist real places in one city on Android: NomadSwipe.
  • You want an itinerary and a map in one view, with collaborative editing: Wanderlog, after you check its price.
  • You want your bookings in one timeline: TripIt.
  • You want a log of where you went: Polarsteps.

FAQ

Is Stippl free?

Stippl's own pricing text says its free plan includes unlimited trips, budgets, journals, packing lists and travel tracking. Its Pro plan is described as starting from 4.99 euros a month and adds unlimited AI trip and day-plan generation, expense export and more.

Is Stippl on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Stippl says it is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad), Android and as a web app. NomadSwipe is Android only for now.

Does NomadSwipe build an AI itinerary like Stippl?

No. NomadSwipe gives you a shortlist of real places and a map for each trip, and you plan each day yourself around that map.

Does Stippl have an annual price?

We did not find one. Stippl's pricing text lists Pro as starting from 4.99 euros a month, so check its pricing page for current options.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Up to 20 recent trips are available offline, according to the Google Play listing and the NomadSwipe site.

How much does NomadSwipe Premium cost?

Premium (Monthly or Lifetime) is bought in the app, and the price is shown in Google Play before you pay. This article does not list NomadSwipe prices.
