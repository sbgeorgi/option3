Mobile, Alabama in 2 days: 15 stops matched to opening days

A Mobile, Alabama 2-day plan of 15 stops from the NomadSwipe app's place data, each start time checked against stored opening hours. Day one is a compact downtown museum day, day two a battleship, house-museum and gardens day. Both days work on Friday and Saturday.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's place data for the city, read on 7 November 2026. The rows I used were last updated on 5 and 6 May 2026, six months earlier, so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, downtown: Colonial Fort Condé, the History Museum of Mobile, the Condé-Charlotte Museum, lunch at The Noble South, the Mobile Carnival Museum, the National Maritime Museum of the Gulf, the Exploreum and dinner at Le Moyne's Chophouse.
  • Day 2, the battleship, two house museums and the west side: the USS ALABAMA Battleship Memorial Park, brunch at Ruby Slipper, the Richards-DAR House Museum, the Oakleigh House Museum, the Mobile Botanical Gardens, the Mobile Museum of Art and dinner at Rock N Roll Sushi.
  • Days of the week: the plan assumes day one is a Friday and day two a Saturday. By the stored hours those are the only two weekdays on which each day works (see the table below), in either order. Sunday and Monday fail both days.
  • Distance: day one is compact, about 2.45 km in total by straight line between stored coordinates. Day two adds up to about 19.3 km, with one 9.99 km leg. These are not driving times.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 7 November 2026 with read-only queries. The city is stored as two destination rows (named Mobile, country USA) with 454 and 10 cards. I excluded destination rows with the country "Custom", which hold user-typed names. Together the two rows hold 464 cards, 454 distinct place IDs and 451 distinct places by case-insensitive title and address. The data is not limited to the city, so I defined "in the city" as a card whose stored address contains "Mobile, AL" and whose stored coordinates fall inside a rough box (latitude 30.55 to 30.85, longitude -88.3 to -87.95). That keeps 267 of the 464 cards (257 place IDs) and drops 197. The box alone holds 294 cards, because 27 cards inside it lack a "Mobile, AL" address, and I excluded those too. After merging cards with the same title and address, I kept the card with stored hours, then the one with more reviews, which leaves 254 places. The counts below are of the 254 places unless stated. An empty stored hours list counts as no stored hours. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates. The 254 places were last updated on 5 and 6 May 2026, and the deck's other rows between 5 May and 30 July 2026.
