What Rome2Rio does, according to its own pages

Rome2Rio is a search engine for journeys. Enter any two places, from a country to an address or an airport code, and its help center says each result shows the transport type, total duration and a price estimate, with a schedule page for the option you pick. Its llms.txt calls it a search and discovery product with no checkout flow: you are sent to an operator or booking site to buy. Wikipedia describes it as an Australian multimodal journey planner, founded in 2010 and acquired by Omio in October 2019. For the app itself, see how to use Rome2Rio app in five steps.

I did not install or test Rome2Rio. What I could check today is that its own pages do not agree with each other, which matters if you are comparing the numbers.

1. Omio

Best for: comparing and buying a train, bus, flight or ferry in one app

Omio is the closest rival in spirit and, per Wikipedia, Rome2Rio's owner. Where Rome2Rio shows options and hands you off, Omio sells the ticket: its listings say you can compare and book, pay in the app and keep mobile tickets on your phone, with live updates such as platform changes and delays where available. The Play listing says 45 countries and more than 2,000 providers; the U.S. App Store record says more than 3,000 companies across 45+ countries, including Japan's Shinkansen, parts of Southeast Asia and Brazil. Its Play listing shows 10M+ downloads.

  • Strength: one place to search and pay, with tickets stored in the app.
  • Weakness: it is a booking app, so it only shows what it sells. Its listings name 45 countries, against Rome2Rio's 160+ (llms.txt).

2. Trainline

Best for: train and bus tickets in the UK and Europe

Trainline's Play listing says you can compare train and bus options from 260 companies, buy digital tickets, get real-time notifications and live timetable tracking, see how busy a train is with Crowd Alerts, and add loyalty and discount cards. It also says you can save plans for later and lock in tickets for up to 7 days, and lists a Cancel for Any Reason add-on. The listing shows 10M+ downloads.

  • Strength: deeper on rail than a world search, with the live tracking and discounts a booking app needs.
  • Weakness: Europe and the UK only, per the listing's partner list, and rail and bus only. No flights or ferries are described.

3. Citymapper

Best for: getting around one city by public transport

Citymapper is the answer to a different question from Rome2Rio: not “how do I reach this city” but “how do I get across it”. Its Play listing describes real-time routes across bus, subway, train, ferry, cabs, ride share, bike share, e-scooters and walking, with step-by-step directions, live arrivals and disruption alerts. You can save Home, Work and favorite stops and lines, and open some official transit maps offline. It lists named U.S. cities and says “many more worldwide”; the list is in the app. The listing shows 10M+ downloads. For a walkthrough, see how to use Citymapper. For local transit, apps like Citymapper are compared too.

  • Strength: live, minute-level transit in the cities it covers.
  • Weakness: its listing describes no intercity routes or train tickets. Coverage is city by city.

4. Google Maps

Best for: one free app for directions, places and saved lists

Google Maps' Play listing describes live-traffic navigation for driving, walking, cycling and public transport, over 250 million businesses and places with photos and reviews, custom lists of favorite saved places that you can share, offline maps, and reservations and hotel booking. It is the one app here that covers both getting there and what is there. The listing shows 10B+ downloads. Saved-list limits are covered in Google Maps list limits.

  • Strength: nothing else here pairs routes with a place database this large.
  • Weakness: it does not compare a train, a bus and a flight side by side the way Rome2Rio does, and its listing mentions no ticket sales.

5. NomadSwipe: the other half of the trip

Best for: deciding what to do in a city once you know how you are getting there

I built NomadSwipe, so weigh this entry with that in mind. NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. You pick a city and it deals real places one card at a time. Swipe right or tap Save to keep a place, swipe left or tap Skip to pass. Everything you save lands in your trip and is pinned on a map.

Where it is not a Rome2Rio alternative

  • It does not search trains, buses, ferries or flights, show fares or plan a route between cities. Use Rome2Rio, Omio or Trainline for that.
  • Its listing is for Android, and it has 50+ downloads on Google Play, so it has no track record next to any app above.
  • The plan without Premium is limited: 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip a month, 2 custom pins a month and a daily limit on saves.

Free to download. Premium is optional, comes as Monthly or Lifetime, and removes those limits. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online, per the Play listing. For how the swipe approach compares with other apps, see swipe travel apps for places in a city.

FAQ

What are the best apps similar to Rome2Rio?

It depends on the job. For booking trains, buses, flights and ferries in one app, Omio. For booking trains and buses in the UK and Europe, Trainline. For live public transport inside a city, Citymapper. For driving, walking and transit directions plus saved places, Google Maps. None of them copies Rome2Rio's global door-to-door search across every mode, so many people keep Rome2Rio for the route and add one of these for the rest.

Is Rome2Rio free?

The app is free to download: its U.S. App Store listing shows a price of 0.00 and its Play listing shows no price. Its Play listing says route searches are unlimited and free. Its home page lists Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Omio, Kayak and Mozio as partners, without saying what each one provides. Check the store listing for in-app purchases in your country.

Can you book tickets on Rome2Rio?

Its own llms.txt, read on 11 November 2026, says there is no checkout flow on Rome2Rio and that users are sent to operator or booking sites to buy. Wikipedia says it added direct booking in 2016, so older write-ups may describe a different product. Omio and Trainline are the two apps here that sell tickets inside the app.

Does Rome2Rio save trips or places?

Partly. Its help center says you can create an account, save a trip and build an itinerary between multiple locations, and that this is currently only on desktop and mobile web browsers, not yet on its phone apps. Its store listings still say "Save and share routes and itineraries". I did not test the app, so check before you rely on it. It does not describe saving restaurants, sights or other places to visit.

Who owns Rome2Rio?

Wikipedia says Omio, a Berlin-based booking platform, acquired Rome2Rio in October 2019, and Rome2Rio's home page still lists Omio among its partners. Rome2Rio's own pages name its company as Rome2Rio Pty Ltd.

Is there an app like Rome2Rio for deciding what to do in a city?

I built NomadSwipe, so weigh this with that in mind. NomadSwipe does not plan routes between cities. You pick a city and swipe through real places, save the ones you like and see them on a map. It is on Google Play for Android phones.
