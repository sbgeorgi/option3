3 Mapstr Alternatives Compared in 2026

Updated 29 October 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only.

Saves from friends and social posts pile up as half-read lists. Mapstr puts them on one map. NomadSwipe takes the other route: it deals you real places in a city one card at a time, and the yes answers go on a map. This page compares three options and says where Mapstr is the better pick.

People look past Mapstr when they have a list of places but no way to choose between them, or when they want suggestions rather than their own saves. NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in a city by swiping, not by saving them one at a time

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Each card shows the category, rating, distance and opening status. The places you save are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z.

  • Strength: you do not have to find the places first. The deck suggests them, and you keep the ones you choose.
  • Limit: it does not import saves from Instagram, TikTok or other apps. Its listed features are swipe, save, map and go.
  • Limit: Android only today; iPhone coming soon.

The free plan includes 3 trips, 2 destination searches a month, 2 custom pins a month and a limited number of swipes. Premium (Monthly or Lifetime, bought in the app) includes up to 20 saved trips available offline.

Mapstr

Best for: putting the places you already know about on one map

Mapstr's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hulab.mapstr) says you can save favourite places onto a map, sort them by tags, bookmark the stops of a trip, follow friends' maps and open your map offline. The listing shows 4.0 from 4.41K reviews, 500K+ downloads, In-app purchases and an update on 20 October 2026. Its home page (https://www.mapstr.com/) lists importing addresses from Instagram and TikTok, seeing friends' addresses and creating a collaborative map.

Its trip page (https://www.mapstr.com/fonctionnalites/preparer-un-voyage) says Mapstr helps you organise trips from the choice of destination onward. The home page also lists personalised suggestions among its features.

  • Strength: one map for saves from friends, social posts and your own searches, with imports from Instagram and TikTok.
  • Limit: its listing describes storing places you already know. Suggestions appear on its site as one feature among several.
  • Limit: we did not confirm what any paid option costs. The listing only shows In-app purchases.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary and a map for places and reservations

Wanderlog's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wanderlog.android) shows a 4.7 average rating from 37.6K reviews, 1M+ downloads, an Editors' Choice badge, in-app purchases and an update on 23 October 2026. It says each place you add is pinned on a Google Maps-based travel map straight away, and that offline access to trip plans is a Pro feature. We did not confirm Pro prices from its pages this run, so this article does not state them.

  • Strength: places, map and route in one itinerary, with each place pinned as you add it.
  • Limit: offline access to trip plans is a Pro feature, and its listing does not show Pro prices.

Google Maps

Best for: custom lists of saved places in the map app

Google Maps' Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.maps) shows 10B+ downloads and an update on 21 October 2026. It says you can create custom lists of your favourite saved places and share them with others. The listing shows an Install button and the label "Contains ads". We did not confirm any paid tier, so this article does not state a price.

  • Strength: saved places in custom lists, shareable from the same app you use for maps.
  • Limit: its listing describes saving and sharing lists, not choosing places from a deck of suggestions.

Which to choose

  • You already have a list of places from friends and social posts and want them on one map: Mapstr.
  • You want to choose places in one city by swiping, on Android: NomadSwipe.
  • You want an itinerary with reservations and a map of your stops: Wanderlog.
  • You want custom lists of saved places in the Google Maps app: Google Maps.

FAQ

Is Mapstr free?

Its Google Play listing shows In-app purchases. We did not confirm the price of any paid option, so check the listing before you pay.

Does Mapstr suggest places?

Its home page lists personalised suggestions as a feature, and its listing describes friends' recommendations. Its main job, as its listing describes it, is saving places you already know.

Can Mapstr import places from Instagram or TikTok?

Its home page lists importing addresses from Instagram and TikTok.

Does NomadSwipe import saves from other apps?

No. NomadSwipe's listed features are swipe, save, map and go. They do not include importing saves from Instagram, TikTok or other apps.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Premium includes up to 20 saved trips available offline.

Does Google Maps let you save places in lists?

Its Google Play listing describes custom lists of favourite saved places that you can share with others.
