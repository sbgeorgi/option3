3 Mapstr Alternatives Compared in 2026

Updated 4 November 2026 (facts checked on 2026-11-04). Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only.

Mapstr saves places you already know onto one map, on Android, iPhone and the web, with 500K+ Google Play downloads. The alternatives here are NomadSwipe, Wanderlog and Google Maps. NomadSwipe, which I built, is Android only and suits you if you would rather choose places by swiping than save them by hand.

People look past Mapstr when they have a list of places but no way to choose between them, or when they want suggestions rather than their own saves. This page is based on the Mapstr, Wanderlog and Google Maps Google Play listings, Mapstr's iPhone store listing and Mapstr's French home page, all read on 4 November 2026. Quotes from Mapstr's site are our translation. If you mostly use Google Maps lists, five Google Maps lists alternatives compared puts Mapstr next to them.

Checked on 2026-11-04. Mapstr is available on Android: its Google Play listing is live with an Install button, and its home page links to Android and iPhone downloads. Nothing we read says its Android app is coming soon. Since our 29 October read, Mapstr's Play listing moved from 4.41K to 4.42K reviews and was updated on 31 October, Wanderlog's moved from 37.6K to 37.7K reviews and was updated on 2 November, and Google Maps was updated on 3 November. NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in a city by swiping, not by saving them one at a time

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Each card shows the category, rating, distance and opening status. The places you save are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z.

  • Strength: you do not have to find the places first. The deck suggests them, and you keep the ones you choose.
  • Limit: it does not import saves from Instagram, TikTok or other apps. Its listed features are swipe, save, map and go.
  • Limit: Android only today; iPhone coming soon.

The free plan includes 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip a month, 2 custom pins a month and a daily limit on saves. Premium comes as a Monthly or Lifetime plan and removes those limits. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online. For the Google Maps side of offline planning, see Google Maps offline for a trip: what works and what does not.

Mapstr

Best for: putting the places you already know about on one map

Mapstr's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hulab.mapstr) says you can save favourite places onto a map, sort them by tags, bookmark the stops of a trip, follow friends' maps and open your map offline. The listing shows 4.0 from 4.42K reviews, 500K+ downloads, In-app purchases and an update on 31 October 2026. It is live with an Install button, so Mapstr is available on Android. Its home page (https://www.mapstr.com/) links Android and iPhone downloads and a web version, lists importing addresses from Instagram and TikTok, seeing friends' addresses and creating a collaborative map, and counts 105,456,789 places saved by its users and a community of 4 million+ users (Mapstr's own figures, shown on 4 November 2026).

Its iPhone store listing (https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id917288465) is free with In-App Purchases, shows 4.7 from 1.1K ratings and lists importing Google Maps, CSV or KML lists. It describes mapstr plus, with monthly and yearly subscriptions and a 7-day free trial, as adding unlimited places, AI search suggestions, proximity alerts, a table-booking filter and a desktop web version. We did not read a price for mapstr plus from the store, so this article does not state one. Mapstr's own help pages disagree with each other on whether the older Premium plan still exists, so check the plan in the app.

Its trip page (https://www.mapstr.com/fonctionnalites/preparer-un-voyage) says Mapstr helps you organise trips from the choice of destination onward, including notes, photos and proximity alerts on each place. The home page also lists personalised suggestions among its features.

  • Strength: one map for saves from friends, social posts and your own searches, with imports from Instagram and TikTok.
  • Limit: its listing describes storing places you already know. Suggestions appear on its site as one feature among several.
  • Limit: some features, such as unlimited places and proximity alerts, are listed under the paid mapstr plus plan in the iPhone store listing. We did not confirm what it costs.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary and a map for places and reservations

Wanderlog's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wanderlog.android) shows a 4.7 average rating from 37.7K reviews, 1M+ downloads, an Editors' Choice badge, in-app purchases and an update on 2 November 2026. It calls the app free to use, says each place you add is pinned on a Google Maps-based travel map straight away, and that offline access to trip plans is a Pro feature. We did not confirm Pro prices from its pages this run, so this article does not state them. For more detail see our page on apps like Wanderlog.

  • Strength: places, map and route in one itinerary, with each place pinned as you add it.
  • Limit: offline access to trip plans is a Pro feature, and its listing does not show Pro prices.

Google Maps

Best for: custom lists of saved places in the map app

Google Maps' Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.maps) shows 10B+ downloads and an update on 3 November 2026. It says you can create custom lists of your favourite saved places and share them with others, and it also lists offline maps for areas with bad signal. The listing shows an Install button and the label "Contains ads". We did not confirm any paid tier, so this article does not state a price.

  • Strength: saved places in custom lists, shareable from the same app you use for maps.
  • Limit: its listing describes saving and sharing lists, not choosing places from a deck of suggestions.

Which to choose

  • You already have a list of places from friends and social posts and want them on one map: Mapstr.
  • You want to choose places in one city by swiping, on Android: NomadSwipe (see swipe travel apps compared for more swipe-style apps).
  • You want an itinerary with reservations and a map of your stops: Wanderlog (see best trip planner apps for nine planners side by side).
  • You want custom lists of saved places in the Google Maps app: Google Maps.

FAQ

Is Mapstr free?

It is free to install, and its store listings show In-app purchases. Its iPhone store listing lists mapstr plus monthly and yearly subscriptions with a 7-day free trial. We did not confirm the price of any paid option, so check the listing before you pay.

Is Mapstr available on Android?

Yes. It has a live Google Play listing (500K+ downloads, updated 31 October 2026), and its home page links Android and iPhone downloads. We checked this on 2026-11-04.

Does Mapstr suggest places?

Its home page lists personalised suggestions as a feature, and its listing describes friends' recommendations. Its main job, as its listing describes it, is saving places you already know.

Can Mapstr import places from Instagram, TikTok or Google Maps?

Its home page lists importing addresses from Instagram and TikTok, and its iPhone store listing lists importing Google Maps, CSV or KML lists.

Does NomadSwipe import saves from other apps?

No. NomadSwipe's listed features are swipe, save, map and go. They do not include importing saves from Instagram, TikTok or other apps.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online.

Does Google Maps let you save places in lists?

Its Google Play listing describes custom lists of favourite saved places that you can share with others.
