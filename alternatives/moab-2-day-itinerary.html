Moab in 2 days: 17 stops, and the 7 park stops with no stored hours

A Moab 2-day plan of 17 stops from the NomadSwipe app's deck: day one covers Arches and Main Street, day two the Island in the Sky district of Canyonlands and an evening in town. Ten stops have stored hours and pass all seven weekdays. The seven park stops have none.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Moab place data, read on 7 November 2026. Rows were last updated on 19 April and 12 May 2026 (the deck is stored as two destination rows), so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Arches and Main Street: breakfast at Sweet Cravings Bakery + Bistro, the Moab Information Center, the Arches National Park card, the Upper Delicate Arch Viewpoint, Skyline Arch and Broken Arch, then lunch at Quesadilla Mobilla, two Main Street galleries and dinner at Desert Bistro.
  • Day 2, Island in the Sky and the evening in town: breakfast at Bonjour Bakery & Café, the Mesa Arch Trailhead, the Green River Overlook and the White Rim Trail card, lunch at Sultan Mediterranean Grill, Rotary Park and dinner at the Trailhead Public House and Eatery.
  • Hours: the 10 stops with stored hours work on every weekday. The 7 stops in the national park areas (all 4 on day one's Arches leg and all 3 on day two's Canyonlands leg) have no stored hours, so nothing here tells you when they open.
  • Distance: this is a drive-heavy plan. By straight line between stored coordinates, day one adds up to about 57.3 km and day two to about 81.2 km, with legs of 22.05 km and 38.92 km back to town. These are not driving times.

A Moab deck where the headline sights have no hours

The Moab deck holds 371 cards, but only 182 sit inside a rough box around the town centre. By stored coordinates 35 are in the Arches area, 12 in the Island in the Sky district of Canyonlands, 45 elsewhere in the Moab region and 97 far away. The deck has 106 sight cards in 9 stored categories, and 91 of them are in the Moab region, in 7 categories. Only 14 of the 106 sights are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews. Two of those 14 are in Salt Lake City and in California, and of the other 12, only 4 have stored hours: Skydive Moab, Moab Jett, the Moab Information Center and Moab Giants. The other 8 are Arches National Park, the Mesa Arch Trailhead, the Green River Overlook, Skyline Arch, the Upper Delicate Arch Viewpoint, the White Rim Trail, Broken Arch and the Moab Backyard Theater, whose hours list is empty.

  • The park stops are pins, not schedules. I include them because they are the most reviewed sights in the deck: the Arches National Park card has 12,244 stored reviews, more than 13 times the next sight in the Moab region. I cannot match them to a weekday, so I give no opening times for them.
  • Entry fees. The National Park Service pages for Arches and Canyonlands list a standard private-vehicle entrance pass at $30 and an annual pass at $55 that admits one vehicle to both parks. The Canyonlands page says timed entry reservations are not required there; the Arches page does not say either way. Both pages read “Last updated: April 18, 2025” when I read them on 7 November 2026, so check the park's page before you go. I read no page on whether one standard pass covers both parks.
  • Add your own pins. Anything you want that the deck lacks is yours to add on the map yourself.

They include Utah's Hogle Zoo in Salt Lake City (4.1 from 1,159 reviews) and The Vintage Cocktail Lounge in Stanton, California (4.5 from 149 reviews), both stored as sights that meet my review line.
  • Skydive Moab, Moab Giants and Hole 'N The Rock. All three have stored hours and reviews: Skydive Moab is rated 5.0 from 883 reviews, Moab Giants 4.1 from 459 and Hole 'N The Rock 3.8 from 637. By straight line from the Moab Information Center they are 26.8, 16.1 and 21.9 km away, so each needs its own detour. I know them only by their stored values.

Questions about this Moab plan

Can I follow this Moab plan on any day of the week?

For the stops that have stored hours, yes. I tested the 10 stops that have stored hours against every weekday, and each is stored as open at its suggested start time with at least one hour left before closing. The tightest spots are Sweet Cravings Bakery + Bistro, stored as closing at 3 PM, and the Moab Information Center, stored as closing at 5 PM. The 7 Arches and Canyonlands stops have no stored hours at all, so I could not test them. Sunday needs no swap: Desert Bistro is stored as opening at 2 PM and the Trailhead Public House at 4 PM, both before their dinner slots.

Do I need a car for this Moab plan?

I read no transit, shuttle or parking source, so I cannot say what is available. The plan is spread out. By straight line between stored coordinates, day one has a 17.91 km leg from the Moab Information Center to the Arches National Park card and a 22.05 km leg back to town, and day two has a 34.35 km leg to the Mesa Arch Trailhead and a 38.92 km leg back. Roads make real legs longer, so treat these as drives and check your own options.

Are Arches and Canyonlands inside the Moab deck?

Partly. Of the deck's 371 cards, 182 sit inside my box around Moab's town centre, 35 inside a box around Arches and 12 around the Island in the Sky district of Canyonlands, by stored coordinates. 45 more are elsewhere in the Moab region and 97 are far away, from Colorado to California and New York. Sights in the two park areas have almost no stored hours: 1 of 32 in the Arches box and 0 of 12 in the Island in the Sky box.

What does it cost to enter Arches and Canyonlands?

I read the National Park Service fees pages for both parks on 7 November 2026. Each lists a standard private-vehicle entrance pass at $30 and an annual pass at $55 that admits one vehicle to Arches and Canyonlands. The Canyonlands page says timed entry reservations are not required to enter that park. Both pages are marked as last updated on 18 April 2025, and the Arches page says nothing about timed entry either way, so check the park's own page before you go.

Why are Skydive Moab, Moab Giants and Hole 'N The Rock not in the plan?

Each has stored hours and a review count, but they sit away from the two routes. By straight line from the Moab Information Center, Skydive Moab is 26.8 km away, Moab Giants 16.1 km and Hole 'N The Rock 21.9 km. Moab Giants is stored as closed on Wednesday, and Hole 'N The Rock, rated 3.8, as closed on Tuesday and Wednesday. They are swaps if you want one of them in place of a park stop.

How were the 17 stops chosen?

I picked cards by hand. For sights I used the Moab-region cards rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews where they fit a route: 8 of the 11 sight stops meet that line. Rotary Park (50 reviews), Lema's Kokopelli Gallery (39) and Moab Made (35) do not, and I kept them because they have stored hours and sit in town. The 6 meal stops are in-town restaurant and bakery cards rated 4.5 or higher with at least 189 stored reviews, each stored as open on every weekday at its suggested time.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 7 November 2026 with read-only queries. They have 300 distinct place IDs and 295 distinct titles (case-insensitive), because some venues, such as Desert Bistro, appear twice with different place IDs and review counts. I excluded destination rows with the country "Custom", which hold user-typed names. The deck is not limited to the town, so I sorted every card by stored coordinates into boxes I defined myself: town (latitude 38.54 to 38.60, longitude -109.58 to -109.50), Arches area (38.60 to 38.85, -109.66 to -109.45), Island in the Sky (38.30 to 38.50, -109.95 to -109.78), the rest of the Moab region (38.1 to 39.0, -110.2 to -109.0) and everything else. The boxes are rough, not official boundaries, and the Arches box also catches points along the highway north of town. "Sight" means a stored category of tourist attraction, park, art gallery, theater and concert hall, historic site, museum, garden, zoo or observation point; the deck has 9 of these. An empty stored hours list counts as no stored hours. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates. Where a venue has two cards I used the newer one.
