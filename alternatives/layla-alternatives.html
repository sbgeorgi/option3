6 Best Layla Alternatives in 2026

Updated 24 October 2026. Written by NomadSwipe, which is one of the apps compared, so read our own entry with that in mind.

Layla plans a trip through a chat with an AI, then offers a human travel advisor to refine the plan and a team to book it. That suits people who want the whole trip handled. If you want something lighter, such as a simple map of real places to save and no booking step, here are six apps worth comparing.

Layla at a glance

  • Planning is free. Layla's FAQ says there is no subscription or planning fee. You pay for the travel arrangements in your booking.
  • Minimum budget. Layla recommends at least €100 per person per night for its tailor-made trips.
  • Premium price is reported inconsistently. Layla's own blog says about $10 a month. Pilot, a competitor, reports $49 a year. Check the app.
  • Ownership. Travolution reported on 3 August 2026 that Layla is now part of Expedia Group. Layla is based in Berlin.
  • Reviews. Trustpilot shows 4.2 out of 5 from 94 reviews, and reviewers praise the easy planning and personalised itineraries. Some reviews describe the AI forgetting earlier instructions, or routing suggestions that do not fit the trip, such as airport stops on a train journey.

None of this makes Layla a bad choice. It is a different kind of tool: a planning service with advisors and bookings. The options below are lighter, and some of them are better for a specific job.

1. NomadSwipe: for choosing places, not building a booked trip

Best for: travellers who want a shortlist of real places they chose themselves

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. You pick a city and it deals real places one card at a time. Swipe right or tap Save to keep a place, swipe left or tap Skip to pass. Everything you save lands in your trip and is pinned on a map.

Where it beats Layla

  • There is no AI chat-to-itinerary step and no advisor to wait for. You decide each place yourself, one card at a time.
  • Up to 20 saved trips are available offline, according to the Google Play listing.
  • Each place shows its photos, directions, website and phone number, and you can sort saved places by rating, distance or A to Z.

Where it does not

  • It does not write an itinerary from a conversation, and the Play listing describes no hotel or flight booking.
  • It has no advisor and no customer care line on the pages we read.
  • It is Android only. The free plan is limited to 3 trips, 2 destination searches and 2 custom pins a month, plus a limited number of swipes.

Pricing

Free to download, with no card needed. Premium is optional, comes as Monthly or Lifetime, and removes the limits above. Google Play shows the current price before you pay.

The two apps solve different problems. NomadSwipe gives you a quick, self-chosen shortlist of places and a map, with no booking funnel. You give up the advisor, the booked itinerary and the bookings Layla handles. If you want someone to plan and book the whole trip, Layla is the better fit.

2. Wanderlog

Best for: itineraries with booking import

Wanderlog is a trip planner for day-by-day itineraries, bookings and sharing plans with travel companions. You can connect or forward confirmation emails, and flight and hotel reservations are added to the trip. It also suggests attractions and nearby places based on your itinerary, and the map and itinerary share one view.

  • Strength: email import and place recommendations in one planner.
  • Weakness: the page we read gives no price for either tier, and offline access is a Pro feature.

Pricing: free to start, with a paid Pro tier. The current price is not stated on the page read, so check the app. Read our Wanderlog alternatives comparison for more.

3. Mindtrip

Best for: an AI assistant that books and watches prices

Mindtrip calls itself a personal travel assistant. It learns how you like to travel, compares flights, stays and restaurants, and lets you book directly or have the assistant handle the details. It also tracks price drops, flight changes and on-trip conditions such as rain forecasts.

  • Strength: the assistant watches for price drops and flight changes.
  • Weakness: its booking and price-watching focus means it sits closer to a booking funnel than NomadSwipe does, and the page shows no plan prices.

Pricing: not stated on the page read. The price examples on the page are illustrations of price drops, not a plan.

4. Tripomatic

Best for: day-by-day city itineraries; offline maps with Premium

Tripomatic lets you organise your trip into days with drag-and-drop scheduling, see routes between stops and discover attractions. Offline maps for your trip places are a Premium feature in the mobile app.

  • Strength: turns a list of places into a daily schedule with routes.
  • Weakness: the free plan allows up to 5 days with planned activities per calendar month across all trips, and Premium pricing is not stated on the page read.

Pricing: free plan with the monthly limit above; Premium removes the limits and the ads.

5. Polarsteps

Best for: recording a trip as you travel

Polarsteps is a travel tracker and trip planner that records where you go. The free plan includes planning, tracking, reliving and following trips, plus up to 5 Travel Buddies. The paid Plus tier adds 3D maps, map styles, step stats and play mode.

  • Strength: a good fit if the record of the trip matters as much as the plan.
  • Weakness: the Plus page does not describe place recommendations or bookings, so it does not replace a city shortlist or a booking tool.

Pricing: free; Plus costs €8.99 a month or €29.99 a year. The page says prices may vary by region.

6. TripIt

Best for: organising booked flights and hotels

TripIt is built around the trip you have already booked. Its Pro tier adds flight status alerts, Seat Tracker, Fare Tracker, check-in reminders, airport maps and terminal and gate updates.

  • Strength: strong alerts and airport tools if you fly often.
  • Weakness: the Pro page lists no place recommendations, so it does not help you choose what to do in a city.

Pricing: free sign-up; Pro is $49 a year after a 30-day free trial. Read our TripIt alternatives comparison for more.

AI suggestions, a map, or your own picks

FAQ

Is Layla free?

Planning a trip with Layla is free, with no subscription or planning fee, according to Layla's FAQ. You pay for the travel arrangements included in your booking. Layla's own blog says a premium version costs around $10 a month, while Pilot, a competitor, reports $49 a year, so check the current price in the app.

Does NomadSwipe build an itinerary from a chat?

No. NomadSwipe deals real places one card at a time. You save the ones you want and see them pinned on a map of your trip.

Can NomadSwipe book hotels or flights?

The Google Play listing does not describe bookings. From a place's page you can get directions, visit its website or call ahead.

Is NomadSwipe available on Android?

Yes. NomadSwipe is free to download on Google Play for Android phones.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Up to 20 saved trips are available offline, according to the Google Play listing.

Who owns Layla?

Travolution reported on 3 August 2026 that Layla is now part of Expedia Group. Layla is based in Berlin, Germany.
