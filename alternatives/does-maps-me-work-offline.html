Does Maps.me work offline? What works without signal, and what does not

Does Maps.me work offline? Yes, once you have downloaded the map for your area: its own store listings say offline maps, offline search and turn-by-turn navigation need no internet. I found no live traffic feature, and downloading maps and booking hotels need a connection. Test it in airplane mode before you fly.

The short answer: yes after the download, with limits

The Play listing opens with "Fast, detailed and entirely offline maps with turn-by-turn navigation" and says "no internet is required". Apple's listing repeats it. The official terms of use, last updated 19 September 2026, describe the service as letting you "access and download maps of areas across the world" and find routes and navigation "whether online or offline".

The key word is download. Maps.me does not carry the world on your phone from the first launch: you pick the countries or regions you need and download them while you have data or Wi-Fi.

How to get maps onto the phone, and what I could confirm

Maps.me's help center (support.maps.me) failed to load with an SSL error on 18 November 2026, so I give no menu names or tap paths. These are the steps the official pages support.

  1. Install the app from the store. The Play listing is "MAPS.ME: Offline maps GPS Nav" under the developer name Maps_Me, with 50M+ downloads. Apple's lookup lists the seller as Convexity Holdings AG and version 18.1.1 from 11 November 2026.
  2. Download the map for each country or region you will visit. The official site says "Download a map, choose your route", and its Maps page lists maps by country. How you start a download inside the app is not on any page I could read.
  3. Do it before you travel. Downloading on Wi-Fi is my advice, not a vendor rule.
  4. Check the region cap. The OpenStreetMap wiki says a subscription is needed to download more than 10 map regions (March 2024 pricing). I could not confirm that from the vendor, so check it in the app before a multi-country trip. The cost side is in is Maps.ME worth it.

A pre-trip offline checklist you can run in ten minutes

This is my own checklist, not Maps.me's. The pages above are claims; the proof is your phone. Run it while you still have Wi-Fi.

  • Switch on airplane mode after the downloads finish, and keep it on for the whole test.
  • Open the map and pan to the neighborhood where you are staying. If it shows blank tiles, that region is missing.
  • Search for the hotel and one restaurant by name. This tests offline search, which is separate from the map.
  • Plan a route between two points you will actually travel, once on foot and once by the transport you plan to use. Note whether public transport appears.
  • Open a bookmark, and note the addresses of your top places somewhere that needs no app.
  • Check available storage. See the storage section below, and keep room for photos.

Storage and battery cautions

The app itself is large before any map is added: Apple's lookup gives 330.4 MB for the iPhone and iPad version. I found no per-country map sizes on the official pages: the Maps page lists countries with city counts, such as 39,344 cities for the United States, and no megabytes. The OpenStreetMap wiki header says the whole planet fits in 27 GB, undated, so treat it as an order of magnitude.

Check the size next to each map before you tap download. The Play listing also warns that "continued use of GPS running in the background can dramatically decrease battery life".

Which app this is today

The official site footer reads "© 2026 Convexity Holdings AG", the Terms of Use name that Swiss company as the party you contract with, and Apple lists it as seller. Wikipedia says Mail.Ru Group bought MapsWithMe in November 2014 and sold it to Daegu Limited in November 2020; I found no page linking Daegu to Convexity, so I do not say how they relate. The history is in what happened to Maps.me.

Organic Maps is a separate app. Its site says it was made by "the same people who created MapsWithMe/Maps.Me". CoMaps describes itself as "a fork of Organic Maps and Maps.Me". The fork route is covered in Organic Maps alternatives.

Where Maps.me stops as a trip planner

Maps.me answers "how do I get from here to there with no signal". It does not answer "which places should I go to in this city". Its listings describe maps, guides, bookmarks and hotel booking, not a shortlist per trip or a day plan. For the verdict, see is Maps.ME worth it. When Maps.me is the right tool

It fits when you will be somewhere with weak or expensive data, you want turn-by-turn directions for walking, cycling or driving from a downloaded map, and you are willing to test it before you go. It is less convincing if you need a planner or need to be sure the region cap will not bite.

Summary: what to remember

  • Maps.me works offline for maps, search and navigation once you have downloaded the area, according to its Play listing, App Store listing and official site.
  • Downloading and updating maps, and booking hotels, need a connection. No live traffic feature is described on the pages read.
  • Bookmarks and travel guides are listed, but the pages do not say whether they need a connection.
  • The OpenStreetMap wiki reports a 10-region cap without a subscription, which I could not confirm from the vendor.
  • Apple's lookup gives the app 330.4 MB before any map is added; no per-map sizes were published on the pages read, so check the size in the app.
  • The only real proof is an airplane-mode test at home.

Questions about Maps.me offline

Does Maps.me work offline?

Yes, once you have downloaded the map for the area. Its Play and App Store listings say maps, offline search and turn-by-turn navigation for driving, walking and cycling work with no internet. Downloading maps, hotel booking and anything else that talks to a server needs a connection. I did not test the app on a phone.

Does Maps.me have live traffic?

No live traffic feature is described in the Play listing, Apple's listing or the official site. Offline routing is built from the downloaded map, so I would not expect traffic-aware routes, but that is my inference, not a statement from the vendor.

Is Maps.me still the same app as Organic Maps?

No. Wikipedia says Maps.me was sold to Daegu Limited in November 2020 and that the founders of the original MapsWithMe then released Organic Maps as a fork in January 2021. The OpenStreetMap wiki says CoMaps, a fork of Organic Maps, started in early 2025. Maps.me itself is listed under Convexity Holdings AG today.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

NomadSwipe is not an offline-maps or navigation app. I built NomadSwipe (Android) to choose places by swiping, and its Play listing says up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online. Offline copies expire, so open a trip online again to refresh it.
