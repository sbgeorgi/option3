Is Sygic better than Google Maps? It depends on how you travel

Is Sygic better than Google Maps? It depends: Sygic for driving on offline country maps if you pay for Premium+ (voice guidance, live traffic); Google Maps for walking, cycling, transit, saved lists and everyday search. Both offer offline maps, but Sygic's free version only plans and shows a route; turn-by-turn needs Premium+.

Which Sygic app, and what each one does

Sygic is two different things, and only one of them competes with Google Maps. Sygic GPS Navigation is a driving navigator: its product page describes guidance that works without an internet connection, 3D maps, parking and fuel-price info, speed camera warnings, CarPlay and Android Auto, and a Motorbike Mode. Sygic Travel is a trip planner (day-by-day itinerary, travel guides, maps you download ahead); its page is still on sygic.com, but its Google Play listing now reads Tripomatic Planner & Maps (formerly Sygic Travel), by Tripomatic s.r.o. This page compares the navigator with Google Maps. The planner has its own page: Sygic Travel alternatives.

Which to pick if...

  1. Sygic GPS Navigation, if you drive abroad or through patchy coverage and want a country map saved on the phone, with voice guidance and live traffic. Plan on Premium+: the support article lists those as paid. Download the map of every country you will cross, because the offline maps page says navigation fails without them.
  2. Different need: NomadSwipe, if you are choosing what to do on a trip and not how to drive there. I built NomadSwipe (Android): it saves real places on one trip map, has a route builder for ordering stops, and shows your trips on a 3D globe. It does not navigate; its route builder opens turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps. iPhone is coming soon.
  3. Google Maps, if you walk, cycle or use transit, want saved lists you can share, or mostly want one app for search, reviews and directions with a live connection. Its offline mode covers driving only and shows no traffic.

Many travelers use both: Google Maps for the city, Sygic for the rental car. That is my reading of the table, not something either company says.

Offline, in plain terms

Both apps let you download maps, but they promise different things. Sygic's help article has you open the menu, tap Offline maps, then Add offline maps, and pick a country or, for large countries, a region. Google's article has you search a place or choose your own map, then tap Download. Google guides you offline only while the entire route is inside the downloaded area, and it drops walking, cycling and transit directions offline. Sygic's pages I read do not say whether its walking directions work offline.

The short version

As of 22 November 2026, Sygic GPS Navigation is the better pick for offline car navigation if you accept Premium+, because the free version only plans and displays a route. Google Maps is the better pick for walking, cycling, transit, saved lists and everyday search, and its offline mode is driving only. Neither plans a day-by-day trip; Sygic's planner is now Tripomatic. Prices and plan names change, so check the purchase screen. Questions about Sygic and Google Maps

Is Sygic better than Google Maps for offline use?

For driving abroad on a downloaded country map, probably yes. Sygic's offline maps page says you can save maps for over 200 countries, and Google's help page says offline guidance works only if the whole route is inside the downloaded area, with no walking, cycling or transit directions and no traffic or alternate routes while offline. The catch is that Sygic's turn-by-turn voice guidance needs Premium+.

Is Sygic free?

Partly. Sygic's support article says the free basic version includes offline maps, search, route planning and a route shown on the map. Voice guidance, turn-by-turn directions, live traffic, speed limits and the lane assistant are listed as Premium+. The Google Play listing shows in-app purchases and I did not read a Premium+ price, so check the price in the app.

Does Google Maps work offline?

Yes, within limits. Google's help page says you can download an area and use Maps to guide you as long as the entire route is within that offline map. While offline, transit, bicycling and walking directions are unavailable, and driving shows no traffic info or alternate routes. Some countries and regions cannot be downloaded.

Is Sygic Travel still around?

The planner has been renamed. Sygic's Sygic Travel page still describes day-by-day itineraries and travel guides, but its Google Play listing now reads Tripomatic Planner & Maps (formerly Sygic Travel), published by Tripomatic s.r.o., and says offline maps need Tripomatic Premium. My Sygic Travel alternatives page covers that app.

Which is better for planning a trip with several stops?

Neither is built around a trip. Google Maps has saved-place lists you can share, and Sygic's route planner lets you add stops to a drive. NomadSwipe (Android, which I built) is a different kind of tool: it saves real places on one trip map and builds a route of stops, then hands the directions to Google Maps.
