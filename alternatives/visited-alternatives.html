Apps Like Visited: 4 Travel Trackers Compared, and Where NomadSwipe Fits

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android). It is not a country tracker, so it is not one of the four trackers compared, but I say where it fits and where it does not. Facts checked on 15 November 2026. Updated 15 November 2026.

Key facts

  • Visited is a tracker you fill in by hand: its own FAQ says there is no GPS tracking and that it does not read photo metadata.
  • Its FAQ lists city and regional maps, itineraries, all travel lists, more photos and no ads as paid, and says the itinerary feature works at the country level.

What Visited is and what it does

The Visited site calls it a travel tracker app for mapping your travels and discovering where to go next. You build a world map from countries you have been to, want to visit and have lived in, with city and regional views and a map of pins for specific places. It adds themed travel lists to check off (the site names world heritage sites, culinary experiences, national parks and film locations), a stats dashboard, shared maps for comparing with friends, and printed travel posters. Its FAQ says the app can break down states, provinces and regions, and names Australia, Canada, France, Germany and the UK next to the United States.

The Google Play listing is titled "Visited: Travel Map with Lists", shows "Contains ads" and in-app purchases, 100K+ downloads and an update on 2 October 2026. Its Data safety section says no data is shared with third parties and that it may collect personal info, app activity and two other types. Apple's listing for Visited: Travel Tracker & Map shows 4.6 from 14K ratings and says Free with in-app purchases. I read both through a browser and installed neither app.

Why people look for alternatives to Visited

I did not survey reviews or forums, so I do not say what Visited's users complain about. What I can show is where Visited's own pages leave a gap, which is usually why someone opens a comparison like this one:

  • Paid tiers. The FAQ says the free part is the country map, travel lists, travel statistics and comparing with friends. It says paid unlocks city and regional maps, itineraries, more inspirational photos, access to all travel lists, no ads and advanced stats. If you want a city map without paying, a tracker with a different split may suit you better, and I did not check what the others lock.
  • Everything is typed in. The FAQ states there is no GPS tracking and no reading of photo metadata, and gives privacy as the reason. If you would rather your phone logged the trip, that is a different app.
  • No custom lists. The FAQ says custom travel lists are not available and are planned for the future, so you tick off Visited's lists, not your own.
  • Planning is coarse. The FAQ describes itineraries built from hotels, places, experiences, cities and dates, and says the feature is premium and works at the country level. It does not describe planning the days inside one city.
  • Looking back versus looking ahead. Visited is built around where you have been. If your question is what to do in a city you land in tomorrow, a tally does not answer it.

Pin Traveler: the closest match with a diary and trip planner

The Play listing describes pinning countries, states, cities and regions on a customisable world map, a travel diary with photos on each pin, statistics on states and countries, distance travelled on road trips, and a trip planner with itinerary templates (itinerary template apps compares more of them). It shows in-app purchases, 4.5 from 1.57K reviews and 100K+ downloads. The Pin Traveler site adds a trip-building view with dates and the route between stops, search across countries, destinations and wishlist pins, and an image of your map to share, and shows 4.7 and 2M+ downloads. Visited's FAQ names it as an alternative, and also says Visited is a strong alternative to it, which is Visited talking about a rival, not evidence.

been: country tracking, kept simple

The Play listing for "been: track visited countries" is the thinnest of the four: mark countries you have visited, browse detailed maps, keep a logbook and checklist, and share them. It shows 4.6 from 12.2K reviews and 1M+ downloads, with in-app purchases. It names no regions, cities, lists or itineraries, so for those it is not comparable with Visited on the page alone. Its Data safety section says it may share app activity, app info and performance, and device or other IDs, which is the opposite of the Visited and Pin Traveler entries. Visited's FAQ lists "Been app" among alternatives; I did not verify that it means this exact listing.

Countries Been: the most places to mark

The Play listing describes a map of countries and cities you have visited, lived in and want to visit, bucket list progress, travel statistics and sharing. It says you can mark over 140,000 places, read Wikipedia, Wikivoyage and images for most places, and see monthly weather for more than 9,000 cities. It shows 4.8 from 25.7K reviews, 1M+ downloads, "Contains ads" and in-app purchases. Its Data safety section says it may share app activity and that data is not encrypted, as declared by the developer. If you want a lot of places to tick and some reading material per place, this is the one the pages support; there is no itinerary tool on the listing.

Polarsteps: a trip log that tracks you

This one answers a different problem. The Play listing splits the app into plan, track, share and relive: an AI itinerary planner, a transport planner, automatic route tracking on a world map, photos, videos and stories on each step, travel stats for countries and distance, Trip Reels and printed Travel Books. It shows 4.7 from 200K reviews, 10M+ downloads and an Editors' Choice badge. The Polarsteps site says 23M+ travelers and 4.7 from 460K ratings. Its Data safety section lists location among the types it may collect, which follows from tracking a route. It is a travel journal first, not a country counter. Visited's FAQ also names Skratch. I did not read any Skratch page, so I do not describe it.

Where the sources disagree

Reading the pages side by side turned up five mismatches. None is a scandal, but each changes what you can safely say.

  1. Visited's rating on one page. The Play header showed 4.4 from 3.48K reviews while the ratings block lower on the same page showed 4.5 from 3.37K. I quote the header figure and say Play showed it.
  2. Visited's list count. The site's home page says over 150 travel lists and Apple's listing says over 150 categories, the premium bullet on the same home page says over 175, and the Play listing says over 185. The FAQ says paid unlocks access to all lists, so the numbers may measure free against paid. I did not check that.
  3. "Millions of travelers". The Visited site says millions of travelers choose it. Google Play shows 100K+ downloads and Apple's listing shows 14K ratings. Those are different measures, and neither one confirms or refutes the other.
  4. Pin Traveler's reach. Its site shows 2M+ downloads and 4.7. Google Play shows 100K+ downloads and 4.5. The site number is presumably all platforms; the page does not say.
  5. Countries Been's coverage. One paragraph of its listing says states and provinces of over 30 countries can be explored, and the list below it says states, provinces or regions of over 25 countries can be downloaded.

Where NomadSwipe fits (and where it does not)

For choosing where to go next, not for keeping score

I built NomadSwipe (Android), so weigh this with that in mind. It is not a country tracker. There is no been-to map, no country or city counter, no stats and no lists to tick off, and it does not log your route. It is a place-discovery and trip-building app, not a review site or a booking platform.

What it does: pick a city and it deals real restaurants, sights, bars and stays one card at a time. Swipe right or tap Save to keep one, swipe left or tap Skip to pass. Cards show the category, the rating, how far away the place is and, where hours are known, whether it is open. Saved places are pinned on a trip map, with a list view sorted by rating, distance or A to Z, and a custom pin for anything the cards miss, such as your hotel. The listing's sample trip is a long weekend in New York with 16 places, all picked by swiping. Visited has shared maps and friend comparison; the NomadSwipe listing describes neither, so I make no claim about them.

The honest split: Visited, Pin Traveler, been or Countries Been keep the record of where you have been. NomadSwipe is for the next city on that map. They do not talk to each other, and I make no claim that a list moves from one to the other. iPhone is coming soon. Who should pick which

  • You want to stay with Visited: you like its themed lists, regional breakdowns and stats, and you accept that you type in every place.
  • Different need, picking what to see once you are there: NomadSwipe (Android), which I built. It is not a tracker, so use it alongside one if you also want the record.
  • A map plus a photo diary on every pin: Pin Traveler, per its listing.
  • The simplest country checklist: been, which names little beyond marking countries and a logbook.
  • A very large set of places to mark, with reading per place: Countries Been.
  • Your phone to log the trip as you travel, then relive it: Polarsteps, which is the only one here that lists automatic route tracking.
  • The least data declared as shared: Visited and Pin Traveler both declare none shared on Google Play; check each app's current Data safety page.

FAQ

What is the Visited travel app?

Visited is a travel tracker from Arriving In High Heels. You mark countries you have been to, want to visit or have lived in on a world map, with regional and city maps, themed travel lists to tick off, and travel stats. This page covers that app, the one at visitedapp.com, not other apps that share the name.

Is Visited free?

Its own FAQ calls it a free travel app with paid features sold as in-app purchases. The free part it lists is the country map, travel lists, travel statistics and comparing with friends. It lists city and regional maps, itineraries, all travel lists and no ads as paid. Its Google Play listing says it contains ads.

Does Visited track your location?

Not by its own account. The Visited FAQ says the app has no GPS tracking and does not read the metadata of your photos, so you enter every place yourself. Its Google Play Data safety section says no data is shared with third parties and lists personal info and app activity among the types it may collect.

What are good apps like Visited?

For the same job, tracking where you have been: Pin Traveler, been and Countries Been, which Visited's own FAQ also names alongside Skratch. For a trip log with automatic route tracking, Polarsteps. For choosing places to visit in a city, I built NomadSwipe (Android), which is not a country tracker.

Is there an app like Visited that tracks your route automatically?

Polarsteps. Its Google Play listing lists automatic route tracking on a digital world map, plus photos, videos and stories added to each step, and travel stats for countries visited and distance covered. That is the opposite of Visited's stated approach of no GPS tracking.

Is NomadSwipe a travel tracker like Visited?

No. I built NomadSwipe (Android). It has no been-to map, no country counter, no stats and no lists to tick off. It is for deciding where to go next: pick a city, swipe real places, save the ones you like and see them pinned on a trip map.

Does NomadSwipe have shared maps or friend comparison like Visited?

Its Google Play listing describes neither, so I make no claim about them. I built NomadSwipe (Android), and what the listing describes is swiping real places, saving them to a shortlist per trip and seeing every pick on a travel map.

Is NomadSwipe on iPhone?

Not yet. NomadSwipe is on Google Play for Android phones, and iPhone is coming soon.
