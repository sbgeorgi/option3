Key facts

  • Yelp describes itself as connecting people with local businesses. Its Google Play listing leads with restaurants, food delivery, home services and auto repair, so a traveler looking for sights is a smaller part of what it is built for.
  • Yelp says software, not staff, decides which reviews are recommended, and reviews it does not recommend do not count toward a business's star rating or review count.
  • Of the four alternatives, two carry written reviews (Google Maps, Tripadvisor), one is a check-in app (Swarm by Foursquare; see apps like Foursquare) and one has no reviews at all (NomadSwipe).
  • Star ratings on Google Play measure the app, not the places in it, and the same listing sometimes shows two different review counts. I quote the header figure and say so.
  • NomadSwipe is Android only, shows no user reviews and takes no bookings. It has 50+ downloads on Google Play, so it has little track record.

What Yelp is, from its own pages

The Yelp about page carries one line of description: Yelp connects people with great local businesses. Its navigation lists Restaurants, Home & Garden, Auto Services, Health & Beauty and Travel & Activities. The Yelp Google Play listing shows 4.6 from 660K reviews, 50M+ downloads, a "contains ads" label and an update on 12 November 2026. Its description cites over 200 million Yelp reviews worldwide and lists restaurant reservations and food delivery through partners such as GrubHub and ChowNow, salon booking through partners such as Booksy and Vagaro, quotes from home service contractors, and a bookmark feature to save and share businesses. A line about travel mentions hotels, tours, attractions and parks, and adding places to a trip planner.

The listing's ratings block, lower on the same page, showed 639K reviews rather than 660K. Both are Google Play figures for the app, and neither says anything about how good a restaurant is.

Why people look for something else

This is not a survey. It is what the pages I read give me, labelled by source.

  1. Reviews Yelp hides on purpose. On its recommendation software page, Yelp says automated software evaluates every review on hundreds of signals, that it applies the same rules to advertisers and non-advertisers, and that no employee can override it. It also says a review can land in "not recommended" simply because the software knows too little about the reviewer, and that those reviews do not count toward the star rating. Some people value that. If you want to read everything, it means an extra tap at the bottom of each page.
  2. A directory of services, not a trip tool. The order of the Play description, delivery first, then salons, home repair and pet services, shows who the app is organised for.
  3. Ads. The Play listing carries a "contains ads" label. Google Maps and Tripadvisor carry the same label, so this does not separate Yelp from the other two.
  4. App friction, in a sample of three. The Play page showed three user reviews when I read it, dated 2021, 2022 and June 2026. One reviewer said food photos had been lost or buried. One said saving places and keeping notes was awkward and that the map would not let them tap places unless they zoomed in far. One reported a link that kept opening the website. Yelp replied to each with a support link. Three reviews are an anecdote, and they may no longer describe the app.
  5. Where you are going. Yelp's locations page lists US cities only, and its country selector shows United States. But the yelp.ca and yelp.co.uk addresses redirected to Toronto and London pages with recent reviews of local places. I looked at one city each, so I cannot say how deep coverage is in the city you plan to visit.

Alternatives to Yelp, one at a time

Google Maps: the widest set of places

The Google Maps listing is the closest match to what most people use Yelp for. It says you can search for local places and things to do and decide from user reviews and photos, filter restaurants by cuisine, hours, price and rating, see how busy a place is, and ask questions about a place. It adds custom lists of favorite saved places that you can share, reservations, takeout and delivery ordering, and hotel booking. It is also a navigation app, with routing for driving, walking, cycling and public transport. The trade-off is that one app does everything, so the place-finding part sits next to routing, traffic and Street View. Its listing was updated on 12 November 2026. More on the saving side in remembering places to visit in a city. For vegan and vegetarian food specifically, see apps like HappyCow.

Tripadvisor: reviews built around a trip

The Tripadvisor listing calls the app a way to plan and book trips. It says you can save experiences, hotels and restaurants, get an itinerary planner with an AI trip builder that bases recommendations on your saves, book stays, and browse reviews from travelers, with AI summaries of what they say. Tripadvisor's about page says the group aims to be the world's most trusted source for travel and experiences. Like Yelp, it is review-led, but the listing contains no home services or auto repair, only travel categories. Updated 3 November 2026 on Play. Two of our own pages cover it in more depth: apps like Tripadvisor and Tripadvisor against Google Maps. For the itinerary side alone, see itinerary planner apps compared.

Swarm by Foursquare: check-ins and lists, no reviews described

This one needs a caveat. The Foursquare consumer app on Google Play is Swarm, and its listing describes one-tap check-ins, a personal map and travel log, local search for coffee shops and restaurants, custom lists such as a "Tokyo Trip" list, and a social map of where your friends have checked in. It says every place comes from Foursquare's own place database. It describes no written reviews. The foursquare.com homepage I read is a pitch to marketers and developers about location data, says Foursquare has joined Infillion, and links to Swarm in its navigation. I did not look for any other Foursquare app, so I do not say whether a separate city guide exists. Swarm was updated on 23 October 2026 on Play. It is the most social of the five and the least like Yelp.

NomadSwipe: choosing places, not reading about them

For a few days in a city you do not know

I built NomadSwipe (Android), so weigh this accordingly. Pick a city and it deals real restaurants, sights, bars and stays one card at a time. Swipe right or tap Save to keep a place, swipe left or tap Skip to pass. Each card shows the category, the rating, how far away the place is and, where hours are known, whether it is open. Open a trip and every saved place is pinned on a travel map; the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z. The Play listing's sample is a long weekend in New York with 16 places, all picked by swiping. Related reading: swipe travel apps compared and how to plan a trip on a map.

What it is not: it is not a review site, and its listing describes no way to write or read reviews inside the app. It does not say where its ratings come from, so I make no claim about that. It is not a booking platform or a delivery app. It is Android only and iPhone is coming soon. And it has 50+ downloads on Google Play, so it has far less history than the other four. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online, and the copies expire.

Which app for which job

  • You want many written reviews of a restaurant or a plumber: Yelp, or Google Maps. Yelp is the only one of the five whose listing names home services and contractor quotes.
  • You want reviews and booking for a trip in one app: Tripadvisor.
  • You want reviews, saved lists and directions in one app: Google Maps, with the limit that it does all of it at once.
  • You want a personal map of where you have been, with friends' check-ins: Swarm by Foursquare.
  • You want to decide where to go without reading anything: NomadSwipe, then check any place you are unsure about in a review app.

FAQ

What is the best app like Yelp for a traveler?

It depends on the job. Google Maps has the widest set of places and reviews on its listing, and Tripadvisor is built around travel reviews and booking. I built NomadSwipe (Android), which has no reviews: it is for choosing places by swiping, then saving them on a map. I did not use any of these apps for this page.

Is Google Maps a good alternative to Yelp?

For finding a place and reading reviews, its own Play listing says it covers over 250 million businesses and places with photos and reviews, plus custom lists, reservations and navigation. Its Play rating is 3.4, but that is a rating of the app, not of the places in it. Yelp's listing says it filters reviews with software, which is a different approach.

Does Tripadvisor have reviews like Yelp?

Yes. Its Play listing says you can browse reviews from travelers, save experiences, hotels and restaurants, and use an AI trip builder. It is aimed at trips, so it leaves out the home services, auto repair and salon listings that Yelp's listing leads with. For a closer comparison read Tripadvisor against Google Maps.

Is Foursquare still an app like Yelp?

Partly. The consumer app on Google Play is Swarm, a check-in and place-discovery app, and its listing describes check-ins, lists and a social map rather than written reviews. The foursquare.com homepage I read today is aimed at marketers and developers and links to Swarm in its navigation. I did not look for any other Foursquare consumer app.

Does Yelp work outside the United States?

I can only report what loaded. Yelp's locations page lists US cities and its country selector shows United States. The yelp.ca and yelp.co.uk addresses I opened today showed Toronto and London pages with recent reviews. I looked at one city each, so I cannot judge how deep Yelp's coverage is where you are going.

Why do some Yelp reviews not count toward the star rating?

Yelp says automated recommendation software evaluates every review, and reviews it does not recommend stay reachable through a link at the bottom of the business page but do not count toward the star rating or review count. Yelp says the software also looks for suspected conflicts of interest and solicited reviews.

Is NomadSwipe a review site like Yelp?

No. I built NomadSwipe (Android), a place-discovery and trip-building app: you swipe real places in a city, save the ones you like, see them on a map and build a trip. Its Google Play listing describes no way to write or read user reviews inside the app, and it is not a booking platform. For what other people thought of a place, use Yelp, Google Maps or Tripadvisor.

Can I book a table or order food in NomadSwipe?

No. The listing describes no reservations, delivery or booking. It says you can open a place to see photos and start directions, then visit its website or call it when the place lists them. I built NomadSwipe (Android), and booking a table would happen on the place's own site or in another app.

Is NomadSwipe on iPhone?

Not yet. NomadSwipe is on Google Play for Android phones, and iPhone is coming soon.
