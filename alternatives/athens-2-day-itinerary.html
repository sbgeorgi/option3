Athens in 2 days, planned from 16 real places

Athens in 2 days, planned from 16 places pulled from the NomadSwipe app's Athens deck: five museums, a garden, a gallery, four places to eat, a cafe and four bars. Each day stays in one part of the centre, and every rating and review count is shown as stored.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts and addresses are the values stored in the app's Athens place data, read on 3 November 2026. They may be older than that date, so treat them as a snapshot.

Before you start

  • This is a plan for the centre: both days sit within a few kilometres of Syntagma Square.
  • The Acropolis hill is not one of the 16 stops. The app's Athens data has no card for it, so the plan uses the Acropolis Museum, and the hill is yours to add (see the notes at the end).
  • Opening hours and ticket prices are left out. I tried the Acropolis Museum's own site and it returned an error, so check each place's website the day you go.

Day 1: Acropolis Museum to Monastiraki Square

Day one is the compact one. By straight line between stored coordinates, the eight stops sit within 1.2 km of each other, and going stop to stop adds up to about 2.1 km. Streets and slopes make the real walk longer.

  1. Acropolis Museum: the stored card is a museum on Athanasiou Diakou. Wikipedia says it opened on 20 June 2009 on the southeastern slope of the Acropolis hill, with more than 4,250 objects across 14,000 square metres (Wikipedia). Start here.
  2. LIONDI Traditional Greek Restaurant: stored as a Greek restaurant, on Makri, about 40 m from the museum by stored coordinates. A natural lunch stop.
  3. Museum of Greek Folk Musical Instruments: stored as a museum, at 1 Diogenous.
  4. Taf The Art Foundation: stored as an art gallery, at 5 Normanou.
  5. Falafellas (Athens): stored as a Mediterranean place to eat, on Aiolou.
  6. Vintage Wine Bar & Bistro: stored as a bar, on Mitropoleos.
  7. 360 Cocktail Bar: stored as a bar, with an address on Monastiraki Square. Wikipedia describes Monastiraki as a flea market neighbourhood in the old town, with a metro station on the square (Wikipedia).
  8. A for Athens rooftop bar: stored as a rooftop bar, on Miaouli. End the evening here.

Day 2: museums north of Syntagma, Kolonaki and the garden

Day two spreads out more. The straight-line total is about 4.4 km, and the longest single leg, from the first stop to the second, is about 1.4 km. The two stops furthest apart are 1.9 km from each other. Hold the garden and the cafe for the afternoon, when you are heading back toward the centre.

  1. National Archaeological Museum: stored as a museum, at Eikostis Ogdois Oktovriou 44. Wikipedia places it in the Exarcheia area and says the current building was begun in 1866 and completed in 1889 (Wikipedia). Start early and give it the morning.
  2. Kotsanas Museum of Ancient Greek Technology: stored as a museum, at 6 Pindarou.
  3. Frankie: stored as a restaurant, with Kolonaki in its address, on Skoufa.
  4. Byzantine and Christian Museum: stored as a museum, on Leoforos Vasilissis Sofias.
  5. National Garden: stored as a garden, entered from Leoforos Amalias. Wikipedia says it sits behind the Greek Parliament, between the Kolonaki and Pangrati districts, and covers 15.5 hectares (Wikipedia).
  6. Tazza: stored as a cafe, on Petraki.
  7. Rhino Vegan Beat Athens: stored as a restaurant, on Perikleous.
  8. GB Roof Garden: stored as a rooftop bar, with Syntagma Square in its address. Finish here.

Getting around

  • Metro: Wikipedia lists three Athens Metro lines. Syntagma is served by lines 2 and 3, and Monastiraki by lines 1 and 3 (Wikipedia). The Acropolis Museum's entrance is next to Akropoli station (Wikipedia).
  • Day one on foot: the stops are close enough that a walking day is realistic, but allow time for the slopes around the museum.
  • Day two with one hop: the first leg, from the National Archaeological Museum to Kotsanas, is the longest, so a taxi or the metro saves your legs for the rest.
  • Fares: I left fares out. The ticket page I found was last updated in 2021, so check the current fares with the operator.
  • In the app: each card shows its distance, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 3 November 2026 with read-only queries. Athens is stored as two destination rows, both named Athens, holding 499 and 248 cards. Together that is 747 cards, which share 683 distinct normalized titles, so some places appear as more than one card. I measured distance from a reference point at Syntagma Square.

  • Of the 747 Athens cards, 432 have a stored rating, and 345 of those rated cards are rated 4.0 or higher. That is four in five of the rated cards, but fewer than half of all 747 cards.
  • 179 of the 432 rated cards are rated 4.5 or higher.
  • I grouped the cards by their stored category into four buckets I defined myself: 454 restaurants, cafes and other places, 116 museums, galleries, gardens, parks and attractions, 89 hotels and 88 bars and nightlife places. The Athens deck is therefore mostly food and drink, with restaurants, cafes and others making up more than half of the cards.
  • 521 of the 747 cards sit within 3 km of the reference point, 172 sit more than 5 km away, and 23 sit more than 50 km away. One of those 23 has an address in Cincinnati, Ohio, which shows that a destination name alone can pull in places from elsewhere.
  • Searching card titles for the Parthenon, the Temple of Olympian Zeus, Hadrian's Library, the Panathenaic Stadium and Anafiotika returned no matching card. The Acropolis matches were the museum and its cafe, a gallery, a restaurant and hotels, and the Ancient Agora match was a museum card with no stored rating.
  • I chose the 16 stops by hand: places within 3.5 km of the reference point, excluding hotels, nightclubs and travel agents, all rated 4.1 or higher as stored, chosen to mix categories and keep each day in one area. They are five museums, one gallery, one garden, one cafe, four places to eat and four bars. They are not simply the 16 highest-rated cards.
  • Using straight-line distance between stored coordinates, day one totals about 2.1 km and day two about 4.4 km. These are not walking times.

Limits: the ratings and review counts are stored values that may be older than the 3 November 2026 read date, since the stored rows were last updated between 2 May and 16 July 2026. I left out hours and prices. I did not copy any third-party review text.
