Albuquerque in 2 days: 15 stops matched to opening hours

This Albuquerque 2-day itinerary has 15 stops from the NomadSwipe app's 459 distinct places. Day one is a walkable Old Town and museum loop that works Wednesday to Saturday. Day two is a driving day to the Sandia Peak Tramway, with a Sunday and Tuesday swap.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Albuquerque place data, read on 6 November 2026. Every stored row was last updated between 8 May and 13 July 2026, so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Old Town and the museums: the ABQ BioPark Botanic Garden, Tingley Beach, Old Town Albuquerque, the Rattlesnake Museum, lunch at Los Compadres, the Albuquerque Museum, the New Mexico Museum of Natural History and Science, Explora and dinner at Antiquity.
  • Day 2, east side and downtown: Coda Bakery, the National Museum of Nuclear Science & History, the Sandia Peak Tramway, La Cumbre Brewing, dinner at Himawari Ramen and the Kimo Theatre.
  • Days of the week: day one works Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Day two works Monday and Wednesday to Saturday. Day one has swaps for Sunday and Tuesday, day two has swaps for Sunday and Tuesday, and Monday is a poor day for day one (see the weekday table).
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 5.0 km by straight line between stored coordinates, with no leg over 1.22 km. Day two adds up to about 38.7 km, with two legs of about 14.7 km. These are not travel times.

A deck with few sights, a lot of food, and some places that are not in Albuquerque

Of the 459 distinct places in the Albuquerque deck, 98 are sights by my grouping, 276 are restaurants, cafes, bakeries and other food places and 35 are bars, breweries and pubs. The sights span ten stored categories, and 58 of the 98 have a stored rating. Only 20 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews, and 5 of those lie outside a rough box around the city, including a botanical garden in Phoenix and zoos in Colorado Springs, Oklahoma City and Tucson. That leaves 15 cards, which are 14 places because Tingley Beach is stored twice under two place IDs. This plan uses ten sights from those, five of them museums, and adds five restaurants, a bakery and a brewery for the rest of the day.

  • Prices are left out. I did not source ticket prices from official pages this run, so check each place's own website the day you go. Official sites often block automated readers, and I did not use any of them.
  • Some well-known names are missing. A title search found no card for Petroglyph National Monument, the Rio Grande Nature Center, Sandia Crest or the Balloon Fiesta (details in the findings).
  • Add your own pins. Anything you want that the deck lacks is yours to add on the map yourself.

What I left out, and why

  • Duplicate cards. The deck has 463 cards for 459 distinct place IDs, and 420 distinct titles. Tingley Beach is stored under two IDs, as a park and as a beach, and chains repeat once per location. I used each place once.
  • Cards outside the city. 19 of the 459 places sit outside a rough box around Albuquerque. Among the sights, 8 are outside it. The highest-rated outside cards include the Desert Botanical Garden in Phoenix (stored latitude 33.46, longitude -111.94, 4.8 from 21,727 reviews), the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo in Colorado Springs, the Oklahoma City Zoo and the Reid Park Zoo in Tucson. I skipped them. The Coronado Historic Site in Bernalillo (stored as a historic site, 4.5 from 279 reviews, latitude 35.33) also falls just outside the box, so I left it out.
  • Day trips east. Tinkertown Museum (stored at Sandia Park, a museum, 4.7 from 482 reviews) is stored as open Friday to Monday and closed Tuesday to Thursday. It is about 10.5 km farther east than the Tramway by stored coordinates, so I left it out of a two-day plan.
  • Sights with no stored hours. The Turquoise Museum (4.6 from 693 reviews) is stored under the category "School" with no hours, and the Popejoy Hall theatre (4.1 from 103) has none. I used only two such cards, Tingley Beach and the Kimo Theatre.
  • Other sights. The New Mexico Steam Locomotive (4.9 from 207 reviews) is stored as open only on Wednesday and Saturday from 9 AM to 2 PM, so it would have limited the plan to two weekdays. The Balloon Museum is stored at 4.0 from 79 reviews, under my 100-review line, and the 16 art gallery cards have at most 18 reviews each.

Questions about this Albuquerque plan

Can I follow this Albuquerque plan on a Sunday, Monday or Tuesday?

Not as written. On Sunday, drop the Rattlesnake Museum and eat at Don Sushi instead of Antiquity, because both are stored as closed that day, and open day two at 10:30 AM because Coda Bakery opens then. On Tuesday, drop the New Mexico Museum of Natural History and Science on day one and replace the Sandia Peak Tramway with the ABQ BioPark Zoo on day two, because both are stored as closed. On Monday, four day-one stops are stored as closed and I did not build a swap, while day two works as written. The stored hours were read on 6 November 2026, so confirm them before you go.

Which days of the week work for both days?

Day one works as written from Wednesday to Saturday. Day two works as written on Monday and from Wednesday to Saturday. So Wednesday then Thursday, Thursday then Friday and Friday then Saturday all work with the stops and times as written. All 13 stops that have stored hours are stored as open at their suggested start times on those days.

Do I need a car on day two?

Plan on a car or a ride. By straight line between stored coordinates, day two runs 4.2 km from the bakery to the Nuclear Science museum, 14.7 km on to the Sandia Peak Tramway, 14.7 km back to La Cumbre Brewing and 5.1 km to downtown. I read no transit source, so I give straight-line distances only and no travel times. Day one stays within 1.6 km and is the walkable one.

How were the 15 stops chosen?

I picked cards by hand from the app's Albuquerque data with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 100 stored reviews, kept them inside a rough box around the city, and checked each suggested start time against the stored weekly hours. Two stops, Tingley Beach and the Kimo Theatre, have no stored hours, so I kept them for their ratings and their position between other stops.

Where a place had two rows I kept the one with stored hours, then the one with more reviews. The counts below are of the 459 places. An empty stored hours list counts as no stored hours.   • Of the 459 places, 292 have a stored rating, and 206 of those are rated 4.0 or higher (71%). 109 are rated 4.5 or higher (37%). The median stored rating is 4.2.
