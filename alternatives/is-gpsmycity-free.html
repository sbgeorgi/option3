What the free version gives you

Google Play describes the free app in these words: "The app is free to download. Afterwards, you can evaluate the walking tours - see the attractions and use the fully functional offline maps included in each of the city walk guides, all for free." It also lists thousands of travel articles and the "FindMe" location feature as free. The home page says it covers 1,800+ cities and over 7,000 self-guided walks, and that no internet or data plan is needed.

The free tier is therefore a way to see what each walk visits and to carry the city map, not to be guided along it. Both listings say a small payment unlocks the route maps and turn-by-turn navigation. Both also show ads: Google Play says "Contains ads" and the App Store lists "Advertising". The paid upgrade removes them, according to the listing text.

Is it worth paying for?

Test first, since the free part is real: install it, open the walks for your city and see whether the stops and the offline map are what you want. Pay only if you want to be guided along the route. One detail that matters: the $14.99 walks plan and the $14.99 articles plan cover different content, so pick the one that matches what you will use. For scale, the U.S. App Store shows 4.03 from 1,264 ratings (version 5.4.0, released 23 September 2026), and Google Play shows 500K+ downloads and 4.34 from 2,960 ratings, with an update on 27 October 2026. I did not read written reviews, so I cannot tell you what users complain about.

Turn-by-turn directions, route maps, custom walks and ad removal are paid, at $14.99 to $20.99 a year on the U.S. App Store, with a 3-day trial. I could not confirm Android prices per plan. Questions about whether GPSmyCity is free

Is GPSmyCity free to download?

Yes. Its Google Play listing and its U.S. App Store listing both say the app is free to download. Both also show in-app purchases and advertising, so free to install is not the same as free to use for navigation.

How much does GPSmyCity cost?

The U.S. App Store lists annual subscriptions at $14.99 (city walks), $14.99 (travel articles) and $20.99 (walks and articles together), plus single city guides at $4.99. Google Play shows in-app purchases of $1.99 to $20.99 per item, but does not say which price is which, and prices vary by country. I could not confirm an Android price per plan.

Does GPSmyCity work offline?

Its listings and website say yes: no internet or data plan is needed, and the offline city maps in each walk guide are free. Turn-by-turn directions and walk route maps are the paid part.

Can I try GPSmyCity before paying?

Yes in two ways. You can look at walk sights and offline maps for free, and the App Store listing and GPSmyCity's own About and Contact page both describe a 3-day free trial on subscriptions. You cancel it in your Apple or Google account, not with GPSmyCity, before the trial ends.

Is there something like GPSmyCity where I pick the places myself?

NomadSwipe (Android), which I built, is a different kind of app: you swipe through real places in a city, save the ones you like and see them on a map. It has no walking-tour routes, no audio guide and no turn-by-turn directions. For more GPSmyCity-style options, see the GPSmyCity alternatives page.
