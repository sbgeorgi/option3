Is 2 days enough in Lisbon? 23 sights mapped by area

Is 2 days enough in Lisbon? Yes for the best 11 of 23 well-rated sights, if you pick two areas and avoid Monday: Belém and Ajuda one day, the Baixa and Alfama museums the next. No for everything: the 23 need 4.6 days at five a day, and all 47 sight cards need 9.4.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this page. The counts and distances come from the NomadSwipe app's stored Lisbon place data, read with a read-only query on 11 November 2026. The opening hours I quote as fact come from Turismo de Lisboa pages I read the same day (see Sources).

The verdict in numbers

I count sights and divide by a pace, as on my how many days you need in Lisbon page. The pace is my assumption, so here are three.

So two days holds 8 to 12 sights. At five a day that is 10 of the 23 and 10 of the 47. "Enough" therefore means enough for a chosen part. The rest of this page is about choosing it.

Which 23 sights, and why this page and my other Lisbon page differ

I kept the 284 distinct places inside a box from 38.68 to 38.78 N and 9.22 to 9.08 W (the app's Lisbon destination holds 290 cards with 290 distinct place IDs). In that box, 47 places have a stored sight-type category (museum, gallery, attraction, monument, park, garden, landmark, castle, zoo, aquarium, bridge, plaza and similar) across 18 stored categories. 28 of the 47 have stored opening hours. 23 are rated 4.0 or higher with 100 or more reviews, and those are the "well-rated" 23 here.

This is not the same 23 as on my other Lisbon page. That page used a 5 km radius and a type list with lookouts, so it counts four viewpoints, LX Factory and the Doca de Santo Amaro, and files Belém, Ajuda, the Oceanário and the Coach Museum as farther out. This page uses a wider box and a filter that did not match the lookout type, so the viewpoints are missing and Belém is in. Both sets happen to need 4.6 days at five a day. They are two readings of one deck.

The two-day plan that works: Belém, then the centre

I picked the two densest groups. I added the Ajuda palace to Belém because it is only 1.24 km from the Coach Museum, and I dropped the Money Museum from the centre because it is stored as closed on both Monday and Tuesday. The hops below are straight lines, so real walking is longer.

That is 5.5 sights a day and 6.4 km of straight-line hops in total, but only from Tuesday on. It leaves out 12 well-rated sights, among them the Gulbenkian, the Azulejo museum, the zoo, the Oceanário and Eduardo VII Park. A nearest-neighbour chain through the 12 most-reviewed sights would run 23.6 km and touch all five groups, so I would not call that a two-day trip.

What a Monday does to two days

Eight of the 23 are stored as closed on Monday and 3 on Tuesday. Counting the 7 with no hours as not closed, 15 are not stored closed on Monday, 20 on Tuesday and 13 on both. The closures cluster by area: on Monday Belém loses the Tower, the Coach Museum and MAC/CCB, and the centre loses the Pantheon, Fado and Aljube museums. A Monday inside a two-day plan costs you one of the two main days.

  • Tuesday to Sunday trip: the plan above stands, with no stored closure on a Tuesday except the Money Museum, which I left out. For Sunday closures, see the opening-day table on my days page.
  • Monday and Tuesday trip: do the Gulbenkian side on Monday (Gulbenkian Museum, CAM Gulbenkian, Eduardo VII Park and the zoo, 3.2 km of hops, three of the four stored as open on Monday) and Belém and Ajuda on Tuesday. That is 9 sights, not 11, and the centre is dropped. The Gulbenkian is stored as closed on Tuesday, so the order cannot be swapped.

One caution on the Gulbenkian: its own site shows a "Reopening of the Museum" item with no date, and I could not read its hours there. The app's stored Monday and Tuesday values are all I have for it, so check before you plan a day around it.

Questions about two days in Lisbon

Is 2 days enough in Lisbon if I arrive on a Monday?

Yes, but for fewer sights. Of the 23 well-rated sights, 8 are stored as closed on Monday and 3 on Tuesday, including Belém Tower, the National Coach Museum and the National Pantheon on Monday. A Monday and Tuesday plan that works is the Gulbenkian side and the zoo on Monday (4 stops) and Belém and Ajuda on Tuesday (5 stops), which is 9 sights instead of 11.

Can I fit Belém and the Oceanário into 2 days in Lisbon?

You can, but it adds a third area. The Oceanário is 13.3 km in a straight line from Belém Tower and about 6 km from the central museums and the Gulbenkian, so pairing it with Belém means crossing the whole city. The two-day plan on this page leaves it out.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the app's Lisbon destination (country code PRT) on 11 November 2026 with a read-only query: 290 cards, 290 distinct place IDs, 284 distinct places inside latitude 38.68 to 38.78 N and longitude 9.22 to 9.08 W. I counted the 47 with a sight-type category, grouped the 23 well-rated ones by coordinate rules I chose, and computed straight-line distances with the haversine formula in Python locally. I read three Turismo de Lisboa pages the same day for the hours check.

  • 47 sight places across 18 stored categories, 28 with stored hours, 23 rated 4.0 or higher with 100 or more reviews.
  • The two-day plan holds 11 sights: 5 in Belém and Ajuda (3.7 km of hops) and 6 in Baixa and Alfama (2.7 km).
  • 8 of the 23 are stored as closed on Monday, 3 on Tuesday, and 7 have no stored hours. 15 are not stored closed on Monday and 13 on both days.

Limits: this is a counting rule with a pace I chose, and the data has no visit times. Review counts are a popularity proxy: the Oceanário alone has 41,129 of the 124,523 stored reviews across the 23, so I lead with counts and distances. Stored hours are a snapshot and may be out of date. The box, the groups and the category list are my choices, and a filter that matched lookouts would add the Alfama viewpoints. I read no ticket, price or transit page, and I could not read the Gulbenkian's hours from its own site.
