What happened to Wanderlog? It is still running, and this is what changed

What happened to Wanderlog? It is still running: on 10 November 2026 its site loaded and Apple shows an app update released on 4 November 2026. I found no outage or shutdown notice. What changed is new features, Pro and trial complaints, and offline wording that varies by source. For a different planner that changed owners, see what happened to Inspirock.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android) and mention it in one section near the end, so read that section with that in mind. I did not test Wanderlog by hand. Every Wanderlog fact comes from a page I read on 10 November 2026, listed under Sources, and I give each source's own date.

I found no evidence of either. Four things were live when I checked on 10 November 2026:

What people may be noticing

If the app feels different, these are the dated, sourced candidates. None is an official change notice.

  • New features and a visual refresh. Import from anywhere and Trip Journal arrived within the last six months, per the timeline above, and the newest notes mention refreshed visuals and map changes.
  • Pro, trials and charges. Both store listings describe the app as free with an optional Pro subscription. The U.S. App Store listing shows Pro in-app purchases as annual plans at $31.99, $39.99, $49.99 and $59.99 and monthly plans at $5.99 and $16.99, and says Pro adds Google Maps export and Gmail linking. On Trustpilot (read 10 November 2026) Wanderlog shows 1.8 out of 5 from 52 reviews, and the 2026 reviews I saw repeatedly mention trials that convert to paid plans, an unexpected annual charge (April and March 2026 reviews) and a Pro paywall (January 2026). These are individual reviews, so treat them as reports, not facts about every account (Trustpilot).
  • Offline wording differs by source. The help center, dated 10 March 2023, says Pro members can download a trip plan for offline access. The Google Play listing says "Access your trip plans offline (Pro)" in one line and "All your holiday plans are automatically stored offline" in another, and the App Store listing uses the same automatic wording. Test the trip on your own phone before you rely on it.
  • Booking emails and logins. A July 2026 Trustpilot review says forwarded booking emails often do not appear in a trip, and an August 2026 review describes the browser version redirecting to the app in a loop. Wanderlog's own notes list a September fix for logouts and an August fix for loading trips on a slow connection (App Store version history).
  • Pop-ups. One Google Play reviewer in September 2026 described recommendation pop-ups on a Pro account; Wanderlog's reply said it has no ads right now.

If Wanderlog stopped working for you

Wanderlog's help center has no troubleshooting article for loading or login problems that I could find. These steps come from the pages I did read.

  1. Update the app first. Recent release notes list fixes for logouts (2.220), for Pro subscriptions (2.220) and for trip loading on a slow connection (2.216), so an old build may be the cause (App Store version history).
  2. Check that your trip is saved for offline use. Per the help center (10 March 2023), open the trip in the app, wait for it to finish loading, and look for the download icon next to it on the Profile tab. The article says this is for Pro members.
  3. Check forwarded bookings. The help center (4 March 2023) says reservations go to a unique address shown under Reservations and attachments, reached by tapping the flights, lodging or rental car icon and choosing Forward email. Use the address shown for that trip (help article).
  4. Email support with the details it asks for. Write to support@wanderlog.com and include your account email, a link to the trip plan, screenshots or recordings, and your device operating system (help article, 1 March 2023).
  5. If the problem is a charge you did not want. The help center (4 March 2023) says: on the website go to wanderlog.com/settings/pro and click Unsubscribe; on iOS or Android open your profile, tap the three dots, choose Pro membership, tap Unsubscribe, then finish in your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Check your store's subscription list afterward (help article).

Questions about what happened to Wanderlog

Did Wanderlog shut down?

I found no sign of it. On 10 November 2026 wanderlog.com loaded, Apple's lookup showed version 2.228 released on 4 November 2026, and the Google Play listing showed an update on 2 November 2026. I found no outage or shutdown notice on its site, help center or blog.

What changed in the Wanderlog app recently?

Its App Store version history lists Trip Journal in May, fixes for Pro subscriptions and logouts in early September, and Import from anywhere, which builds an itinerary from a pasted link or shared reels, from late September. Version 2.228 on 4 November 2026 adds a visual refresh and map fixes.

Why does Wanderlog ask me to pay now?

I found no dated announcement of a new paywall. The app listings describe a free app with an optional Pro subscription, and the help center, dated March 2023, marks offline downloads and route optimization as Pro. Trustpilot reviewers from 2026 describe trials that convert to paid plans, so check the price screen before you accept a trial.

How do I fix Wanderlog when it will not load or log in?

Update the app, since recent release notes list fixes for logouts and slow-connection trip loading. If it still fails, email support@wanderlog.com with your account email, a link to the trip, screenshots or a recording and your device operating system, which is what its help center asks for.
