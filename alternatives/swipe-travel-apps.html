Swipe Travel Apps Compared in 2026

Updated 2 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only for now.

Swipe travel apps show you places one card at a time and let you keep the ones you like. Honua swipes through photos of places around the world, and its App Store listing is for iPhone. NomadSwipe swipes through real places in one city on Android, and saves your picks to a trip map.

In our search on 2 November 2026, most swipe-based place apps we found were listed for Apple devices, and few showed a Google Play listing. This page compares the two we could read on their store listings that day, and says where each one is the better pick.

NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in one city by swiping

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Each card shows the category, rating, distance and whether the place is open. Save a place to keep it, or skip it. Saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list can be sorted by rating, distance or A to Z.

  • Strength: a shortlist of places you chose yourself, before you have a plan.
  • Limit: it does not build an itinerary. You plan each day yourself around the map.
  • Limit: it is Android only for now. An iPhone version is coming soon.

The free plan includes 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip a month, 2 custom pins a month and a daily limit on saves. Premium (Monthly or Lifetime, bought in the app) includes up to 20 saved trips available offline.

Honua

Best for: browsing photos of places around the world and saving favorites on a map

Honua's App Store listing says: "With Honua you swipe through photos of locations all around the world." You add the ones you like to your favorites, and the listing says "Your favorites are saved on a map for you to visit anytime." It also says the app can help you prepare "for your next big trip", and that it is supported by photographers who share their pictures. The listing shows the price as Free and does not mention in-app purchases.

  • Strength: a map of the places you saved, on iPhone, at no listed price.
  • Limit: its App Store listing is for iPhone and Mac, and it does not mention Android.
  • Limit: it is built around photos of locations worldwide, so it is a weaker fit for shortlisting one city's places.

Which to choose

  • You use an iPhone and want to browse photos of places and keep favorites on a map: Honua.
  • You use Android and want to shortlist real places in one city, with rating, distance and opening status: NomadSwipe.
  • You want an AI itinerary with budgets and a journal: see our Stippl alternatives page.

FAQ

Is NomadSwipe free?

NomadSwipe has a free plan with 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip a month, 2 custom pins a month and a daily limit on saves. Premium (Monthly or Lifetime) is bought in the app, and its price is shown in Google Play before you pay.

Is Honua on Android?

Its App Store listing does not mention Android, so this page does not claim an Android version.

Is NomadSwipe on iPhone?

Not yet. NomadSwipe is Android only today, and an iPhone version is coming soon.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Premium includes up to 20 saved trips available offline.

Does NomadSwipe build an itinerary for me?

No. It gives you a shortlist of real places and a map for each trip, and you plan each day yourself around that map.
