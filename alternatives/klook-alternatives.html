What Klook does, so you know what you are replacing

Klook's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.klook) shows 10M+ downloads and an update on 13 November 2026. It lists tours, hotels, car rentals, tickets for movies and events, rewards tiers (KlookCash, coupons), a wishlist, and e-vouchers you can download for offline access. Its examples lean toward Asia and the Pacific: museums in Singapore, tour packages in South Korea, a Phuket vacation package and wine tasting in Melbourne. Its help centre (https://www.klook.com/faq/category-61-question-934/) has separate sections for ferries in Thailand, Malaysia and Vietnam, and for trains and buses in Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Mainland China, Hong Kong and Europe. Its cancellation article says each activity is either free-cancellation, conditional or no-cancellation, so the policy is set per activity.

That mix of activities and transport is what people usually miss when they leave. If you only need tours and tickets, the marketplaces below cover it. If you also want trains, ferries or car rental in the same app, look at Trip.com or KKday.

NomadSwipe: a different need, planning rather than booking

Best for: deciding what to do in a city, not buying tickets

Different need: plan the trip and save places on one map, not book tickets. NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. You pick a city, it shows real places one card at a time, and each card shows the category, rating, distance and opening status. You swipe to skip or save; saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z. It works in any city worldwide.

  • Strength: it answers "what is worth doing here" before you open a ticket marketplace, and the places you save sit on one map.
  • Limit: it does not sell tickets, tours, hotels or transport. Pair it with a booking app for anything that needs a voucher.
  • Limit: Android only today; iPhone coming soon.

The plan without Premium is limited: 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip a month, 2 custom pins a month and a daily limit on saves. Premium comes as a Monthly or Lifetime plan and is optional. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online. For a wider list of swipe-style apps see swipe travel apps compared.

GetYourGuide

Best for: tours, day trips and tickets to big landmarks

GetYourGuide's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.getyourguide.android) shows 10M+ downloads and an update on 21 November 2026. It says you can find over 100,000 experiences, and names the Colosseum, Eiffel Tower, London Eye and Sagrada Familia among its tickets. It lists reserve now and pay later, instant confirmation, offline tickets, 24/7 customer service by email, phone and WhatsApp, and free cancellation up to 24 hours before the activity for most bookings. It does not mention trains, ferries or car rental. More in apps like GetYourGuide.

  • Strength: the cancellation window and the pay-later option are stated in the listing itself.
  • Limit: "most bookings" is not all of them; check the activity page.

Viator

Best for: the biggest catalogue of tours and activities

Viator's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.viator.mobile.android) says one app holds more than 300,000 travel experiences, tours and tickets. It shows 5M+ downloads and an update on 19 November 2026. It lists free cancellation up to 24 hours in advance, reserve now and pay later, installments with Klarna, payment by credit card, PayPal or Apple Pay, wishlists, tickets offline, and reviews from Tripadvisor and Viator travellers. Reliability questions are covered in is Viator reliable, and the two big marketplaces are set against each other in Viator or GetYourGuide.

  • Strength: installments with Klarna and pay-later are both in the listing.
  • Limit: tours and tickets only; no hotels or transport in its listing.

Tiqets

Best for: museum and attraction tickets

Tiqets' Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tiqets.tiqetsapp) says it is present in over 500 destinations, sells tickets for attractions, guided tours, skip-the-line entries and boat tours, and also an audio guide of a city. It says tickets are stored offline on your phone. It shows 1M+ downloads and an update on 12 November 2026. The listing does not state a cancellation policy, so I do not give one.

  • Strength: a narrow focus on attractions, with offline tickets.
  • Limit: the listing says nothing about hotels or transport.

KKday

Best for: tickets plus transfers, SIM cards and Wi-Fi

KKday's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kkday.member) calls it a one-stop travel companion with tickets, private car services, airport transfers, Wi-Fi, SIM cards, accommodation and outdoor activities. It lists e-tickets, and payment by Visa, MasterCard, JCB, UnionPay, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay, LINE Pay, Octopus, PayMe and more. It shows 1M+ downloads, an update on 20 November 2026 and the label "Contains ads". The listing does not say which regions it covers, so I make no regional claim.

  • Strength: the closest listing to Klook's mix of tickets and getting around, with a long list of payment methods.
  • Limit: no cancellation policy in the listing.

Trip.com

Best for: flights and hotels, with tour tickets on the side

Trip.com's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ctrip.english) shows 50M+ downloads and an update on 22 November 2026. It leads with flights and hotels, then lists car hire, tour tickets, trains, eSIM and travel insurance. It names credit and debit cards, PayPal, Google Pay and Apple Pay, and a Trip Coins travel credit for future trips.

  • Strength: flights, hotels, trains and tickets in one account.
  • Limit: tours are one item in a long list, not the focus.

Tripadvisor

Best for: reading reviews before you book

Tripadvisor's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tripadvisor.tripadvisor) shows 100M+ downloads and an update on 19 November 2026. It says you can book from over 400,000 experiences and 600,000 hotels and get 5 percent back in Trip Cash, stay flexible with free cancellation on most experiences, save favourite hotels, restaurants and experiences, and browse traveller reviews. It carries the label "Contains ads". For the wider picture see apps like Tripadvisor.

  • Strength: reviews and a save list sit in the same app as the booking.
  • Limit: "most experiences" again, not all.

Airbnb Experiences

Best for: activities run by local hosts, alongside a stay

Airbnb's Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.airbnb.android) shows 100M+ downloads and an update on 23 November 2026. It says you can find thousands of experiences hosted by locals, such as food tours, nature walks, classes and tickets to attractions, next to homes, hotels and services such as car rentals, which it says are handled by a third party. We did not read Airbnb's refund terms for this article.

  • Strength: experiences hosted by locals, in the app you may already use for stays.
  • Limit: a smaller part of a listing that is mostly about stays.

FAQ

What is an app similar to Klook?

Apps that also sell tours, tickets and activities: GetYourGuide, Viator, Tiqets, KKday, Trip.com, Tripadvisor and Airbnb Experiences. Klook's Google Play listing also names hotels, car rentals and trains, buses and ferries; Trip.com, KKday and Airbnb also list car or transport services.

Is NomadSwipe a booking app?

No. I built NomadSwipe, an Android app for choosing real places in a city by swiping, saving them and seeing them on one map. It does not sell tickets, tours or hotels, so it replaces Klook only for the planning step.

Do apps like Klook offer free cancellation?

It depends on the app and the activity. Klook's help article describes free, conditional and no-cancellation activities. GetYourGuide's listing says free cancellation up to 24 hours before for most bookings, Viator's says up to 24 hours in advance, and Tripadvisor's says free cancellation on most experiences. Check the activity page before paying.

Which app similar to Klook lets me pay in installments?

Viator's Google Play listing lists paying in installments with Klarna and reserve now, pay later. GetYourGuide's listing also lists reserve now, pay later. We did not see installments on the other listings.
