What GPSmyCity does

The baseline: self-guided walks with GPS and offline maps

GPSmyCity's Google Play listing describes "thousands of self-guided walking tours, travel articles, and offline city maps in 1,800+ cities", and says the walks and articles work offline without a data plan. Its website reports over 7,000 self-guided walks, custom walks in 670+ cities, turn-by-turn walking directions and a detailed walk route map, and names iOS and Android.

The listing says the app is free to download, shows In-app purchases, and says a small payment is needed for the walk route maps and turn-by-turn navigation. The amount is not in the text we read, so this article does not state one. The listing also says you can create your own walking tour from the attractions that interest you. The parts of the listing and site we could read do not describe an audio narrator. We read no user reviews for GPSmyCity this run, so this article lists no complaints about it.

NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing real places in a city by swiping

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner. You pick a city and swipe through real places one card at a time: right to save, left to skip. Each card shows the category, rating, distance and opening status. Saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z. The Lisbon sample trip below has 16 places, all picked by swiping.

  • Strength: you do not have to find the places first. The deck suggests them, and you keep the ones you choose.
  • Limit: no turn-by-turn navigation and no audio guide. Directions, website and call are one tap from a saved place.
  • Limit: no walking tour routes.
  • Limit: it does not import saves from Instagram, TikTok or other apps. Its listed features are swipe, save, map and go.
  • Limit: Android only today; iPhone coming soon.

The free plan includes 3 trips, 2 destination searches a month, 2 custom pins a month and a limited number of swipes. Premium comes as Monthly or Lifetime. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline, per NomadSwipe's Play listing (OFFLINE section), and its website lists offline trips on both plans.

VoiceMap

Best for: narrated walks, drives and boat rides

VoiceMap's Google Play listing describes GPS audio walks, cycles, drives and boat rides in over 600 destinations. It says a downloaded tour works offline and includes an offline map, and it describes Virtual Playback, which turns a tour into a podcast you can play at home. The listing shows 100K+ downloads and an update on 26 October 2026.

Its website says the app is free to download and that tours are bought individually, with credit passes for discounted access. Its listing says over 2,100 free and paid tours in 80 countries, and its website counter shows 2,353 tours in 87 countries. It names Android and iOS.

  • Strength: per its website, the app plays the narration automatically when you reach the right place, after you tap Start.
  • Limit: the listing describes over 2,100 free and paid tours, so some tours cost money. We did not confirm a per-tour price.
  • Limit: the tours are made by local publishers and guides. You do not choose the places yourself.

Tripomatic

Best for: a day-by-day itinerary with offline maps

Tripomatic's Google Play listing describes a day-by-day itinerary, estimated travel times and walking distances between stops, and AI help with planning. It lists 1M+ downloads and an update on 22 October 2026. The Sygic Travel web address now redirects to tripomatic.com, so our Sygic Travel alternatives page covers the same product line.

  • Strength: the day-by-day itinerary and the offline maps are both in the same app, per its listing.
  • Limit: the listing says offline use needs Premium, which also gives unlimited offline map downloads and walking navigation. We did not read the Premium price.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary with reservations and a map of stops

Wanderlog's Google Play listing calls it a "completely free travel app" for planning trips. It says you can add unlimited stops and view places to visit on a map. It also says "All your holiday plans are automatically stored offline", and marks "Access your trip plans offline (Pro)". The listing shows 1M+ downloads and an update on 23 October 2026. We did not read Pro prices this run. For more on Wanderlog, see our Wanderlog alternatives page.

  • Limit: the listing marks "Access your trip plans offline (Pro)" next to its offline claim, and we did not read the Pro price.

Google Maps

Best for: custom lists of saved places in the app you already have

Google Maps' Google Play listing says you can create custom lists of your favorite saved places and share them with others, and that offline maps help in areas with bad signal. The listing shows 10B+ downloads and an update on 30 October 2026. The listing text we read does not mention a paid tier, and it does not describe guided walks.

  • Limit: saving and sharing lists is not the same as choosing places from a deck of suggestions.

Which to choose

  • You want narrated walks you start with a tap: VoiceMap.
  • You want to choose the places yourself, on Android: NomadSwipe.
  • You want walking routes with turn-by-turn directions and offline maps: GPSmyCity.
  • You want a day-by-day itinerary with offline maps: Tripomatic.
  • You want an itinerary with reservations and a map of stops: Wanderlog.
  • You want lists in the Google Maps app you already use: Google Maps.

FAQ

Is GPSmyCity free?

The Google Play listing says the app is free to download, shows In-app purchases, and says a small payment is needed for the walk route maps and turn-by-turn navigation. The amount is not in the text we read, so check the listing before you pay.

Does GPSmyCity work offline?

Its listing says the walking tours and articles run offline without a data plan. Turn-by-turn navigation is part of the payment its listing describes.

Does VoiceMap work offline?

Yes, once a tour is downloaded. Its listing says a downloaded tour works offline and includes an offline map.

Can I choose places by swiping instead of building a walking tour?

That is what NomadSwipe does on Android: you swipe through real places in a city, save the ones you like and see them on a map. It has no walking tour routes and no turn-by-turn navigation.

Is NomadSwipe available on iPhone?

Not yet. NomadSwipe is Android only today, and iPhone is coming soon.

Does NomadSwipe import saves from Instagram or TikTok?

No. Its listed features are swipe, save, map and go, and they do not include importing saves from other apps.
