Bruges in 2 days: 16 stops that fit the stored opening hours

A 2-day Bruges itinerary built from the 464 places in the NomadSwipe app's Bruges deck. Every stop is within 1.1 km of the Markt, every suggested start time falls inside the stored opening hours, and the page tells you which days of the week the plan works.

Updated 9 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Bruges place data, read on 4 November 2026. The stored rows were last updated on 9 May 2026, so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Markt and the east: the Belfry, the Provincial Palace, the Torture Museum, lunch on Vlamingstraat, then the Adornes Domain and the Kantcentrum, dinner at De Pepermolen and a drink at Le Trappiste.
  • Day 2, Dijver and the south: the Groeninge Museum, Musea Sculpta, lunch at De Gastro, tea at Carpe Diem, Minnewater Park, dinner at ONE Restaurant and a rooftop drink.
  • Days of the week: day one works Monday, Thursday and Saturday. Day two works Thursday to Sunday. Four back-to-back pairings fit both (see the weekday table).
  • Distance: each day adds up to about 2.4 km by straight line between stored coordinates, so both days are on foot. These are not walking times.

A small core, and why that shapes the plan

The Bruges deck is concentrated around one square. Using a reference point I set at the Markt, 148 of the 174 cards within 3 km sit within 1 km of it. Wikipedia describes the Markt as the central square of Bruges, covering about 1 hectare, with the 13th-century Belfry on its south side (Wikipedia), and describes the historic city centre as an oval of about 430 hectares and a UNESCO World Heritage Site (Wikipedia). With that little ground to cover, the problem is not distance. It is opening days, which is why most of this page is about them.

  • Prices are left out. I did not source ticket prices from official pages this run, so check each place's own website the day you go.
  • The big landmarks are not cards. I searched the deck for a church, basilica, begijnhof, cathedral, windmill, canal and boat tour, and for the Burg and Rozenhoedkaai in addresses. None of those returned a landmark card (details in the findings). The only landmark-style card on the Markt is the historic-centre card, which I use to start day one.
  • Add your own pins. Anything you want that the deck lacks, such as a canal boat or a church, is yours to add on the map yourself.

Day 1: Markt, the Belfry and the east side

Day one stays near the square. By straight line between stored coordinates, the nine stops are within 0.9 km of each other, the farthest from the Markt reference point is 0.76 km away, and going stop to stop adds up to about 2.4 km. The longest single leg, about 0.7 km, is from lunch to the Adornes Domain. Times below are my suggested start times.

  1. 9:00, Historic centre of Brugge (Bruges): stored as a historic site, with 18,121 stored reviews and a 4.8 rating, the most-reviewed card in this plan. Its stored address reads "Grote Markt, 2000 Anvers", which is an Antwerp postcode, but its stored coordinates are on the Markt, 0.06 km from my reference point. I treat the address as a data quirk. It has no stored hours. Start here, on the square.
  2. 9:15, Belfry of Bruges: stored as a historic site, open daily 9 AM to 8 PM as stored. Wikipedia says the belfry was added to the Markt around 1240, stands 83 metres tall, leans 87 centimetres to the east, and has a staircase of 366 steps that the public can climb for an entry fee (Wikipedia). Going early is my choice, not a sourced queue time.
  3. 10:30, Provincial Palace: stored as a historic site at Markt 3, 0.09 km from the Belfry by stored coordinates. Stored as closed on Tuesdays and open 10:30 AM to 6 PM on the other days.
  4. 11:00, Torture Museum Oude Steen Brugge: stored as a museum, on Wollestraat, 4.3 from 1,545 reviews. Open daily 10:30 AM to 6:30 PM as stored.
  5. 12:30, Vlaminck '14: stored as a restaurant, at Vlamingstraat 14, 4.5 from 660 reviews, open daily from noon to 10 PM as stored. A swap if you want a different lunch: Ter Halle Grill Restaurant on the Eiermarkt, 4.7 from 317 reviews, closed Sunday and open only from 6 PM on Monday.
  6. 14:00, Adornes Domain: stored as a museum, 4.4 from 595 reviews, open Monday to Thursday and Saturday from 10 AM to 5 PM, closed Friday and Sunday as stored. It is the plan's furthest stop to the east.
  7. 15:30, Kantcentrum vzw: stored as a museum, at Balstraat 16, 0.08 km from the Adornes Domain by stored coordinates. Closed Sunday, open 9:30 AM to 5 PM the other days as stored, so keep the 15:30 start.
  8. 18:00, De Pepermolen: stored as a restaurant, 4.6 from 645 reviews, open evenings from 6 PM to 9:15 PM and closed Wednesday as stored. About 0.4 km from the Kantcentrum.
  9. 20:00, Le Trappiste: stored as a bar, 4.7 from 1,516 reviews, the highest-rated bar card in the deck with at least 1,000 stored reviews. Open Monday to Thursday 6 PM to midnight and Friday and Saturday 5 PM to 1 AM, closed Sunday as stored.

Day 2: Dijver, Minnewater and the south

Day two moves south from the square. The seven stops are within 0.9 km of each other, the farthest from the Markt reference point is 1.09 km, and going stop to stop adds up to about 2.4 km. The legs run from 0.12 km (Minnewater Park to ONE Restaurant) to 0.67 km (dinner to the rooftop bar). Times are again my suggested start times.

  1. 10:00, Groeninge Museum: stored as a museum, at Dijver 12, 4.3 from 1,744 reviews, open 10 AM to 6 PM and closed Monday as stored. Wikipedia says it holds Flemish and Belgian painting across six centuries, from Jan van Eyck to Marcel Broodthaers, including van Eyck's Madonna with Canon van der Paele of 1436 (Wikipedia).
  2. 11:30, Musea Sculpta: stored as a museum, at Mariastraat 10, 4.8 from 158 reviews, 0.27 km from the Groeninge Museum by stored coordinates. Closed Tuesday as stored. I read no page describing it this run, so I only repeat the stored values.
  3. 12:30, De Gastro: stored as a restaurant, at Braambergstraat 6, 4.6 from 2,811 reviews. Lunch hours are noon to 3 PM and it is closed Tuesday as stored.
  4. 15:00, Carpe Diem Bakery & Tea-room: stored as a bakery, at Wijngaardstraat 8, 4.3 from 861 reviews. Open 7 AM to 6 PM, closed Monday and Tuesday as stored. An afternoon break, 0.6 km from lunch.
  5. 16:00, Minnewater Park: stored as a park, at Minnewater 1, 0.31 km from the tea room. It has a 4.4 rating from only 11 reviews, so treat that number lightly. It has no stored hours.
  6. 18:00, ONE Restaurant: stored as a restaurant, 4.7 from 1,196 reviews, 0.12 km from the park. Dinner is 6 PM to 9 PM, and it is closed Monday and Tuesday as stored.
  7. 20:00, ODA Rooftop Bar: stored as a bar, at Steenstraat 96, 4.8 from 301 reviews, the highest-rated bar card in the Bruges deck with at least 300 stored reviews. Open noon to midnight, closed Tuesday and Wednesday as stored. It is the one place on this list that is a longer hop, 0.67 km back toward the centre.

What I left out, and why

  • Four tourist museums below 4.0. Choco-Story (3.6, 3,945 reviews), the Frietmuseum (3.3, 1,343), XPO Salvador Dalí (3.6, 674) and the Diamond Museum (3.4, 402) are all stored within 0.8 km of the Markt. Of the 10 museum cards within 3.5 km, these are the four under 4.0. If one is why you came, swap it in for the Kantcentrum or Musea Sculpta.
  • Brugse Vrije. It is stored as a museum with a 4.2 rating from 281 reviews, but its stored hours show it open on Saturday and Sunday only. That looks wrong for a museum next to the Markt, so I left it out rather than build a day around it.
  • The Gunpowder Tower and Boudewijn Seapark. The tower (3.7 from 98 reviews, 1.19 km from the reference) and the Seapark (3.1 from 439 reviews, 2.97 km) are two more cards under 4.0 that I looked at and left out.
  • Hotels and rentals. 115 of the 464 cards are hotels or vacation rentals, so I skipped them for a day plan.

One day or three days in Bruges instead of two

This page plans two days, and I did not build other lengths. With one day, take day one: nine stops within 0.9 km of each other, about 2.4 km by straight line, which works on Monday, Thursday and Saturday by the stored hours (see the weekday table). If the weekday is wrong, swap in the day-two stops that are open, and check each one first. With three days, I have no routed third day to offer. The deck has 56 sight cards in all, only 19 of them within 1 km of the Markt, and the four tourist museums I left out score below 4.0 (see what I left out). The honest way to stretch two days is to swap those museums in and add your own pins for a canal boat or a church, as the landmark search found no cards for them.

Choosing between Bruges and another town? This page has no Ghent data. I have not run the same count on a Ghent deck, so I will not say which of the two is better for two days. The Bruges numbers above (a core of 148 cards within 1 km of the Markt) are what you can compare against whatever you find for Ghent.

Getting around between the stops

  • On foot both days. The farthest pair of stops on either day is under 0.9 km apart by straight line, which is why I did not add transport. Streets and canals make the real walk longer than the straight line.
  • The one longer hop: day two ends with the 0.67 km leg from dinner back toward the Markt, and day one has the 0.7 km leg east to the Adornes Domain.
  • Transport and fares: I left them out because I did not read an official page for them this run.
  • In the app: each card shows its distance, and the list sorts by rating, distance or A to Z.

Questions about this Bruges plan

Can I follow this Bruges plan if I arrive on a Tuesday?

Do day one and skip one stop. By the stored hours, the Provincial Palace is the only day-one stop closed on Tuesday. Day two is the weak one on a Tuesday: Musea Sculpta, De Gastro, Carpe Diem, ONE Restaurant and ODA Rooftop Bar are all stored as closed. The stored hours are from 9 May 2026, so confirm them before you go.

Which stops are still open on a Sunday?

All of day two works on a Sunday as stored. Day one loses three stops on a Sunday: Adornes Domain, the Kantcentrum and Le Trappiste are stored as closed. The Belfry, the Torture Museum and Vlaminck '14 are stored as open every day.

Why are Choco-Story and the Frietmuseum not in the plan?

They are well-known museum cards but their stored ratings are below the 4.0 line I used: Choco-Story is 3.6 from 3,945 reviews and the Frietmuseum is 3.3 from 1,343. The XPO Salvador Dalí card is 3.6 and the Diamond Museum card is 3.4. If a museum on that list is the reason you came, add it as a swap.

How were the 16 stops chosen?

I picked cards within 1.1 km of a reference point at the Markt with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 30 stored reviews, then checked every suggested start time against the stored opening hours. Minnewater Park is the exception, with 11 reviews, and the historic-centre card has no stored hours.

Can I spend one day or three days in Bruges instead of two?

One day works: day one is a complete plan of nine stops, but only on Monday, Thursday or Saturday by the stored hours. For three days, this page has no routed third day. The deck has 56 sight cards in all, 19 within 1 km of the Markt, and the four tourist museums I left out score below 4.0, so a third day would rest on weaker data.

Is 2 days in Ghent or Bruges better?

I cannot say from this page. It has Bruges data only, and I have not run the same count on a Ghent deck, so there is no Ghent number to compare. What the Bruges data shows is a small core: 148 of the 174 cards within 3 km sit within 1 km of the Markt, and each day of this plan adds up to about 2.4 km by straight line. Whether that suits you is a question of taste, not something these ratings decide.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app's place data on 4 November 2026 with read-only queries. Bruges is stored as two destination rows: Brugge with 464 cards and Bruges with none. I used Brugge. Its 464 cards share 462 distinct titles (case-insensitive). I measured distance in a straight line from a reference point that I set at the Markt (51.2093 N, 3.2247 E), using each card's stored coordinates. All 464 rows were last updated on 9 May 2026.

  • Of the 464 cards, 350 have a stored rating, and 259 of those rated cards are rated 4.0 or higher (74%). 113 are rated 4.5 or higher. The median stored rating is 4.3.
  • I grouped the cards by stored category into buckets I defined myself: 224 restaurants and food places, 115 hotels and rentals, 29 bars and breweries, 16 cafes and bakeries, 56 sights (museums, attractions, galleries, historic sites, parks, a zoo and similar), and 24 others such as sports, spas and theatres. The bucket totals sum to 464. Only 56 cards are sights, and 19 of those sit within 1 km of the reference point.
  • 174 of the 464 cards sit within 3 km of the reference point, and 148 of those are within 1 km. 282 cards (61%) sit more than 5 km away, 62 more than 50 km away, and 51 more than 1,000 km away. The eight most-reviewed of the 51 are in the United States and Mexico, for example a Las Vegas Strip card and a resort in Quintana Roo. Check a card's address before you save it.
  • 291 of the 464 cards have stored weekly hours. Of those, 84 show Monday as closed (29%), 76 show Tuesday as closed (26%), 61 show Wednesday as closed (21%) and 49 show Sunday as closed (17%). Tuesday is almost as closed as Monday.
  • All 17 museum cards have stored hours, and 9 of them show Monday as closed. Of the 187 restaurant cards with stored hours, 49 are closed Monday, 48 Tuesday, 47 Wednesday and 24 Sunday.
  • Landmark search: card titles and addresses were searched for church, kerk, basilica, begijn, beguinage, cathedral, burg, windmill, molen, canal, boat, lake of love, hospital, halve maan, grote markt, jan van eyck, rozenhoedkaai and simon stevin. No church, basilica, begijnhof, cathedral, windmill, canal or boat card came back. The matches were restaurants and bars whose names or addresses contain one of the terms (for example De Pepermolen and Bistro Zevenkerken), a gallery in Damme, and the historic-centre card.
  • The historic-centre card is the most-reviewed card within 5 km of the reference point, at 18,121 stored reviews and 4.8. Its stored address has an Antwerp postcode while its coordinates sit on the Markt.
  • Four cards have stored addresses on the Eiermarkt (Aux Merveilleux de Fred, Ter Halle Grill Restaurant, Seafoodbar Belge and a waffle and ice cream shop), all within 0.13 km of the reference point.
  • I chose the 16 stops by hand: cards within 1.1 km of the reference point with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 30 stored reviews, chosen to mix categories, keep each day in one area and fit the stored opening days. The exceptions are Minnewater Park (11 reviews) and the historic-centre card (no stored hours). They are three historic sites, five museums, one park, four restaurants, one bakery and two bars. They are not simply the 16 highest-rated cards.
  • Using straight-line distance between stored coordinates, day one totals about 2.4 km (longest leg 0.70 km) and day two about 2.4 km (longest leg 0.67 km). These are not walking times.

Limits: the ratings, review counts and hours are stored values from 9 May 2026 that may have changed since, and the stored hours for the Brugse Vrije look wrong. The suggested start times are mine and are checked only against stored hours. I left out ticket prices, transport fares and walking times, and I did not copy any third-party review text.
