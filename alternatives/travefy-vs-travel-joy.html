Travefy vs Travel Joy: Which Travel Advisor Software Fits?

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), which appears once below and is not a rival to either product. Facts about Travefy and TravelJoy checked on 14 November 2026. Updated 14 November 2026.

Travefy vs Travel Joy (the product spells itself TravelJoy): both are software for professional travel advisors, not trip apps for travelers. Travefy leans on itinerary and client-app depth plus 200+ supplier connections; TravelJoy starts cheaper, publishes its card fees and adds cruise booking. Neither helps you pick places to visit.

Key facts

  • Both are built for advisors who sell trips to clients. Travefy's pricing page lists Core at $39 a month billed annually and Premium at $59; TravelJoy's pricing page lists Starter at $19 a month and Pro at $32 a month billed annually.
  • Trials differ: Travefy says 10 days, TravelJoy says 7 days with no credit card.
  • TravelJoy publishes its payment fees (Pro: 3.5% plus 30 cents on cards, 1.5% on bank transfers). I found no processing fee on the Travefy pages I read.
  • On Google Play on 14 November 2026, Travefy's client app Trip Plans showed 4.7 from 2.89K reviews and 100K+ downloads; TravelJoy's Itineraries app showed 3.7 from 25 reviews and 10K+.
  • Only TravelJoy's pages mention cruise booking under a host agency's IATA number; only Travefy's list a website and landing page builder.

Six questions that decide it

I worked through the pages in the order I would ask these, doing the sums where the pages give numbers. Each step names the product the pages favour.

  1. What is the monthly budget for one seat? Travefy Core is $39 a month billed annually. TravelJoy Pro is $32 billed annually, and TravelJoy's features FAQ gives $39 billed monthly, so the same $39 buys different things depending on how you pay. TravelJoy Starter at $19 is the lowest entry on either site, but it caps you at 12 trips a year. TravelJoy on price, if you can live with Starter's limits.
  2. Will you take card payments through the software? TravelJoy states its rates. My arithmetic on a $5,000 client payment: Pro costs $175.30 by card ($5,000 x 3.5% plus 30 cents) or $75 by bank transfer; Starter costs $250.30 by card or $150 by bank transfer. I found no equivalent figure for Travefy, which lists invoices and card authorization forms but not a rate, so ask Travefy before you compare. TravelJoy, on what is published.
  3. Do you need a team? Travefy states $20 a month for each added seat on annual Core. TravelJoy Pro lists team members as an add-on without a price on the pages I read, and Starter has none. Travefy, on what is stated.
  4. Is it mostly group trips? TravelJoy puts group bookings in Pro and names group trips as a headline feature. Travefy lists group travel in its itinerary builder. Roughly even; pick on the demo.
  5. Is cruise your main product? Both have cruise libraries. Travefy cites 80+ cruise lines. TravelJoy's cruise page describes side-by-side sailings in one proposal and booking under your host's IATA, but says existing members join a waitlist for booking access. Travefy for content today; TravelJoy if the booking route matters and you can wait.
  6. How much does the client app matter? Travefy's Trip Plans has the larger Play footprint and the higher rating, and its site promotes live flight updates and offline mode. TravelJoy's app listing also names live flight details and offline viewing, but its listing has only 25 reviews and some of those on the first page say they could not open an itinerary. Travefy, on this evidence.

Where the pages do not line up

TravelJoy's two pages disagree on its starting price. Its features page FAQ says pricing starts at $32 a month billed annually, while its pricing page lists Starter at $19. I treated the pricing page as the current one, but a buyer should confirm which plan is still on sale. The Premium count disagrees too: the Premium page lists "3 free Pro accounts" in its feature list, and its FAQ describes two Pro team accounts plus one assistant account, which looks like the same three seats worded two ways.

Travefy's pages are more consistent but leave gaps. The pricing page says a monthly option exists without a figure in the text I read, and its home page quotes "Trusted by 50,000+" travel brands and a "#1" ranking from advisors, which are Travefy's own claims, not something I could check. Ratings need care as well: 4.7 from 2.89K reviews and 3.7 from 25 reviews are not the same evidence, and Travefy's Play description is short on features its own site calls out, such as live flight updates.

What each does better

Travefy

Travefy is the more connected tool on paper. Its itinerary page lists AI Smart Import, 200+ supplier integrations, a client app under your own logo and templates you can copy from other advisors, and its CRM page adds secure document storage and forms that update client profiles. The Agency plan lists a shared template library, supplier management and access to the CRM API. If your day is mostly building polished itineraries and keeping client records, it is built for that. For the wider market, see Travefy alternatives.

TravelJoy

TravelJoy is the cheaper way in, and it is the more open about costs: fee rates for cards and bank transfers sit on the pricing page. Its AI pages describe Copilot drafting itineraries and Magic Email turning a forwarded supplier quote into a proposal, and Premium adds SMS, Zapier and email campaigns for larger operations. If you want to start small, move money inside the platform and later add cruise booking, it is the better-priced start. TravelJoy's own pages also say it does not use client data to train public AI models.

The short version

  • Travefy suits advisors who want deep itinerary tools, supplier connections, a website builder and a strong client app; TravelJoy suits advisors who want a lower start, published payment fees and cruise booking.
  • Travefy Core is $39 a month billed annually; TravelJoy runs from $19 (Starter, 12 trips a year) to $32 (Pro, billed annually).
  • The Travefy client app has the larger and higher-rated Play listing, with very different sample sizes.
  • I started neither trial, so confirm current prices, seat costs and processing fees with each company before you commit.

FAQ

Is Travel Joy the same as TravelJoy?

In this comparison, yes. The product that sits next to Travefy is TravelJoy at traveljoy.com, an all-in-one platform for travel advisors with a CRM, itinerary builder and payments. It is spelled as one word on its own site. I found no other Travel Joy product that competes with Travefy.

Is Travefy or TravelJoy cheaper?

TravelJoy starts lower. Its pricing page lists Starter at $19 a month for 12 trips a year and Pro at $32 a month billed annually, while Travefy's pricing page lists Core at $39 a month billed annually. The two are not like for like: TravelJoy's Starter has no AI, group bookings or team members, and TravelJoy adds card-processing fees that I did not find stated on Travefy's pricing page.

Do Travefy and TravelJoy both have a mobile app for clients?

Yes. Travefy's clients use Trip Plans, which showed 4.7 from 2.89K reviews and 100K+ downloads on Google Play on 14 November 2026. TravelJoy's clients use Itineraries by TravelJoy, which showed 3.7 from 25 reviews and 10K+ downloads. The two listings are very different sample sizes, so the ratings are not directly comparable.

Which one handles payments?

Both offer payment features. TravelJoy lists payments and authorizations and publishes its fees: on Pro, cards cost 3.5% plus 30 cents and bank transfers 1.5%; on Starter, 5% plus 30 cents and 3%. Travefy lists invoices with payment schedules and credit card authorization forms, and I found no processing fee stated on the pages I read.

It has no client management, invoices or proposals. It only overlaps with the research step before an itinerary exists.

Method, sample and limits

"Travel Joy" is ambiguous, so I first checked a Google results page for the query, which pointed to traveljoy.com, and then confirmed on that site that it is a platform for travel advisors, the same buyers Travefy serves. On 14 November 2026 the pages read were the Travefy home, pricing, itinerary, CRM and Trip Plans pages, the Google Play listing for Trip Plans, the TravelJoy home, pricing, features, Premium, AI and cruises pages, and the Google Play listing for Itineraries by TravelJoy, all with a headless browser signed out. Neither product was installed or trialled, so this is what the pages say, not how the software behaves. I did not read help centers or terms, so seat prices, monthly Travefy pricing and Travefy processing fees are left out where the pages did not state them. The $175.30 and $75 payment figures are my own arithmetic on the stated TravelJoy rates for an example $5,000 payment, not quotes from either company. Prices vary by region and change often; Play ratings and downloads are Google Play's figures on that day. NomadSwipe facts come from its Google Play listing.
