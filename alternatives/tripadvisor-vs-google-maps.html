Tripadvisor vs Google Maps: Which Is Better for Planning a Trip?

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), which appears once below as a third option. Facts about Tripadvisor and Google Maps checked on 14 November 2026. Updated 14 November 2026.

Tripadvisor vs Google Maps for trip planning: pick Tripadvisor for traveler reviews, booking and an itinerary planner built on your saves; pick Google Maps for your own saved lists on a map, shareable and co-editable, plus offline map areas and navigation. Both are free to download.

Key facts

  • On Google Play on 14 November 2026, Tripadvisor showed 4.5 from 1.47M reviews and 100M+ downloads; Google Maps showed 3.4 from 19.6M reviews and 10B+.
  • Tripadvisor's App Store page shows Free; Google Maps's App Store page shows Free with in-app purchases. Both Play pages say the app contains ads.
  • Only Tripadvisor describes an itinerary planner with an AI trip builder that works from your saves. I found no itinerary builder in the Google Maps pages I read.
  • Only Google Maps describes lists you can share by link and let others edit, and offline map areas. I found no offline or sharing statement on the Tripadvisor pages I could read.
  • Reviews work differently: Tripadvisor's help page says a review cannot be edited, only deleted and resubmitted; Google's help page says you can edit yours.

Five questions that decide it

I built this walk-through from the pages above, in the order I would ask the questions. Each step names the app the pages favour.

  1. Whose opinions do you want? Tripadvisor's Play listing leads with traveler reviews, tips from a travel community and AI summaries. Google Maps's Play listing offers reviews and photos too, across any business on the map, not only travel. If the question is "is this museum worth a morning", Tripadvisor is the one built for it. If it is "is this cafe open and busy now", Google Maps.
  2. Do you want to book in the same app? Both list booking. Tripadvisor names hotels, and its App Store page adds tours and attraction tickets. Google Maps lists reservations, takeout and hotel booking. On what is written, Tripadvisor for tours, even for the rest.
  3. Do you want to share the shortlist? The Google Maps lists article covers a view link, an edit invitation and following someone else's list. I could not read any Tripadvisor page on sharing saves. Google Maps, on what is stated.
  4. Will you have a signal? Google's offline maps article says to download an area while connected and signed in, and warns that transit, bicycling and walking directions are unavailable offline. Tripadvisor's pages gave me nothing on offline use, so check the app before a flight. Google Maps, with that caveat.
  5. Do you want something to draft the days for you? Tripadvisor's Play listing describes a personal itinerary planner with an AI trip builder that works from your saves. Google Maps's pages describe saving, sharing and scheduled directions, not an itinerary. Tripadvisor, if you want a draft written for you.

Where the two pages overlap, and where they do not

Both apps save places, show reviews and let you book something, so the gap is in what they are built around. Tripadvisor's home page calls it a travel guidance platform backed by more than a billion reviews and opinions. Google Maps's Play listing says it covers over 250 million businesses and places. One is a travel-only review library; the other is a general map where travel is one use.

Ratings need care too. The Google Maps Play page shows 3.4 from 19.6M reviews at the top and 3.3 from 18.9M in the reviews section, and the U.S. App Store shows 4.7 from 7.5M ratings, so even one app gets different numbers in different places. A rating on Google Play and a rating on the App Store are not the same sample, and neither tells you whether the planning features suit your trip. Tripadvisor's own help center page also carries a 2021 copyright line, so its review rules may be older than the app listing.

What each does better

Tripadvisor

Tripadvisor is better when the hard part is deciding what is worth your time and you want other travelers to weigh in. It combines saves, an AI-built itinerary, hotel and experience booking, and review summaries in one app. If you already read Tripadvisor before every trip, its planner keeps that research in one place. For the narrower picture of apps that do one slice of this, see Tripadvisor alternatives.

Google Maps

Google Maps is better when the hard part is getting around and sharing the plan. Its lists sit on the same map you navigate with, you can hand a list to someone with a link and let them edit it, and an offline area helps with a weak signal. It is not an itinerary tool, though, and offline directions have the limits above. FAQ

Is Tripadvisor or Google Maps better for planning a trip?

It depends on the job. Tripadvisor is the better fit if you want traveler reviews, booking and an itinerary planner that builds on the places you save. Google Maps is the better fit if you want your own saved lists on a map, shared or co-edited with others, offline map areas and navigation. I did not install either app, so this is based on what their own pages say.

Are Tripadvisor and Google Maps free?

Both are free to download. The App Store shows Tripadvisor as Free, and its description says it is free to join. The App Store shows Google Maps as Free with in-app purchases. Both Google Play pages say the app contains ads. Hotel and tour bookings are a separate matter and carry their own prices.

Which has better reviews, Tripadvisor or Google Maps?

Neither page lets me say one is more accurate. Tripadvisor is built around traveler reviews and offers AI summaries of them. Google Maps shows reviews and photos for any place it lists, and its help page says reviews are public, cannot be anonymous and are moderated. Tripadvisor's help page says a review cannot be edited, only deleted and resubmitted, while Google Maps lets you edit yours.

Does Google Maps work offline for a trip?

Partly. Google's help page says you can download an area and use it offline, and driving directions work offline if the whole route is inside the area. Transit, bicycling and walking directions are unavailable offline, and some countries do not allow offline downloads. The Tripadvisor pages I could read do not describe offline use.

Can I save places and share a list on Tripadvisor or Google Maps?

Both let you save places. Tripadvisor's Play listing says to save experiences, hotels and restaurants. Google Maps has named lists that you can keep private, share by link or invite others to edit, with a limit of 3,000 entries per list. I could not read a Tripadvisor page about sharing saves.
