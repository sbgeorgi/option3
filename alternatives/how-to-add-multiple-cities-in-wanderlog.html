How to add multiple cities in Wanderlog

How to add multiple cities in Wanderlog: a 2025 video says to start a trip, tap Add destination and enter each city, but Wanderlog's own help pages do not document that step. What they do document is lists, sections and days, and a 2024 forum user kept each city in its own trip or section.

Updated 24 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), a place-discovery app, and I wrote this page, so I have an interest in the section on planning city by city. I did not run Wanderlog for this page. Every Wanderlog fact below comes from Wanderlog's own Help Center and FAQ, or from the two third-party sources named in the table, all read on 24 November 2026. There are no screenshots because I did not use the app. For the basics, see how to use Wanderlog; for adding a single place, see how to add places on Wanderlog.

Key facts

  • The step I could not confirm: Add destination, from a 2025 video description. None of the Wanderlog pages I read use that label.
  • What Wanderlog documents: lists and sections, adding places to a list, moving places between lists and days, and changing the trip dates.
  • The workaround users report: one section per city, or one trip per city (a forum thread from January 2024).
  • A real limit: the FAQ says moving an entire day as one block is not available yet.

How to add multiple cities in Wanderlog, step by step

This walk-through follows the sources above, not my own use of the app, and it says where a step rests on a user report. Labels are quoted from the page that uses them.

  1. 1. Decide between one trip and one trip per city.

    The Help Center's Trip settings blurb says "Share with friends. Set dates and destinations", but the four articles listed in that section cover changing dates, adding friends, deleting a trip and printing, not destinations (Help Center: Trip settings section). So the choice is yours. A forum user who planned Iceland, Barcelona, Paris and Colmar/Belgium wrote that they separated one long trip into segments (forum thread), and a second user in the thread called that the idea of separate trips.

  2. 2. Create the trip.

    A forum user's side-by-side notes from January 2024 say Wanderlog requires a trip name, does not require a destination to be selected, and treats dates and tripmates as optional or later (forum thread). That is a user report from that month, not current documentation, so the form may have changed.

  3. 3. Look for Add destination and enter each city.

    This is the only step I found that names a second destination, and it comes from the description of a 2025 video: open Wanderlog, start a new trip, tap Add destination, enter each city and save (SM Tech Solution video). I read the description, not the video, and none of Wanderlog's own pages I read use the label Add destination, so I cannot confirm it. If your app shows it, use it. If it does not, go to step 4 or 5.

  4. 4. Or give each city its own section in the trip.

    The same forum user wrote that Wanderlog lets you set up sections and then items under each section, and made one section per region, then moved the places onto itinerary dates once the dates were final (forum thread). Wanderlog's own pages describe lists and sections too: the FAQ says to reorder sections with Reorder sections in the app or Rearrange sections on the web (Wanderlog FAQ). I did not find a help article on creating a new list, so look for that button in your app.

  5. 5. Or make a separate trip for each city.

    This is the workaround the forum users described, and it needs nothing beyond the trip form in step 2. The cost is that your cities live in separate trips, so you open one trip per city to see its plan. The same forum thread's comparison notes that one email cannot be forwarded to two different Wanderlog trips, which matters if you use reservation forwarding (forum thread).

  6. 6. Add places, and check which list is active.

    The FAQ says to tap Add a place at the bottom of a place list and type your places (Wanderlog FAQ). A place added from a guide goes to the most recently edited list or day, and you can pick other lists or days afterwards (Help Center: Add a place from a guide to trip plan). My own reading of that, not something the page says: if you keep two cities in one trip, add a city's places while that city's list is the one you last edited. For the other ways to add places, see how to add places on Wanderlog.

  7. 7. Move places into the right city or day.

    The Help Center says you can select several places and move or copy them to any list or itinerary date; on the web you tick the boxes and choose Move to, then the list or day (Help Center: Move or delete multiple places at once). To move everything in a list or day, open the three dots by the section title and tap Select all (Help Center: Move or delete all places in a list or day). The FAQ says moving an entire day as one block is not available yet (Wanderlog FAQ).

  8. 8. Set the dates, then check the map.

    Tap the date range to select new dates, and the Help Center says the itinerary places keep their order (Help Center: Change dates of a trip). The FAQ says places in the Itinerary section get lines between them on the map with distances and times, and that the layers button at the top right of the map lets you tick which days to show (Wanderlog FAQ). It does not say how the map treats cities far apart, and I did not test it.

Limits and workarounds

  • No documented multi-city setting. The Help Center has no article on it, so the Add destination step stays unconfirmed until you see it in your own app.
  • Whole days do not move as a block. The FAQ says the option is being worked on, so you tick the places in a day and use Move to.
  • One trip per city loses the single view. It keeps each city tidy, and the forum thread notes that one email cannot be forwarded to two Wanderlog trips.
  • Route tools are per day. The FAQ describes route optimization as working on up to 15 places within one day, and the Help Center describes travel times between itinerary stops (time and distance article). Both are Wanderlog's descriptions and I did not test either, so I would not expect them to order your cities.

Questions about adding multiple cities in Wanderlog

How do I add multiple cities in Wanderlog?

A 2025 third-party video says to open Wanderlog, start a new trip, tap Add destination and enter each city. I could not find that step in Wanderlog's own help pages, so if you do not see it, make one trip, or one list section, per city, which is what a forum user reported doing in January 2024. I did not run Wanderlog myself.

Does Wanderlog support multi-city trips?

Wanderlog's Help Center describes trip settings as sharing and setting dates and destinations, but none of the articles I read explains how to add a second destination. A forum user kept one long trip with a section for each region and also reported splitting a multi-country trip into segments. I did not test either.

Can I add another city to a Wanderlog trip after I create it?

I could not confirm it. The help article on changing dates covers only dates, and the Help Center search for destination returned three articles, none about adding a destination. The video's Add destination step is the only mention I found. If it is missing in your app, create a second trip for the new city.

How do I move places from one day or list to another in Wanderlog?

Wanderlog's Help Center says to select the places with their boxes and use Move to, then choose the list or day. It says you can move or copy places between any list or itinerary date. Its FAQ says moving entire days is not available yet, so you move the places in a day instead.

Where do places I add land if I keep several cities in one trip?

Wanderlog's Help Center says a place added from a guide goes to the most recently edited list or day, and that you can pick other lists or days with Change in the app or the down arrow next to Add to trip on the web. If you keep several cities in one trip, check which list is active before you add.

The short version

To add multiple cities in Wanderlog, the click path I found is Add destination in a new trip, reported by a 2025 video that I could not confirm on Wanderlog's own pages. The reliable approach is to give each city its own section or its own trip, add places to the right list, then move them between lists and days with Move to and set the dates. Wanderlog's help pages document those pieces but not a multi-city setting, and its FAQ says moving a whole day is not available yet. If you plan one city at a time and want to swipe real places and see them on a map first, that is the different need NomadSwipe, which I built, is for.
