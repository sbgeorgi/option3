Apps Like Tripadvisor: 6 Alternatives Compared in 2026

Updated 12 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only.

Apps like Tripadvisor each replace one part of it: Wanderlog for an itinerary on a map, TripIt for bookings, Polarsteps for tracking a trip, Stippl and Mindtrip for AI planning. I built NomadSwipe, an Android app for picking real places by swiping. Tripadvisor itself is listed on Google Play with 100M+ downloads.

Tripadvisor is several products in one app: reviews, hotel and tour booking, and a trip planner. Its Google Play description lists reviews, booking and an itinerary planner with an AI trip builder, so people who want only one of those jobs often look for something narrower. This page compares six apps using each one's own public page, all read on 12 November 2026.

NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in a city by swiping

I built NomadSwipe, an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Tap Save or swipe right to keep a place, and tap Skip or swipe left to pass. Saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and you can build a trip plan from them.

  • Strength: a quick shortlist of places you chose yourself, before you have a plan. Cards show category, rating, distance and opening status.
  • Limit: it does not carry traveler reviews like Tripadvisor, book hotels or tours, or generate an AI itinerary.
  • Limit: it is Android only today.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary and a map in one view

Wanderlog's homepage headline is "One app for all your travel planning needs", and it says it puts your itinerary and your map in one view, so you stop switching between apps and tabs. It also says over 1 million people have tried it. That is the closest match to Tripadvisor's Trips and saves, with the map at the center instead of the reviews. I did not find a price on the pages I could read, so check its current plans. Our own pages cover it in Wanderlog alternatives and how to use Wanderlog.

TripIt

Best for: bookings in one itinerary

TripIt does not recommend places. Its homepage says you forward confirmation emails to plans@tripit.com and it creates an itinerary, no matter where you booked. Its pricing page shows a free version and TripIt Pro at $49 per year, and says you can access your itinerary offline. Pick it if your pain is scattered flight and hotel emails rather than choosing what to do. See TripIt alternatives for the other direction.

Polarsteps

Best for: tracking and sharing the trip itself

Polarsteps describes itself as one travel app to plan, track and relive trips, and says over 23M travelers use it. Its site says tracking keeps working offline. Polarsteps Plus costs 8.99 euros a month or 29.99 euros a year, with 3D maps, more map styles and a 20 percent Travel Book discount on the yearly plan. It is a trip diary more than a place finder. More in apps like Polarsteps.

Stippl

Best for: an AI-written day-by-day plan

Stippl's homepage calls it a free AI travel planner: describe a trip and the AI builds a full day-by-day itinerary with budget, stays and bookings. That is the Tripadvisor AI trip builder idea as a standalone app. If you would rather choose each place yourself, it will feel like more automation than you want. See Stippl alternatives.

Mindtrip

Best for: an assistant that learns your taste

Mindtrip calls itself a personal travel assistant that learns your style and helps you plan and book, with tabs for stays, flights, eats, experiences and events. It is the nearest thing here to Tripadvisor's all-in-one pitch. Read Mindtrip alternatives before choosing between the two.

Which to choose

  • You want reviews and booking in one app: stay with Tripadvisor.
  • You want a trip on a map with an itinerary: Wanderlog.
  • You want to pick real places in one city on Android: NomadSwipe (I built it), or see how to plan a trip on a map.
  • You want your booking emails organized: TripIt.
  • You want to record where you went: Polarsteps.
  • You want an AI to write the plan: Stippl or Mindtrip.

The short version

Apps like Tripadvisor replace one job each, and none of the six here replaces its traveler reviews. For a map-first itinerary use Wanderlog, for bookings use TripIt, for a trip diary use Polarsteps, and for AI planning use Stippl or Mindtrip. I built NomadSwipe for the narrower job of swiping through real places in a city on Android and saving them to a map.

FAQ

What is the best app like Tripadvisor?

It depends on the job. Wanderlog puts the itinerary and the map in one view, TripIt builds an itinerary from forwarded booking emails, and Polarsteps tracks and shares a trip. I built NomadSwipe, an Android app for choosing real places by swiping, which covers only the shortlist step.

Is Tripadvisor free?

Its App Store listing shows the app at a price of Free, and its description says it is free to join. The Google Play page says it contains ads. Hotel and tour bookings are paid separately.

Which apps like Tripadvisor build an itinerary for you?

Tripadvisor's Google Play description mentions an itinerary planner with an AI trip builder. Stippl says its AI builds a full day-by-day itinerary from one prompt, and Mindtrip describes a personal travel assistant that helps you plan and book.

Which app like Tripadvisor lets you pick places by swiping?

I built NomadSwipe, which shows real places in a city one card at a time. You tap Save or swipe right to keep one, and saved places appear on a map for that trip. It is Android only today and does not write an itinerary for you.

Do these apps replace Tripadvisor reviews?

No. Tripadvisor's listing leads with traveler reviews and an AI summary of them. None of the alternatives on this page position themselves as a review site, so many people keep Tripadvisor for reading reviews and use another app to plan.

Can I plan a day-by-day trip on NomadSwipe?

Partly. I built NomadSwipe to swipe real places, save them, see them on a map and build a trip plan, but it does not generate a day-by-day itinerary with AI. You arrange the days yourself around the map.
