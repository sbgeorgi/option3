Mapstr vs Google Maps: Which Is Better for Saving Places?

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), which appears once below as a third option. Facts about Mapstr and Google Maps checked on 14 November 2026. Updated 14 November 2026.

Mapstr vs Google Maps: choose Mapstr if you want a tagged, personal map of places, with friends' maps to follow; choose Google Maps if you want saved lists, navigation and downloadable offline maps in one app. Both are in the Play Store and the App Store, and Mapstr's home page links a web version.

Key facts

  • On Google Play on 14 November 2026, Mapstr showed 4.0 from 4.44K reviews and 500K+ downloads; Google Maps showed 3.4 from 19.6M reviews and 10B+. Ratings from samples that different are not comparable.
  • Google Maps keeps saved places in lists that you can share by link, open to others to edit, or follow, and its help page says a list is limited to 3,000 entries.
  • Mapstr's iPhone store page lists importing Google Maps, CSV or KML lists, so a Google Maps list does not have to be rebuilt by hand.
  • Offline works differently: Google Maps downloads areas of the map, while Mapstr's Play listing says your own map stays reachable offline.
  • I read no price for either app. Mapstr sells a paid plan, mapstr plus, with a 7-day trial; Google Maps' listings show ads and in-app purchases.

Five questions that decide it

I built this walk-through from the pages above, in the order I would ask the questions. Each step names the app the pages favour.

  1. Do your saved places already live in Google Maps? Then you do not have to choose one app. Mapstr's iPhone page lists importing Google Maps lists, so you can keep using Google Maps for the route and move the places over if you want tags and notes. Google Maps if you want zero migration; Mapstr if you want more than a list.
  2. Do you need directions in the same app? The Google Maps listing describes live traffic, rerouting and turn-by-turn navigation. Mapstr's listing tells you to find your itinerary with Google Maps or Waze, so it hands that job to another app. Google Maps.
  3. Do you want to browse other people's taste? Mapstr is built around following friends, chefs, magazines and creators and merging their maps with yours, per its iPhone page and home page. Google Maps lets you follow a list someone shared, but the help page describes it as a list you follow, not a network. Mapstr.
  4. Will you be without signal? Google Maps' help page describes downloading an area and navigating offline as long as the whole route is inside it, with no walking, transit or bicycling directions offline. Mapstr says your map is reachable offline but does not say how much of the underlying map is stored. If you need a route offline, Google Maps; if you only need to see your pins, either, so test it before the trip.
  5. Are you happy to pay if you save a lot? The Mapstr iPhone page puts unlimited places under mapstr plus. Google Maps' pages I read show no paid tier but a 3,000-entry cap on each list. A large collection pushes you toward Google Maps lists or toward paying Mapstr; a small one fits either.

Where the pages disagree with each other

Mapstr's Play listing offers geofencing proximity alerts for saved places without mentioning a plan, while the iPhone store page lists proximity alerts under mapstr plus. I cannot tell from the pages whether Android differs from iPhone here, so check in the app. Mapstr's Club announcement says the old Premium subscription is over and that Premium users move to Mapstr CLUB, while the iPhone page sells mapstr plus, so the plan names have changed at least once and the article is not dated. I trusted the store page for what is sold today.

Ratings differ by store too. Google Maps is 3.4 on Google Play and 4.7 on the U.S. App Store, and Mapstr is 4.0 on Google Play and 4.7 on the U.K. App Store. The two stores have different people rating at different times, so I would not rank the apps by them.

What each does better

Mapstr

Mapstr is built for the places you want to keep and the people whose taste you trust. Tags, notes, photos and a To try or Tried mark turn a pin into a note to yourself, a map can be shared with a link that needs no app, and following other maps is part of its pitch. Its Play listing also describes private custom places, so a spot that exists nowhere else can stay yours. If your list is long and personal, that structure is the point. For comparisons that include Mapstr, see Mapstr alternatives.

Google Maps

Google Maps is the app most people already have open, so saving a place costs one tap on a place page and the list sits next to directions, reviews and busy-times. Its lists can be shared by link or opened for others to edit, and its offline downloads can guide you while the whole route sits inside a saved area. The trade is that a list is a list: names, an icon and a comment, not tags and a To try mark. For more, see Google Maps list limits and alternatives, Google Maps offline for a trip and remembering places to visit in a city.

FAQ

Is Mapstr better than Google Maps for saving places?

It depends on the job. Mapstr is built only for saving places: tags, notes, photos, a To try or Tried mark, and maps you can follow from friends. Google Maps saves places into lists inside an app that also does navigation, reviews and offline map downloads. I did not install either app, so this is based on what their own pages say.

Can I import Google Maps lists into Mapstr?

Mapstr says yes. Its iPhone store page lists importing Google Maps, CSV or KML lists, and its French home page lists importing addresses from Google Maps and Foursquare. I did not read any page that describes the steps, and I found no Google Maps help page about exporting a list to Mapstr.

Does Mapstr or Google Maps work offline?

Both mention it, in different ways. Mapstr's Google Play listing says you can check your map when you are completely offline. Google Maps lets you download an area of the map, and its help page says that offline you cannot get walking, transit or bicycling directions, and that you cannot download offline maps in some countries. Neither page I read says how saved lists behave offline in Google Maps.

Is Mapstr free? Is Google Maps free?

Both are free to install. Mapstr's iPhone store page shows Free with in-app purchases and describes a paid plan called mapstr plus with a 7-day free trial, but no amount appears on that page. Google Maps' App Store page shows Free with in-app purchases, and its Google Play listing says it contains ads. I read no price for either app.

Can I share a list with friends in each app?

Yes in both. Google Maps shares a list by link, lets you set it to Private or Shared (anyone with the link), and lets you invite others to edit. Mapstr's iPhone store page says you can share a map by link that opens in any browser, and its Play listing says you can add friends and follow their maps.
