NomadSwipe

Best for: choosing places in a city by swiping

NomadSwipe is an Android trip planner for finding places. You pick a city and it shows real places one card at a time. Tap Save or swipe right to keep a place, and tap Skip or swipe left to pass. Saved places are pinned on a map for that trip, and the list can be sorted by rating, distance or A to Z.

  • Strength: a quick shortlist of places you chose yourself, before you have a plan.
  • Limit: it does not build a day-by-day itinerary. The Google Play listing describes swiping, saving and a map, and you plan each day yourself around it.
  • Limit: it is Android only.

The base plan includes 3 trips, 2 searched places saved to a trip and 2 custom pins a month, with a daily limit on saves. Premium (Monthly or Lifetime, bought in the app) removes those limits. Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online; offline copies expire.

Sygic Travel (Tripomatic)

Best for: a day-by-day itinerary with travel guides and offline maps

Sygic Travel is now sold as Tripomatic, published by Tripomatic s.r.o. Its Google Play listing, updated 5 November 2026, shows 4.2 stars from 18.4K reviews and describes a day-by-day itinerary planner with estimated travel times and walking distances, sync between devices and its web planner, and invitations for friends to collaborate on trips. Its older Sygic product page says its travel guides cover more than 10,000 locations.

Offline maps and custom AI prompts sit behind Premium. The Google Play listing says you need Premium to use the app without an internet connection, including unlimited offline map downloads and walking navigation. The tripomatic.com site says the same for the mobile app: with Premium you can download offline maps that include all the places in your trip. Premium is a subscription paid through Google Play, and the listing offers a free trial first. Neither page I read states a Premium price, so check it in the store before the trial ends.

The free plan has no time limit and shows ads. According to tripomatic.com, it includes up to 5 days with planned activities per calendar month across all your trips and up to 25 places and hotels per trip. Premium removes those limits and the ads, and adds offline maps, trip export to PDF, GPX and KML, premium map layers, trip attachments, live flight updates and custom AI prompts. The same site lists the trip planner on the web, iOS and Android; this page only covers Android.

  • Strength: a day-by-day itinerary and travel guides in one app, with offline maps on Premium.
  • Limit: offline maps need Premium, and the free plan caps planned days at 5 a month across all your trips.

Wanderlog

Best for: an itinerary with a map in one view

Wanderlog's homepage describes one app for all travel planning: detailed itineraries, your itinerary and your map in one view, and bookings in the same place. It says you can plan with friends in real time, with live syncing and collaborative editing. The homepage lists offline access, described as trip plans downloaded to the phone, in the section about its Pro subscription, but its sources disagree on whether the free plan includes it (see our Wanderlog comparison), so test it before you rely on it. We did not confirm its price, because its pricing page returned a 404 when we checked, so check its site before you decide.

  • Strength: the itinerary and the map sit in one view, and collaborative editing is listed.
  • Limit: offline access appears in its Pro section, and we did not confirm its price.

Which to choose

  • You need offline maps and walking navigation and prefer not to subscribe: Organic Maps or OsmAnd, per their own pages, with a planner on top. See the offline comparison.
  • You want a day-by-day itinerary with guides and offline maps, and you accept Premium: Sygic Travel, now Tripomatic.
  • You want to shortlist real places in one city on Android: NomadSwipe.
  • You want an itinerary and a map in one view, with collaborative editing: Wanderlog, after you check its price.

Questions people ask

What is a good Sygic Travel alternative for offline use on Android?

It depends on what you need offline. For offline maps, the pages I read describe Google Maps downloaded areas (no walking, bicycling or transit directions offline), Organic Maps and OsmAnd (offline maps with navigation). For a trip plan on the phone, Wanderlog lists offline access under Pro. NomadSwipe is not an offline-maps tool: up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online, and copies expire.

How much is Tripomatic Premium?

The tripomatic.com page and the Google Play listing text I read do not state a price. The listing says you subscribe through Google Play after a free trial, so check the price in the store before the trial ends.

Is Sygic Travel the same app as Tripomatic?

Yes, according to its Google Play listing, which calls the app Tripomatic (formerly Sygic Travel).

Does Sygic Travel work offline on Android?

Only with Premium. The Tripomatic listing on Google Play says you need Premium to use the app without an internet connection, with unlimited offline map downloads and walking navigation. Premium is a subscription with a free trial.

Does Tripomatic work offline?

Only with Premium. Its Google Play listing says Premium is needed to use the app without an internet connection.

Does NomadSwipe work offline?

Up to 20 saved trips are available offline after you open them online. Offline copies expire, according to the Google Play listing.

Does NomadSwipe build a day-by-day itinerary?

No. It gives you a shortlist of places and a map for each trip, and you plan each day yourself around that map.

Does Tripomatic limit the version without Premium?

Yes. According to tripomatic.com, the free plan has no time limit and shows ads, with up to 5 days with planned activities per calendar month across all your trips and up to 25 places and hotels per trip. Premium removes those limits and adds offline maps.

How much does NomadSwipe Premium cost?

Premium (Monthly or Lifetime) is bought in the app, and the price is shown in Google Play before you pay. This article does not list prices.
