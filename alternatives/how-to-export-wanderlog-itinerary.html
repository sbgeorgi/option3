How to export a Wanderlog itinerary

How to export a Wanderlog itinerary: on the website, open the trip, click the three dots at the top right and choose Print this trip, then print to PDF. To send your places to Google Maps, Wanderlog Pro has an Export to Google Maps button on the map view. I found no spreadsheet or calendar export.

Every Wanderlog route below comes from Wanderlog's own help center, FAQ and product pages, all read on 21 November 2026 and listed under Sources. I did not log in to Wanderlog or press any of these buttons for this page, so there are no screenshots, and I cannot tell you how a printout or an exported map looks. Some help articles are about four years old, so a menu name may have moved. I built NomadSwipe, so I have an interest in the last section.

Step by step

  1. 1. Print the trip, or save it as a PDF.

    Wanderlog's help article "Print your trip" says printing is done from the Wanderlog website: log in, open your trip plan, tap the three dots in the upper right corner and select Print this trip. For a PDF, the Wanderlog FAQ says you cannot save a trip as a PDF inside the app, but you can in a web browser. If you are in the mobile app, tap Share and copy the link, open the link in your browser, then use Print from the three-dot menu at the top right. The FAQ says you should be able to print to a PDF there. That last part depends on your browser or computer, not on Wanderlog. The FAQ also says there is no Google Maps print: it suggests clicking Directions between two places and printing the directions from the Google Maps website.

  2. 2. Share the trip with a link or an invite.

    This hands someone the live plan rather than a file. In the app open the trip and tap Share; on the website use the add-a-person button. Per the help article "Add friends to plan together", you can invite people by email or message and set whether they can only view or can also edit. The FAQ adds the wording: choose Can edit to collaborate (then copy the link or invite by email) or Can view for a read-only copy. Who can see the plan is set from the three dots near the top of the trip, then Trip Settings, with three options named in the FAQ: Public, Friends (your Wanderlog followers) or Private (only you and people with the link). In the app you can also make a trip story with photos and share its link (the plus sign at the bottom, then Trip story). I did not find a page that says whether the person needs an account to open a link. See how to use Wanderlog for the sharing step in the context of a full plan.

  3. 3. Export your places to Google Maps (Pro).

    The Wanderlog FAQ gives one sentence: switch to the map view and press the Export to Google Maps button at the right top corner of the screen. Wanderlog's Pro page lists it under premium features, saying all your places are exported to Google Maps and that they update automatically if your plans change; its pricing page says the same in fewer words. The travel maps page says you can export your entire itinerary to Google Maps with the Pro subscription. Not documented on the pages I read: whether the button is in the app, the website or both, what the result is called in Google Maps, and whether times, notes or day order come across. Test it on one small trip before you count on it.

  4. 4. Open one place or one leg in Google or Apple Maps.

    For a single stop, tap the place to see its details and press Directions (in the app), or press Directions on the map (on the website), per the help article "Open directions to a place in Google/Apple Maps". For two neighbouring stops, press Directions next to the travel time and distance between them: the app lets you pick your map app, and on the website it opens Google Maps ("Open directions to next itinerary stop"). Neither article names a plan. This sends a route, not the whole itinerary.

  5. 5. Build a map to embed on a blog.

    The help center FAQ says Wanderlog's custom maps are easy to make, completely free, and can be embedded on a blog or site, and points to a separate step-by-step guide. Wanderlog's embed page calls it a free map for your article. I did not open the guide, so I do not know whether it copies a trip or starts from a blank map. Treat it as a way to publish a map, not as a backup of your itinerary.

What is free and what needs Pro

Wanderlog's help center says its website and app stay free for as many trips as you want to plan, with a paid plan called Wanderlog Pro for premium power-user features. For export, the pages I read put exactly one route behind Pro: Export to Google Maps. Wanderlog's Pro page shows the free plan from $0 and Pro Annual starting at $39.99 a year, with Export to Google Maps in its list (the help center's Pro-features section also covers offline downloads and route optimization, which are not exports). Prices change, so check the live page. Printing, sharing and directions carry no Pro label on the help pages I read.

What you cannot export

  • No spreadsheet, CSV or calendar file. I found no such button or article on the help center, the FAQ or the product pages. The FAQ says there is no calendar view in a trip plan.
  • No PDF button in the app. The FAQ says to do it from a web browser through Print.
  • No email-me-my-itinerary option. Emails appear only the other way, for forwarding bookings into a trip, and for email invites when you share. I found no way to email the plan out.
  • No printed Google Map. The FAQ says Wanderlog has no Google Map print for now.
  • Offline downloads are not a file. The help center says Pro members can save a trip plan inside the app for offline use. That stays in the app; which trip planner offers offline maps and today view covers the planners built around it.

Questions about exporting a Wanderlog itinerary

How do I export a Wanderlog itinerary?

Wanderlog's pages describe these routes: print the trip from the website (three dots, Print this trip) and choose a PDF printer, share a view or edit link, export your places to Google Maps with Pro, open Directions for one stop in Google or Apple Maps, or build an embeddable map for a blog. I found no spreadsheet, CSV or calendar export. For trips with more than one city, see multi-city Wanderlog trips.

Can I export a Wanderlog trip as a PDF?

Not inside the app. Wanderlog's FAQ says you can do it in a web browser: open the trip on the website (from the app, use Share and copy the link), choose Print this trip from the three-dot menu and print to PDF. Whether a PDF option appears depends on your browser or computer.

Is Export to Google Maps free in Wanderlog?

No. Wanderlog's Pro page and pricing page both list Export to Google Maps as a Pro feature. The Pro page shows Pro Annual starting at $39.99 a year and a Start free trial button; I did not read the trial terms.

Can I export a Wanderlog itinerary to Excel, Google Sheets or a calendar?

None of the Wanderlog help or product pages I read mention it. The help center and FAQ list printing, sharing, directions and the Google Maps export, and the FAQ says Wanderlog has no calendar view. If you need a spreadsheet, copy the places by hand.

Can I move my Wanderlog itinerary into NomadSwipe?

No. I built NomadSwipe (Android) and it does not import a Wanderlog itinerary. You pick a city, swipe real places and save the ones you like; you can add your Wanderlog places there by hand.

Short answer

To export a Wanderlog itinerary, use the website: open the trip, choose Print this trip from the three dots and print to PDF. Pro members can press Export to Google Maps on the map view to send their places to Google Maps. You can also share a view or edit link, or open Directions for one stop. Wanderlog's pages I read name no spreadsheet, CSV or calendar export. That is as read on 21 November 2026.
