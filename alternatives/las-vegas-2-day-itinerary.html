Las Vegas (Nevada) in 2 days: 17 stops, 15 checked against stored opening hours

A 2-day Las Vegas (Nevada) itinerary of 17 stops from the NomadSwipe app's place data: nine along Las Vegas Boulevard South on day one, eight around Fremont Street on day two. Fifteen stops have stored hours, and every start time falls inside them on all seven weekdays.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app's Las Vegas place data, read on 5 November 2026. The stored rows were last updated between 21 April and 20 June 2026, so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, Las Vegas Boulevard South: Bouchon Bakery, Madame Tussauds, lunch at Joe's, the Bellagio Conservatory, the Eiffel Tower Viewing Deck, the Arte Museum, dinner at Prime Steakhouse, the Fountains of Bellagio and a late drink at Carnaval Court.
  • Day 2, Fremont Street and the north: breakfast at AmeriBrunch, the Mob Museum, lunch at Carson Kitchen, the Science & Natural History Museum, the Neon Museum, axe throwing, dinner at Andiamo and a drink at The Laundry Room.
  • Days of the week: unlike a lot of city plans, both days pass on all seven weekdays by the stored hours. The weekday risk in this deck sits in the cards I left out (see the closed-day table).
  • The two areas are far apart: the reference points for the two days are 7.1 km apart by straight line, so I kept one area per day and added no transport leg.

Why one area per day

The app's Las Vegas data holds 746 cards under two destination names, Las Vegas and Las Vegas (Nevada), which add up to 608 distinct places. Of those, 175 sit within 4 km of a point I set on the Fountains of Bellagio card's coordinates (36.1127 N, 115.1739 W), and 85 sit within 2 km of a second point I set on Fremont Street (36.1699 N, 115.1398 W). The two points are 7.06 km apart. Almost every stop worth timing is in one of the two clusters, so the plan is two compact days instead of one stretched one.

  • Prices are left out. I did not source ticket prices from official pages this run, so check each place's own website the day you go.
  • I only describe what the data says. Each stop gets its stored category, rating, review count, address and hours. I did not read any official page or review text for these places this run.
  • Two stops have no stored hours. The Bellagio Conservatory and the Fountains of Bellagio cards have none, so I could not match them to a day or time. Check their own pages before you build around them.

Day 1: Las Vegas Boulevard South

Day one stays on one road. By straight line between stored coordinates, every stop is within 1.1 km of the Fountains of Bellagio card's coordinates, and going stop to stop in the order below adds up to about 3.6 km. The longest single leg, about 0.8 km, is from lunch to the Conservatory. Times are my suggested start times.

  1. 8:00, Bouchon Bakery: stored as a bakery at 3355 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily from 6 AM to 5 PM, 4.1 from 2,238 reviews. It is the first stop of the day and the furthest from the reference point, at 1.08 km.
  2. 10:00, Madame Tussauds Las Vegas: stored as a tourist attraction at 3377 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily 10 AM to 8 PM, 4.1 from 6,151 reviews. It opens at the start time, so the 10:00 start has no margin on the opening side.
  3. 12:30, Joe's Seafood, Prime Steak & Stone Crab: stored as a restaurant at 3500 Las Vegas Blvd S, 4.6 from 9,631 reviews, with 11:30 AM to 9 PM hours and 10 PM closing on Friday and Saturday. The most-reviewed restaurant in this plan. A swap if you want fish at a different table: Water Grill Las Vegas, 4.5 from 1,792 reviews, at the same street number.
  4. 14:00, Bellagio Conservatory & Botanical Gardens: stored as a garden at 3600 Las Vegas Blvd S, 4.7 from 16,104 reviews, 0.25 km from the reference point. No stored hours.
  5. 15:30, Eiffel Tower Viewing Deck: stored as a tourist attraction at 3655 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily from noon to midnight, 4.4 from 7,602 reviews. It opens at noon, which is why it sits in the afternoon, not first thing.
  6. 17:00, Arte Museum Las Vegas: stored as a museum at 3716 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily 10 AM to 10 PM, 4.7 from 2,915 reviews, the highest-rated museum card in this deck with at least 2,000 stored reviews. A second card, Museum of Illusions, has the same address (4.5 from 370 reviews, open until midnight Friday and Saturday) and works as a swap.
  7. 19:00, Prime Steakhouse: stored as a restaurant at 3600 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily from 5 PM to 10 PM, 4.2 from 4,651 reviews. Dinner has to start after 5 PM, so the museum is the filler before it, 0.55 km away.
  8. 20:30, Fountains of Bellagio: stored as a tourist attraction, 4.7 from 93,816 reviews, the most-reviewed card in the whole Las Vegas data. It is 0.15 km from dinner, so it fits here, but it has no stored hours and no stored show times. I could not match it to a day.
  9. 21:30, Carnaval Court Las Vegas: stored as a bar at 3475 Las Vegas Blvd S, open daily from noon to 3 AM, 4.2 from 802 reviews. It is 0.7 km from the fountains. Swaps: Vanderpump Cocktail Garden (4.0 from 2,402 reviews, 3 PM to midnight Monday to Wednesday and longer on other days) or minus5 ICEBAR (see the findings for why that name appears three times).

Day 2: Fremont Street and the north

Day two is looser. All eight stops are within 1.24 km of the Fremont Street reference point, but going stop to stop adds up to about 4.4 km because the plan runs out to the Science & Natural History Museum and back. The longest leg, 1.47 km, is from lunch at Carson Kitchen to that museum. The other six legs are all under 1 km.

  1. 8:00, AmeriBrunch Cafe: stored as a restaurant at 316 Las Vegas Blvd N, 4.5 from 1,981 reviews. Open 8 AM to 2 PM, or to 3 PM on Saturday and Sunday. It is the earliest-closing stop on either day, so day two starts here.
  2. 9:30, The Mob Museum: stored as a museum at 300 Stewart Ave, open daily 9 AM to 9 PM, 4.5 from 12,224 reviews, 0.6 km from breakfast.
  3. 12:00, Carson Kitchen: stored as a restaurant at 124 S 6th St, 4.5 from 4,908 reviews, open from 11:30 AM to 10 PM (11 PM Friday and Saturday). Swap: Pizza Rock, 4.5 from 9,102 reviews, open to midnight on Friday and Saturday, 0.3 km from the Fremont Street point.
  4. 13:30, Las Vegas Science & Natural History Museum: stored as a museum at 900 Las Vegas Blvd N, open daily from 9 AM to 4 PM, 4.3 from 485 reviews. It closes first of any museum here, so it gets the early slot with 2.5 hours of margin.
  5. 15:30, The Neon Museum Las Vegas: stored as a museum at 770 Las Vegas Blvd N, 4.3 from 7,672 reviews. Open daily from 3 PM to 11 PM as stored, so it cannot be a morning stop, and 15:30 leaves it 30 minutes after opening.
  6. 17:00, Axehole Vegas | Axe Throwing Bar: stored as a tourist attraction at 450 Fremont St, 5.0 from 1,028 reviews. Open 10 AM to 1 AM Monday to Thursday, 11 AM to 2 AM on Friday and Saturday, and noon to 1 AM on Sunday. It is 0.9 km from the museum.
  7. 18:30, Andiamo Steakhouse: stored as a restaurant at 301 Fremont St, open daily 4 PM to 11 PM, 4.6 from 3,438 reviews, 0.2 km from the axe bar.
  8. 20:00, The Laundry Room: stored as a bar at 525 Fremont St, 4.5 from 789 reviews. Open 6 PM to 10 PM Sunday to Thursday and 5 PM to 9 PM on Friday and Saturday, so on a Friday or Saturday a 20:00 start leaves one hour.   • The deck holds two cards from far outside the region. One titled "Tours, Centre-Val de Loire, France" sits about 8,756 km from the Fountains point by its stored coordinates and Andersonville National Historic Site in Georgia sits about 2,877 km away.

  • The Science & Natural History Museum is stored twice, under two different place IDs and two spellings of the category, with the same rating and review count. The plan counts it once.

Questions about this Las Vegas plan

Can I follow this Las Vegas plan on any day of the week?

Yes, as stored. I tested every suggested start time against the stored hours for all seven weekdays, and both days pass on every one. The tightest margin is The Laundry Room on a Friday or Saturday, where the stored closing time is 9 PM and the plan starts at 8 PM. The stored hours are from April to June 2026, so confirm them before you go.

Which Las Vegas places in the app are closed on a Tuesday?

Among the cards I looked at near the two areas, Zak Bagans' The Haunted Museum is stored as closed on Tuesday, Wing Lei as closed on Tuesday and Wednesday, and XS Nightclub as closed on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. None of them is in the plan. Across all 430 places with stored hours, 37 show Tuesday as closed and 45 show Monday as closed.

Why are Carlo's Bakery, SlotZilla and the Gondola Rides card not in the plan?

Their stored ratings are below the 4.0 line I used: Carlo's Bakery is 3.7 from 4,034 reviews, SlotZilla Zipline is 3.9 from 3,690 and Gondola Rides is 3.9 from 2,929. The Gondola Rides card also has no stored hours. If one of them is why you came, add it as a swap.

How were the 17 stops chosen?

I picked cards within 1.3 km of one of two reference points, one on Las Vegas Boulevard South and one on Fremont Street, with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 150 stored reviews. Then I checked each start time against the stored hours. The Bellagio Conservatory and the Fountains of Bellagio cards are the exceptions: they have no stored hours.

Does this include Las Vegas, New Mexico?

No. The app has a separate destination for Las Vegas in New Mexico, and I left it out. Two Nevada destination rows, Las Vegas and Las Vegas (Nevada), hold the same city, so I merged them for this page.
