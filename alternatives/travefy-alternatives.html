Travefy Alternatives Free: 4 Options Checked on 12 November 2026

Updated 12 November 2026. Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe, which is Android only. The options checked here are other tools, not NomadSwipe.

Travefy alternatives that are free to start include Pilot and Trip Playbook's Starter plan, while Travefy itself costs $39 a month billed annually after a 10-day trial. Travefy is built for travel advisors. NomadSwipe is a consumer Android app and not a replacement for it.

Most people searching for a free Travefy alternative fall into two groups. Some are advisors who want cheaper software for client itineraries, and some are travelers who landed on Travefy while planning a trip and do not need a business tool at all. This page covers both, using each vendor's public pages read on 12 November 2026, and says where an option falls short.

What Travefy is, and what it costs

Travefy's homepage describes travel software for travel professionals: a drag-and-drop itinerary builder, proposals, a CRM, a website builder and a client mobile app with live flight updates and offline access. Its pricing page lists Core at $39 a month billed annually, with a monthly option whose price is not shown, and Premium at $59 a month billed annually. A New Travel Agent Program is listed at $25 a month, and Agency seats are $20 a month for each additional seat on Core-based teams.

The pricing page offers a 10-day free trial with all features of the chosen plan, and the homepage says no credit card is required. Neither page lists a permanent free plan, so "free" with Travefy means the trial. One thing to know if you are cost-comparing: the homepage calls it the "#1 itinerary builder by travel agents", but that is Travefy's own claim.

Pilot

Best for: a traveler who wants a free planner

Pilot's own comparison page calls it a free trip planning app for building an itinerary with a map and notes, and says map, collaboration and offline access on mobile are included at no cost. It says it earns from hotel bookings made through the app and does not sell sponsored placement. Pilot describes Travefy as an itinerary organizer for advisors with no free tier.

  • Strength: planning is stated as free and it targets individual travelers and groups.
  • Limit: these claims are self-reported on a page written to win the comparison, and it is not advisor software, so there are no client proposals or invoicing.

Trip Playbook

Best for: an independent agent testing a free plan

Trip Playbook is a web-based tool for travel agents to build itineraries, which it calls playbooks, and share them by link or export them as PDF. Its Starter plan is listed at $0 a month with 3 active playbooks, 10 clients, 5 AI summary generations a month and built-in templates. Pro is $19 a month and adds unlimited playbooks, custom templates and PDF download, and Enterprise is $49 a month. The site says it is currently in beta.

Its own blog post compares itself with Travefy and Axus, and quotes Travefy at $35 a month, which differs from the $39 on Travefy's current pricing page. Treat vendor comparisons as marketing.

  • Strength: a real $0 tier aimed at advisors.
  • Limit: beta software, no PDF export on Starter, and no CRM or invoicing listed.

Wanderlog

Best for: a traveler planning a trip with friends

Wanderlog's homepage describes one app to plan an itinerary, find lodging and import reservations, with live syncing and collaborative editing, offline access listed as a feature, and links to iOS and Android apps. A reviewer quoted on that page describes a fully functional free version and an annual-only paid tier. The vendor's own price was not confirmed on this date. For more detail see our Wanderlog alternatives and how to use Wanderlog pages.

  • Strength: a consumer planner with collaboration and iPhone and Android apps.
  • Limit: not made for advisors, and its price was not confirmed on this date.

TripCreator

Best for: a travel business that wants one platform

TripCreator's pricing page calls it "the operating system for travel businesses" and lists an itinerary builder, AI, a booking engine, payments, sales and CRM, reporting and a mobile app. It lists no free plan: after a demo you get a trial account, and a credit card is required to subscribe. We could not read plan prices on the page. It says it takes no commission or fees on customer payments.

  • Strength: a broad business feature set.
  • Limit: no free plan listed and prices not visible, so it is not a free option.

NomadSwipe

Best for: a traveler choosing places in a city, not an advisor

I built NomadSwipe, and it does a different job from Travefy. It is an Android app: you pick a city, it shows real places one card at a time, and you save the ones you like onto a map for that trip. The list can be sorted by rating, distance or A to Z, and you plan each day yourself. It has no client proposals, no CRM, no invoicing and no white-label branding.

  • Strength: a quick shortlist of places you picked yourself.
  • Limit: Android only, and nothing for running a travel business.

Which to choose

  • You run a travel advisor business and need CRM, invoicing and a client app: Travefy or TripCreator, after a demo or trial.
  • You are an independent advisor testing a cheaper start: Trip Playbook Starter, knowing it is beta and limited.
  • You are a traveler planning your own trip with friends: Pilot or Wanderlog.
  • You want to shortlist real places in a city on Android: NomadSwipe.

FAQ

Is there a free Travefy alternative?

Pilot says it is completely free for planning, and Trip Playbook lists a Starter plan at $0 a month with 3 active playbooks. Pilot is a consumer planner and Trip Playbook is a newer advisor tool in beta, and both facts come from the vendors' own pages. Neither matches Travefy's CRM, invoicing and website builder.

Does Travefy have a free plan?

Not according to its pricing page, which lists Core at $39 a month billed annually and Premium at $59 a month billed annually. Travefy does offer a 10-day free trial, and its homepage says no credit card is required.

Is NomadSwipe a Travefy alternative for travel advisors?

No. I built NomadSwipe, and it is a consumer Android app: you swipe through real places in a city, save them, see them on a map and plan your own days. It has no client proposals, CRM, invoicing or branding tools, so an advisor running a business should look at the advisor tools in this list.

What do Travefy's plans include?

Core covers the itinerary and proposal builder, a CRM, a website and landing page builder, invoicing and commission tracking, mobile apps and email integration. Premium adds priority support, premium onboarding, a custom domain and business email hosting, according to Travefy's pricing page.

Which option fits a solo traveler planning one trip?

A consumer planner is the better match than software built for advisors. Pilot and Wanderlog are built for travelers, and NomadSwipe is an Android option if you want to choose places by swiping first. Travefy's own homepage says it is software for travel professionals.
