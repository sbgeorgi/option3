Yestrip vs TripIt for Real-Time Navigation During Travel

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), which appears once below and does not do real-time navigation. Facts about Yestrip and TripIt checked on 14 November 2026. Updated 14 November 2026.

Yestrip vs TripIt for real-time navigation during travel: on the pages I read, neither is a turn-by-turn navigation app. TripIt Pro sends real-time flight alerts and airport walking directions; Yestrip shows your next stop and when to set off, but says it has no live flight status.

Key facts

  • TripIt's real-time features sit mostly in TripIt Pro: flight alerts, a Go Now leave-for-the-airport prompt, terminal and gate reminders, and airport maps with walking directions. Pro costs $49 a year on its site, with a 30-day free trial.
  • The free TripIt app's Play listing describes driving directions between two points, transport options and a search for nearby restaurants, parking and ATMs, and says to plot the trip on Google Maps or Apple Maps.
  • Yestrip's site describes a Today view with your next stop, the way there, the day's tickets, weather and a reminder when it is time to go. One tap opens the route. Its Play listing adds: “There is no live flight status.”
  • Yestrip is a very small app on Google Play (10+ downloads, no rating shown on 14 November 2026); TripIt showed 4.7 from 96.8K reviews and 5M+.
  • Both treat bookings as email: TripIt by forwarding to its own address, Yestrip by forwarding, pasting or opening a PDF.

Five questions that decide it

I built this walk-through from the pages above, in the order I would ask the questions. Each step names the app the pages favour.

  1. Is the movement you care about a flight? TripIt's Pro page lists real-time flight alerts, terminal and gate reminders, a Go Now prompt and baggage claim info. Yestrip's Play listing says there is no live flight status. TripIt, if you pay for Pro.
  2. Do you want to be walked through an airport? Only TripIt's pages describe interactive airport maps and walking directions to a gate or connection; the pricing page puts the navigation itself under Pro. Yestrip does not mention it. TripIt.
  3. Do you want the next stop on a city day? Yestrip's site builds a day plan with times and short walks, then shows the next stop, the way there and a reminder to set off. TripIt's pages describe an itinerary built from bookings, with map tools around it, not a stop-by-stop day plan. Yestrip.
  4. Do you need street directions inside the app? TripIt's Play listing mentions driving directions between two points; Yestrip says one tap opens the route. Neither page says “turn-by-turn”, so check a maps app. Even.
  5. Will you pay? Yestrip says it is free with a monthly AI credit allowance. TripIt is free for the itinerary and sells Pro for $49 a year. Yestrip on cost; TripIt on live alerts.

Where the pages stop short

Neither app's pages describe turn-by-turn voice navigation, so “real-time navigation” here means three different things: live flight data (TripIt Pro), a next-stop view with route hand-off (Yestrip) and map tools around an itinerary (TripIt). If you need spoken directions at every junction, plan on a dedicated maps app; see Citymapper alternatives for city transit apps and Google Maps as an offline trip planner.

Two limits on the numbers. Yestrip's Play listing shows 10+ downloads and no rating, so there is no review sample to weigh; TripIt's 4.7 from 96.8K reviews is a large one, though the ratings section of the same page showed 92.6K, so treat the count as approximate. And Yestrip's chat answers are, in its own words on the Play listing, suggestions rather than guarantees, with opening hours and connections possibly out of date.

Who each fits

TripIt

TripIt is built around bookings. Forward a flight, hotel or car confirmation and it assembles an itinerary, then, with Pro, watches the flights. If your trip is mostly flights and connections, it is the one with live alerts. See how to use TripIt, TripIt alternatives and Stippl vs TripIt.

Yestrip

Yestrip is built around the plan for the day. It writes a day-by-day AI plan, keeps tickets with the trip, shows what comes next and offers an offline map download. It is newer and tiny by Google Play's numbers, and it says in plain words that it has no live flight status. It suits a city break where the day is the unit, not a connection through a hub.

FAQ

Can Yestrip or TripIt do real-time navigation?

Not as turn-by-turn navigation, on the pages I read. TripIt Pro adds real-time flight alerts and airport walking directions, and the TripIt app lists driving directions between two points. Yestrip's Today view shows your next stop and the way there, and its Play listing says there is no live flight status. I did not install either app, so I cannot say how the route hand-off behaves.

Does TripIt give real-time flight alerts for free?

No. Its pricing page puts real-time flight alerts, Go Now and terminal and gate reminders under TripIt Pro, which it lists at $49 per year with a 30-day free trial on the Pro page. The Google Play listing shows the yearly renewal as $48.99.

Is Yestrip free?

Its site calls it free and says a paid subscription is not offered at the moment. AI requests use credits, with 40 free every month per account, so the free part has a monthly ceiling on AI use.

Which one works better at the airport?

TripIt, on what the pages say: Pro lists interactive airport maps, walking directions, connecting-gate help and baggage claim info. Yestrip's pages mention flights only as bookings to import and say it has no live flight status.
