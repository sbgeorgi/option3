Is Culture Trip shut down?

I found no evidence of a shutdown. Five things were true when I checked on 27 November 2026:

  • The theculturetrip.com home page loaded with guides, a Trips section described as "bookable adventures", short films and a newsletter form. Its footer reads "© Copyright 2026 International Travel Connect, LLC". One other page, /about, returned an edge-server error when I fetched it, which I read as a single failed request rather than an outage.
  • The Google Play listing for Culture Trip: Book Travel lists U.S. News & World Report L.P. as the developer, shows 1M+ downloads and says "Updated on Nov 24, 2025".
  • The U.S. App Store listing shows the same developer, about 11K ratings averaging 4.8, and version 6.5 dated "Sep 10". The page shows no year, so I cannot say which September.
  • I found no shutdown notice from the company or from U.S. News in the news coverage I read.
  • Third-party outage trackers rely on user reports and often show stale dates, so I did not use them.

So the company did not close. The business behind the name changed owners and changed what it sells, and that is the real answer to the question.

One pattern stands out in the company's own numbers: it claimed 15 to 20 million monthly unique visitors in May 2020 and about 9 million in July 2023. Those are two press statements three years apart, so treat the drop as a rough signal, not a measured trend.

What actually changed

The company changed what it sells

Culture Trip began as an advertising-funded content site, became a hospitality-focused travel agency after its 2018 funding, added tours in 2021 and in 2022 narrowed to curated small-group trips. PhocusWire summarises it as having "shifted strategy several times since its founding". The 2023 buyout release describes it as a "purpose-led small-group tour operator" with content at its core.

The website, and the app, changed owner

The sale in February 2024 covered the website. The app's listings now name U.S. News & World Report L.P. as developer, which fits that sale, though no source I read states that the app itself was part of the deal. What U.S. News said it was buying was a travel guide and tours booking site to add to its own travel business, alongside rankings and advice. What the sale statement does not say is what happens to Culture Trip's own small-group trips, and I could not confirm who runs them today. The site's Trips tab still describes bookable adventures.

The listing text is dated

The Play listing still says "In these uncertain times, cancel or change for free on select properties", a line that reads as pandemic-era, and still quotes a customer review dated 10 December 2020. That does not prove anything is broken, but it is a sign that the descriptive text is no longer being actively rewritten.

Culture Trip app not working: what I found

I found no outage report from the company, from U.S. News or in the news pieces I read, and I could not reproduce anything because I did not install the app. The evidence I have is thin:

  • The App Store's "What's New" for version 6.5 reads "Improved article loading and display, including travel guides, images, and place recommendations. Fixed an issue that prevented some articles from opening." That is a documented bug fix for the reading experience, in the version dated "Sep 10".
  • The Play listing was last updated on 24 November 2025, a year before I checked, so an Android phone running an old build may not have the latest fix.
  • On Trustpilot the company shows 2.2 out of 5 from 8 reviews, with none in the last 12 months. Eight reviews is too few to show a pattern, and Trustpilot files the company under Travel Agency, so it says little about the app.

If the app will not load for you, these steps are the usual order:

  1. Install the latest version of the app, since the one release note I found is about articles failing to open.
  2. Open theculturetrip.com in a browser. If the guides load there but not in the app, it is the app; if neither loads, check your connection first.
  3. Clear the app cache or reinstall if articles still fail. Your saved plans should sit with your account, but I could not verify that, so note anything you saved before you remove it.
  4. Contact the company. Trustpilot lists heretohelp@theculturetrip.com as its contact address; I did not test whether it answers.

Bottom line

As of 27 November 2026, Culture Trip is still running: theculturetrip.com loads and its app is listed on Google Play and the App Store under U.S. News & World Report L.P. What happened is a change of business and owner, not a closure: layoffs in May 2020, a management buyout in July 2023 and a sale of the website to U.S. News on 26 February 2024. Questions about what happened to Culture Trip

Did Culture Trip shut down?

No evidence of it. On 27 November 2026 theculturetrip.com loaded, and its Google Play listing, updated 24 November 2025, and App Store listing were both live. The website was sold to U.S. News & World Report on 26 February 2024.

Who owns Culture Trip now?

PhocusWire reported on 26 February 2024 that U.S. News & World Report acquired TheCultureTrip.com from Culture Trip Travel U.K. Both app stores now list U.S. News & World Report L.P. as developer. The site footer names International Travel Connect, LLC, and I did not find a source that explains how that company relates to the others.

What is Culture Trip?

Founded in London in 2011, it is a travel brand that publishes guides, stories and videos and sells trips, stays and experiences. Its app collects guides into plans on a map, per its Play listing.

Why is the Culture Trip app not working?

I could not confirm an outage. The latest App Store notes say version 6.5 fixed an issue that prevented some articles from opening, so update the app, then try the website, and contact the company if it still fails.
