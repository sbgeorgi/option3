Roatan in 3 days: 18 stops grouped by area, from 541 real places

A Roatan 3-day plan of 18 stops from the NomadSwipe app’s data, grouped by area because the island is long and thin: West Bay and West End on day one, Sandy Bay and the middle of the island on day two, French Harbour and east on day three. Stored hours rule out Sunday for days two and three.

Disclosure: I built NomadSwipe (Android), and I wrote this guide. Ratings, review counts, addresses and opening hours are the values stored in the app’s place data for Roatán, read on 7 November 2026. The stored rows were last updated between 11 April and 5 November 2026, so treat them as a snapshot.

The plan at a glance

  • Day 1, West Bay to West End (7 stops): breakfast at Luna Muna, Gumbalimba Park, the South Shore Canopy Zipline, Tabyana Beach, lunch at La Palapa Beach Bar, Stanley Submarines and a sunset drink at Sundowners Beach Bar.
  • Day 2, Sandy Bay and the middle of the island (6 stops): Manawakie Eco Nature Park, the Blue Harbor Tropical Arboretum, Jungle Top Adventures Zipline, lunch at Blue Bahia Beach Grill, Carambola Botanical Gardens and dinner at The Sunken Fish Restaurant.
  • Day 3, French Harbour to Jonesville Bight (5 stops): Arch’s Iguana & Marine Park, the Pirates of the Caribbean Canopy Tour, lunch at Kristi’s Overlook, Parrot Tree Plantation and Hole in the Wall.
  • Days of the week: day one works every day. Days two and three need a day that is not Sunday (see the table below).
  • Distance: day one adds up to about 7.2 km by straight line between stored coordinates, day two to about 10.6 km and day three to about 10.3 km. The day centres are 6.6 and 14.9 km apart. These are not driving times.

A deck of 3,076 cards for 541 places

Roatan is not one clean deck in the app. Its cards sit in 29 destination rows with names such as West End, Honduras, Sandy Bay, Honduras, Roatan Island and Coxen Hole, and they are mixed in with cards from other places. Inside a box around the island there are 3,076 cards, 886 distinct place IDs and 541 places once repeats of the same name and position are merged. Of the 541, 108 are sights by my grouping, and they span 18 stored category values. Only 55 of those 108 are rated 4.0 or higher with at least 100 stored reviews, and 64 of the 108 have no stored opening hours. This plan uses 11 sights and 7 places to eat or drink, all rated 4.1 or higher with at least 136 reviews.

  • Many of the sights are operators, not places you walk into. 51 of the 541 places have dive, diver, diving or scuba in the title (9%), and 29 of the 108 sights do (27%). Another 27 of the 108 sights are named as tours, ziplines, canopy tours, excursions or adventures. I used the fixed places and left the dive shops as swaps (details below).
  • Prices, ferry and cruise times are left out. I did not source fees, transport or port schedules from official pages this run, so check each place’s own channel before you go.
  • Add your own pins. Anything you want that the deck lacks is yours to add on the map yourself.

Which days of the week work

I parsed the stored weekly hours of the 15 stops that have them with a script, set a suggested start time for each, and tested every weekday. A stop fails a day if its stored hours are closed, do not cover the start time, or leave less than one hour before closing. Tabyana Beach, the Pirates of the Caribbean Canopy Tour and Parrot Tree Plantation have no stored hours, so the script cannot test them.

What I left out, and why

  • Cards with a US address on island coordinates. 79 of the 541 places have an address in the form “town, two capital letters, five digits” while their coordinates are on the island. Turquoise Bay Dive & Beach Resort (4.5 from 1,110 reviews, address in Panama City Beach) is one. I could not tell which of the address and the coordinates is wrong, so I took no address from a US-address card, though three of the 18 stops have such repeat cards (see the question on how the stops were chosen).
  • Cards whose repeats disagree about hours. Roatan Island Brewing Co (4.9 and 5.0 from about 280 reviews) has two cards that give different Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Saturday hours, and one of them has a Florida address. Victor Bodden Tours is stored as closed on Saturday and open 9 AM to 4 PM or 9 AM to 5 PM on other days, depending on the card.
  • Hours that look wrong. Mayan Eden (a zoo, 4.5 from 288 reviews) is stored as open 7 AM to 5 AM every day, and Roatan Henry Tour as open 7 AM to 4 AM on Saturday. I used neither.
  • Hotels, resorts and rentals. They are the largest group of cards and almost none has stored hours, so I skipped them for a day plan.

Questions about this Roatan plan

Can I follow this Roatan plan on any day of the week?

Day one works on all seven days. Days two and three do not work on a Sunday: Manawakie Eco Nature Park and Carambola Botanical Gardens are stored as closed on Sunday, and so is Arch’s Iguana & Marine Park. With three days, put day one on the Sunday. I tested the 15 stops with stored hours against every weekday, and the three without stored hours (Tabyana Beach, Pirates of the Caribbean Canopy Tour and Parrot Tree Plantation) could not be tested. The hours were read on 7 November 2026, so confirm them before you go.

Do I need a car or a taxi for this Roatan plan?

I read no transit, taxi or road source, so I cannot say what is available. Day one is compact, with six legs of 0.17 to 3.90 km by straight line between stored coordinates. Day two has two legs of about 4 km and 3 km, and day three has one leg of 4.89 km. Treat those as rides and check your own options, because roads make each real leg longer than the straight line.

How far apart are the three areas?

By straight line between the average stored coordinates of each day’s stops, day one (West Bay and West End) is 6.6 km from day two (Sandy Bay and Coxen Hole), and day two is 14.9 km from day three (French Harbour and Jonesville Bight). Day one and day three are 21.0 km apart. The stored extent of the box runs about 37.6 km corner to corner (33.0 km east to west), so the island is long enough that one base for every stop is a stretch.

Where are the dive shops in this plan?

None is in it. 51 of the 541 places have dive, diver, diving or scuba in the title (9%), and 29 of the 108 sights do, but I did not read what any of them offers, charges or requires, so I kept them out of a fixed schedule. If diving is why you are going, swap one of the day-one stops for a dive shop: Sun Divers, Roatan Divers and Coconut Tree Divers in West End and Infinity Divers Aqua Center in West Bay are all stored as 4.9 from at least 1,534 reviews, with stored hours.

Does this plan work for a cruise day?

I read no cruise-port, ferry or transfer source, so this guide cannot say. What the stored data does give is distance: day one is the shortest of the three, about 7.2 km in total by straight line, and every stop on it has stored hours except one beach. Check your own arrival and departure times against the stored hours table above.

How were the 18 stops chosen?

I picked cards by hand from the part of the deck that sits on the island, with a stored rating of 4.0 or higher and at least 100 stored reviews, then grouped them by area and checked each start time against the stored weekly hours. The addresses and hours shown are from island-address cards, and ratings and review counts are the highest among a place’s repeat cards. Three stops also have repeat cards with US addresses (La Palapa Beach Bar 8 of 19 cards, Sundowners Beach Bar 8 of 13, Manawakie Eco Nature Park 4 of 6), which I did not use. I skipped places whose duplicates disagree about hours in a way I could not settle. The lowest rating in the plan is 4.1 and the fewest reviews are 136.

Findings, method and sample

Sample and method. I read the NomadSwipe app’s place data on 7 November 2026 with read-only queries. Roatán has no single destination row. I took every row whose country is HND, excluded two rows named Custom Pin (7 cards, none of them on the island) and the rows with the country “Custom”, and kept the cards whose stored coordinates fall in a box around the island (latitude 16.20 to 16.52, longitude -86.62 to -86.20). The box keeps 3,076 cards from 8 destination rows: West End, Honduras (15 rows, 1,744 in the box), Sandy Bay, Honduras (4 rows, 945), Roatan, Honduras (115), Roatán, Honduras (84), Coxen Hole (2 rows, 69), Trujillo (63), Roatan Island (32) and Area, Honduras (24). Those rows are not clean: the West End and Sandy Bay rows hold 509 and 987 more cards outside the box, with stored latitudes from -25.86 to 56.95, and the Trujillo row holds 105 more. The box also drops mainland Honduras (the Tegucigalpa rows alone hold 216 cards at latitudes 13.92 to 14.12). Near the coast but outside the box are 83 further place IDs whose addresses name La Ceiba (25), Utila (20), Trujillo (15), Guanaja (3) or no town I searched for (20), so other Bay Islands are excluded by coordinates, not by name. Inside the box the stored extent runs from latitude 16.267 to 16.429 and longitude -86.602 to -86.293. I counted 886 distinct place IDs and merged repeats that share a name (case-insensitive) and a position (rounded to three decimals, about 110 m) into 541 places, taking the highest rating and review count among the repeats, and calling a place a sight if any of its cards is one. A sight is a card whose stored category is one of: tourist attraction, outdoor, beach, botanical garden, garden, park, zoo, lookout, hiking, amusement place, sports and recreation, boat tours, tours, activities, art gallery or museum (counted case-insensitively). An empty stored hours list counts as no stored hours. Areas are the nearest of six anchor points (West Bay, West End, Sandy Bay, Coxen Hole, French Harbour, Oak Ridge), each set to the average coordinates of cards whose address names that area, so the Coxen Hole group is the middle of the island and not just the town. I measured distance in a straight line between stored coordinates.
