NomadSwipe support and trip planner help

NomadSwipe support starts here: quick answers about the trip planner app are below, and anything else goes to privacy@nomadswipe.com. NomadSwipe runs on Android; you swipe real places in a city, save the ones you like into a trip and see them on a map.

Email support
Contact

Email privacy@nomadswipe.com with the device type, app version if known, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy questions

Review the Privacy Policy for information about collection, processing, sharing, retention, and privacy rights.

Account deletion

To request deletion of a NomadSwipe account or associated personal information, use the account deletion page.

Helpful details to include

For support requests, include the email address associated with the account, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and any relevant screenshots if you choose to share them.

Trip planner questions

Which phones can run NomadSwipe?

Android phones, through Google Play. An iPhone version is on the way, but it is not available yet.

What do I get without paying?

The free plan gives you 3 trips, 2 destination searches a month and 2 custom pins a month.

What are the Premium options?

Premium is optional. You can choose a Monthly plan or a Lifetime purchase inside the app, or keep using the free plan.

Can I open my trips without a connection?

Up to 20 trips are kept available offline on your phone.

How do I sort the places I saved?

Open a trip and sort its saved places by rating or by distance.

What does a place card show?

Tap a place for its photos, rating and opening hours, then use the buttons for directions, its website or a call.

Can I plan a trip together with friends?

No. NomadSwipe has no group, sharing or sync features.
