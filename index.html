First you swipe. Then you go.

NomadSwipe turns trip planning into a few minutes of swiping. The screens on this page come from a real trip planned that way: a long weekend in Lisbon, 16 places, every one of them swiped.

  1. Swipe through real places

    Open NomadSwipe in any city and you get a deck of real places nearby: sights, food, museums, nights out. Swipe right or tap Save to keep one, swipe left or tap Skip to pass. Each card shows the category, rating, distance and whether it is open now.

  2. Keep what fits the trip

    Every place you save goes straight into your trip, so the shortlist builds itself while you browse. The card tells you enough to decide: the Oceanário de Lisboa, for example, is rated 4.5 and open until 7 PM.

  3. See the whole trip on a map

    Your trip opens as a map with every saved place pinned, so you can see what sits close together and plan each day by neighbourhood. Prefer a list? Sort it by rating, distance or A to Z.

  4. Go, with everything one tap away

    Tap any place for its photos, then get directions, open the website or call ahead. Your trips are kept on your phone, so the plan is still there when the signal is not.

    Before you install

    Is NomadSwipe free? Yes. NomadSwipe is free to download. The free plan includes 3 trips, 2 destination searches and 2 custom pins a month, plus a limited number of swipes. Premium removes those limits.

    Is NomadSwipe on iPhone? Not yet. NomadSwipe is available for Android phones on Google Play.

    Does NomadSwipe work offline? Yes, for trip data. Up to 20 of your recent trips are saved on your phone, so you can open them without a connection.

    Which cities does it work in? Any city. The cards show real places around you, or around a destination you search, each with its rating, opening hours and distance.

    Can I add places that aren't in the app? Yes. Drop a custom pin for anything not on the cards, such as your hotel or a friend's flat. The free plan includes 2 custom pins a month, and Premium has no limit.

    Can I plan a trip with friends? Yes. Inviting a friend to view a trip is free. To plan the trip together, you and the friend you invite both need Premium: collaborators can edit the trip's destinations, and trip chat with friends is part of Premium.

    How much does Premium cost? Premium comes as a monthly plan or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Google Play shows the current price in your currency before you pay, and you can use the free plan for as long as you like first.

    How do I delete my account? Follow the steps on the account deletion page, or email privacy@nomadswipe.com.

    How do I get help? Visit the support page or email privacy@nomadswipe.com.
