## Tips

- One day covers one or two neighborhoods.
- Start from a shortlist of places you want to see.
- Check opening hours the day before.
- Leave 20 to 30 minutes after each stop, and keep one block of the day with nothing planned.
- Keep the last row for a place you might swap in.
