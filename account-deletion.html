Data request

Delete your NomadSwipe account

You can delete your NomadSwipe account and its data yourself in the app at any time. If you can no longer open the app, send one email to privacy@nomadswipe.com and we will delete it for you.

Request deletion by email

Delete in the app

  1. Open NomadSwipe and go to Profile.
  2. Under Account actions, tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out. If you signed up with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple once more so we can also revoke NomadSwipe's Sign in with Apple access.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email privacy@nomadswipe.com from the email address of your NomadSwipe account, if you can.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my NomadSwipe account" and include your username.

We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We complete email requests within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: email address, username, bio and sign-in connections (Google, Apple).
  • Your trips and their stops, and your place in trips other people shared with you.
  • Places you saved or skipped, your reviews and your map marks.
  • Your direct messages and chats, your trip chat messages, friends and invitations.
  • Business claims and the documents and photos uploaded with them, and support or feedback attachments.
  • Error reports linked to your account, and your purchase record at RevenueCat, the service that checks Premium purchases.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports you made about content or reviews stay in our moderation records without your name or account, so we can keep the service safe.
  • Place information that comes from public sources and is not about you (place names, photos, opening hours) stays in the app.
  • Copies in our database backups are overwritten as the backups rotate, within 30 days.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of App Store and Google Play purchases under their privacy policies.
  • If the law requires us to keep something, for example to answer a legal claim, we keep only that, only for as long as needed.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app you can delete trips and stops, remove friends, clear map marks, and change your username and bio. To have any other personal data deleted while you keep your account, email privacy@nomadswipe.com.

Cancel Premium before you delete your account

Deleting your NomadSwipe account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. If you pay for Monthly Premium, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions first, or renewals continue to be charged by Apple or Google.

More privacy choices

To access, correct or export your data, or to withdraw consent, see the Privacy Policy or email privacy@nomadswipe.com.
